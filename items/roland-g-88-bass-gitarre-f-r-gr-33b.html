Roland Roland G-88 Bass-Gitarre für GR-33B

Bass Guitar Year: 1980

Description

The G-88 is a controller bass for the Roland GR-33B bass guitar synthesizer (with 24-pin connection) and has the body shape of a typical Fender bass. In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal of a special pickup, which outputs the signals of the four strings separately and can thus control GR synthesizers, the bass also has a normal bass output that delivers the sound of the single-coil pickup with dual magnets. The G-88 can therefore also be used as a regular bass. The bass features a body made of walnut and ash with a neck-through design of maple and walnut, manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (specifically by the supplier Fuji Gen Gakki). Sound control is provided by three larger, golden and two smaller, golden and black (plastic) knobs: Master Volume, Cutoff Frequency, and Balance between bass and synthesizer level (large), separate controls for bass and treble (gold), and controls for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato strength. A three-stage switch located between the Resonance and Vibrato controls alternates between bass and synthesizer signal or both.

Features

  • Controller bass for GR-33B synthesizer
  • 24-pin connection for GR synthesizers
  • Normal bass output
  • Single-coil pickups with dual magnets
  • Walnut and ash body
  • Neck-through design made of maple and walnut
  • Manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (Fuji Gen Gakki)
  • Master Volume, Cutoff Frequency, Bass/Synthesizer balance controls
  • Separate controls for bass and treble
  • Controls for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato intensity
  • Three-stage switch for Bass, Synthesizer, or both signals
