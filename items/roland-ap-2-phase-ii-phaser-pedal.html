Roland AP-2 'Phase II' Phaser-Pedal

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1975

Description

The Roland AP-2 'Phase II' is a floor effect pedal for guitar in a sturdy, medium-brown die-cast casing. It is a phaser with two controls, RATE and RESONANCE, and an effect/bypass switch on the top. RATE controls the modulation speed, RESONANCE controls the tonal character of the phaser. The 'INSTRUMENT' jack input is on the left side, unlike most other Roland effects, and the output (jack, mono) is on the right. This complicates cabling multiple effects. To change the two (!!) 9V block batteries, the casing must be unscrewed from the bottom; power supply operation is not provided. The casing shape is the same as used for (Roland) AS-1, AF-60, and (Boss) BF-1. The AP-2 'Phase II' was manufactured by the Roland Music Engineering Group (Roland MEG), which was later renamed 'Boss'.

Features

  • Phaser effect
  • RATE control (modulation speed)
  • RESONANCE control (phaser tone)
  • Effect/Bypass switch
  • Sturdy die-cast casing
  • Mono instrument input (left side)
  • Mono output (right side)
  • Powered by 2x 9V block batteries
  • No AC adapter option
