Boss SP-1 Spectrum

Guitar/Bass Effect Pedal Year: 1980

Description

The BOSS Spectrum is a tunable parametric filter capable of boosting a specific frequency band, continuously adjustable between 500 Hz and 5 KHz. The second control on the SP-1 is the balance knob, which determines the volume ratio between the normal and effect signals.

Features

  • Tunable parametric filter
  • Frequency band adjustable from 500 Hz to 5 KHz
  • Balance control for mixing normal and effect signals
  • 9V battery or AC adapter power
  • 3.5mA current draw
  • 80dB S/N ratio
  • 220K Ohm input impedance
  • Over 600 Ohm output load impedance
  • Compact dimensions (2.8 x 2.2 x 4.9 inches)
  • Lightweight (1.1 lbs)
