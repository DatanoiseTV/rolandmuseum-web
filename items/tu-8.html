TU-8

Utility Year: 1994

Description

The TU-8 is a guitar/bass tuner with a needle meter for precise tuning indication and LEDs for string status. In addition to standard tuning, the TU-8 supports open tunings (D, E, G, A major) or guitar tunings 1 or 2 semitones lower than normal. The tuning range extends from A0 to C8, with an adjustable reference pitch from 438 to 445 Hz. A built-in microphone allows tuning of acoustic guitars.

Features

  • Guitar/bass tuner
  • Needle meter for precise tuning
  • LEDs for string status
  • Supports standard tunings
  • Supports Open Tunings (D, E, G, A major)
  • Supports guitar tunings 1 or 2 semitones lower
  • Tuning range: A0 to C8
  • Adjustable reference pitch: 438 to 445 Hz
  • Built-in microphone for acoustic guitars
  • Powered by 1 x 9V block battery or PSA adapter
