Roland MC-50

Sequencer Year: 1990

Description

The MC-50 is a compact, song-oriented MIDI sequencer with two independent MIDI outputs, a 3.5" floppy drive, and a backlit display. Unlike its predecessors, the operating system is integrated, not loaded from disk. It includes an 8-track sequencer and a performance package program. However, it cannot directly handle standard MIDI files (SMF) or bulk dumps from other devices; alternative operating systems can be loaded from disk for this (MRB-500 Bulk Librarian or MRM-500 SMF-Converter). In normal operation, it's an 8-track sequencer, with each track recording all 16 MIDI channels. There's an additional rhythm track for up to 240 rhythm patterns with up to 32 notes each. Up to 8 songs can be stored. The MC-50 can be synchronized internally or externally via MIDI clock signals and a FSK signal (Tape Sync).

Features

  • 8-track sequencer
  • 16 MIDI channels per track
  • rhythm track (240 patterns)
  • 3.5" floppy disk drive
  • backlit display
  • MIDI Clock sync
  • FSK (Tape Sync)
