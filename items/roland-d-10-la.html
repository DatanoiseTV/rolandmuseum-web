Roland D-10 LA

Digital Synthesizer Year: 1988

Description

The D-10 is a 9-part multitimbral, 32-voice digital synthesizer with 61 velocity-sensitive keys and MIDI. It uses LA synthesis, combining PCM samples without a filter and simpler synthesis with a filter. A "Timbre" consists of two "Tones", each made of one or two "Partials". A partial can be a PCM oscillator or a simple pulse-width-modulated oscillator. The filter is warm-sounding and offers resonance. It includes a drum computer for creating songs from 1- to 2-bar patterns. It features two modes: Performance (layering or splitting two sounds) and Multitimbral (controlling 8 parts simultaneously via MIDI).

Features

  • 9-part multitimbral
  • 32-voice polyphony
  • 61 velocity-sensitive keys
  • LA synthesis (PCM and simple oscillators)
  • Dynamic filter
  • Digital reverb (8 algorithms)
  • Drum computer
  • Performance and multitimbral modes
  • MIDI
  • Expandable memory via memory card
