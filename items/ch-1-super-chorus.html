CH-1 SUPER Chorus

Compact FX Year: 1990

Description

The CH-1 Super Chorus boasts all the advantages of BOSS's proven compact format. Unlike conventional chorus devices, however, this effect pedal allows for the creation of a wide variety of chorus effects. Punchy sounds with lots of presence are possible with the CH-1, as are the warm, sustained chorus effects found in so many popular songs. Characteristic of the CH-1 are its wide frequency response and high signal-to-noise ratio of 96 dB. The result of this sophisticated effect circuit is an exceptionally pure sound, comparable to the professional quality of much more expensive rack effects. Controls for effect level, EQ, modulation rate, and depth are used to create all desired chorus effects. Further features of the CH-1 include a separate stereo output. The effect pedal can be powered either by batteries or by an optionally available AC adapter (PSA series). Its versatility makes the CH-1 the ideal chorus pedal for any purpose and any musical style.

Features

  • 9V battery or PSA-series AC adapter power supply
  • Power consumption: 9V, 15 mA
  • S/N ratio: -95 dBm or less (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 400 g
  • Controls for effect level, EQ, modulation rate, and depth
  • Separate stereo output
  • Wide frequency response
