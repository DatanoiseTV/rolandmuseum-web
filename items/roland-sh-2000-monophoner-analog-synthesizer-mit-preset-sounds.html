Roland Roland SH-2000 Monophoner Analog-Synthesizer mit Preset-Sounds

Synthesizers Year: 1974

Description

The SH-2000 is a monophonic, analog preset synthesizer with 37 (non-velocity-sensitive) keys. The keyboard offers a kind of aftertouch, which can be used to control vibrato, pitch bend or a "growl" effect, among other things. With the 30 sounds stored in the presets, the parameters envelope sustain, modulation intensity, rate, filter cutoff and resonance can be changed; completely individual settings are not possible. The SH-2000 generates sounds with one oscillator, has no audio effects, no headphone or control connections, and unlike all other Roland synthesizers has a logarithmic volt/hertz control.

Features

  • Monophonic analog synthesizer
  • 37 keys (non-velocity-sensitive)
  • Preset sounds
  • Aftertouch control
  • Single oscillator
