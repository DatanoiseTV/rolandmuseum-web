Roland D-110

Synthesizer Module Year: 1988

Description

The D-110 is a 9-part multitimbral, 32-voice, digital MIDI synthesizer module in a 19" rack format. It uses LA synthesis, a combination of PCM samples without filters and simple synthesis with filters. A sound program, called a 'Timbre,' consists of two 'Tones,' which are formed from one or two 'Partials.' A Partial is either a PCM oscillator (ROM samples) followed by an amplifier, or it consists of a simple, pulse-width-modulable oscillator (triangle/square) with a dynamic filter and amplifier. 13 structures are available for connecting the two Partials to a Tone, so a sound program (Timbre) can consist of up to 4 oscillators. The filter offers resonance without self-oscillation and sounds very 'warm.' The internally available 256 PCM samples are somewhat shorter than those of the first LA synthesizer, the D-50. The sound generation is the same as in the D-5, D-10, and D-20, and concludes with a digital reverb that offers 8 algorithms.

Features

  • 9-part multitimbral
  • 32-voice polyphony
  • LA synthesis
  • PCM samples
  • Digital reverb
  • 19" rack format
