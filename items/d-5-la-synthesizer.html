D-5 LA Synthesizer

Digital Synthesizer Year: 1989

Description

The D-5 is a 9-part multitimbral, 32-voice digital synthesizer with 61 velocity-sensitive synthesizer keys, an arpeggiator, and MIDI. 8 tonally playable parts and a rhythm part use LA synthesis, a combination of 2 tones via one of 13 structures. A tone consists either of a PCM oscillator (ROM samples) with a subsequent amp, or a simple oscillator (triangle/square/...) with a dynamic filter and amp. The samples are slightly shorter than those in the D-50. The sound generation is the same as in the D-10, D-20, and D-110. A PG-10 programmer can be connected for programming. Compared to the D-10, it also differs by having a slightly lower-quality keyboard, a different user interface, an external power supply, and an additional arpeggiator.

Features

  • 9-part multitimbral
  • 32-voice polyphony
  • Digital synthesizer
  • 61 velocity-sensitive synthesizer keys
  • Arpeggiator
  • MIDI connectivity
  • LA synthesis engine
  • 8 tonal parts + 1 rhythm part
  • Tone structures: combination of 2 tones via 1 of 13 structures
  • Tone elements: PCM oscillator (ROM samples) + amp, or simple oscillator (triangle/square/...) + dynamic filter + amp
  • Shorter samples than D-50
  • Sound generation identical to D-10, D-20, D-110
  • Compatible with PG-10 programmer for editing
  • External power supply (ACI Netzteil included)
  • Dimensions: 978 x 84 x 279 mm
  • Weight: 6.6 kg
  • Includes PJ-1M Audio-Kabel
