Roland MKS-10 Piano Module PLANET P

Pianos Year: 1984

Description

The MKS-10 PLANET-P is a 16-voice, analog piano sound generation module in a 19-inch rack format with 2 rack units. Exactly 16 immutable presets are available, 8 of which can be selected on the MKS-10 itself, while the remaining 8 can only be switched via MIDI program change. The MKS-10 is exclusively polyphonic (not multi-timbral) and can receive data on a freely selectable MIDI channel. Since nothing is storable, there are only MIDI IN and MIDI THRU jacks. The MKS-10, along with the MKS-30 and MKS-80 modules, and the MKS-1000 and MKS-300 master keyboards, marked the beginning of the separation of MIDI data and sound generation.

Features

  • 16-voice analog piano sound generation module
  • 19-inch rack format with 2 rack units
  • 16 immutable presets available
  • 8 presets selectable on the unit, 8 only via MIDI program change
  • Exclusively polyphonic (not multi-timbral)
  • Receives data on a freely selectable MIDI channel
  • MIDI IN and MIDI THRU jacks
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 482 mm x 89 mm x 400 mm
  • Weight: 10 kg
  • Power consumption: 30W
  • Built-in power supply, Roland power cable
  • Included accessories: LP-25 Audio Cable, MSC-10 MIDI Cable
