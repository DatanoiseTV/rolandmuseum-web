SH-7 Duophonic Analog Synthesizer

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1978

Description

The SH-7 is an analog, duophonic synthesizer with 44 non-velocity-sensitive keys, built into the bottom part of a flight case and featuring an angled control surface. The SH-7 has two oscillators, both independently adjustable in foot (octave) range (2, 4, 8, 16, 32) and waveform (triangle, sawtooth, square, and modifiable pulse). Only VCO1 additionally provides a freely adjustable mix of square waves in 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 foot ranges. VCO2 can be synchronized to VCO1 or used when playing two voices on the keyboard. Mixable noise and a ring modulator complete the sound generation, which is shaped by a fixed high-pass filter and the dynamic VCF and VCA blocks. Two envelopes are available for this. Modulation can be done with the pitch bend lever, the LFO, and a random generator. CV/Gate inputs and outputs are also present, as well as an external audio input, headphone jack, and control input for the filter frequency.
