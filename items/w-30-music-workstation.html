Roland W-30 Music Workstation

Sampler Workstation Year: 1989

Description

The W-30 Music Workstation is a 16-voice digital sampler with a velocity-sensitive 61-key keyboard, a 3.5" floppy drive for sample storage, and a built-in sequencer comparable to the MC-500. Its sample memory holds 2 x 7.2 seconds at 30kHz or 2 x 14.4 seconds at 15kHz and is not expandable. The 12-bit resolution, inherited from the S-50, is compensated by high-quality 16-bit converters, resulting in good sound quality. Sounds, called 'Tones,' are created from up to 32 samples with playback parameters. Tones can be mapped across the keyboard and saved as Patches (up to 16). The W-30 has dynamic filters like the S-330 and S-550. The sequencer features a tempo track and 16 MIDI tracks, each capable of recording data on all 16 MIDI channels. It is compatible with song data from the Director S sequencers, other S-samplers, the MC-500, and Super-MRC. It uses a liquid crystal display and requires an operating system loaded from disk.

Features

  • 16-voice digital sampler
  • 61-key velocity-sensitive keyboard
  • 3.5" floppy drive
  • Built-in sequencer
  • 12-bit sample resolution with 16-bit converters
  • Dynamic filters
  • Compatible with Director S and MC-500 song data
