Roland 102 Expander Unit

Synthesizer Expander Unit Year: 1976

Description

The Synthesizer Expander 102, part of the System-100, is a complete monophonic analog synthesizer. Its architecture is similar to the 101 Basic Unit, featuring a VCO, Noise Generator, Audio Mixer, High Pass Filter, VCF, VCA, Envelope Generator (ADSR), LFO, and Glide. It offers variable pulse width. Unlike the 101, it does not include a keyboard, but instead incorporates a ring modulator, Sample & Hold, and an additional mixer unit. CV (Control Voltage) and GATE inputs and outputs are fully available.

Features

  • Monophonic analog synthesizer
  • Part of the Roland System-100
  • Includes: VCO, Noise Generator, Audio Mixer, High Pass Filter, VCF, VCA
  • Envelope Generator (ADSR)
  • LFO (Low-Frequency Oscillator)
  • Glide function
  • Variable pulse width
  • Ring modulator
  • Sample & Hold
  • Additional mixer unit
  • CV (Control Voltage) inputs and outputs
  • GATE inputs and outputs
  • No integrated keyboard (expander unit)
  • Internal power supply with fixed power cable
