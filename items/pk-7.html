Roland PK-7

MIDI Foot Pedal Year: 1999

Description

The PK-7 is a 20-key MIDI foot pedal controller without internal sounds. It transmits MIDI notes in a fixed range from note number 36 to 55 (C2 to G3) on MIDI channel 2 with a fixed velocity of 120. It's not velocity-sensitive. The PK-7 integrates an expression pedal, two side switches, and a hold switch. These send MIDI CC messages with fixed numbers (18, 19, and 64), with the expression pedal fixed to CC#11. Each control has a separate output jack to connect to a master keyboard or organ's pedal inputs. When a cable is plugged into one of the jacks, the corresponding MIDI message isn't transmitted via MIDI out. It can be connected to a VK-77 through a special PK port for MIDI communication and power.

Features

  • 20 keys
  • Transmits MIDI notes (C2-G3) on channel 2
  • Fixed velocity of 120
  • Integrated expression pedal, two side switches, and hold switch
  • Connects to VK-77 for power and MIDI
  • Separate output jacks for pedal controls
