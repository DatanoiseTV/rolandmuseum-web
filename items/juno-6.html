Roland JUNO-6

Polyphonic Analog Synthesizer Year: 1982

Description

The Juno-6 is a 6-voice analog synthesizer with 61 keys and an arpeggiator. Each voice features one DCO (Digitally Controlled Oscillator), a high-pass filter, a dynamic low-pass filter, a dynamic amplifier, and an envelope generator. Sound programs cannot be stored. The connections are straightforward: headphone and main outputs (L/Mono, R) alongside inputs for a sustain pedal, arpeggio clock, and filter cutoff frequency control. A rear-panel knob for adjusting the master tuning is also present.
