SH-3 Monophonic Analog Synthesizer with 44 keys

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1973

Description

The SH-3 is a monophonic analog synthesizer with 44 (non-velocity-sensitive) keys, built into a flight case. The SH-3 features one VCO, where waveforms (square, pulse, sawtooth, and noise) are mixable and routed through a dynamic 12dB filter with resonance (up to self-oscillation) and a dynamic amplifier (VCA). The VCA can be controlled by two fixed curves or the single ADSR envelope. Two LFOs are available for modulation. There are no audio effects or CV/Gate control connections. Initially, the SH-3 was available under the company name ACE-TONE, and later in 1973 (after the company's founding in 1972), it was also sold under the Roland brand name. With minor circuit changes, the synthesizer was mostly sold as the Roland SH-3A.

Features

  • Monophonic analog synthesizer
  • 44 non-velocity-sensitive keys
  • Built into a flight case
  • Single VCO with mixable waveforms (square, pulse, sawtooth, noise)
  • Dynamic 12dB filter with resonance (up to self-oscillation)
  • Dynamic VCA controllable by two fixed curves or one ADSR envelope
  • Two LFOs for modulation
  • No audio effects or CV/Gate control connections
  • Integrated power supply with fixed power cable
  • Dimensions: 1005 x 150 x 320 mm
  • Weight: 14.5 kg
  • 8W power consumption
