G-303 Guitar for GR-300

Synthesizer Addons Year: 1980

Description

The G-303 is a controller guitar for Roland guitar synthesizers with a 24-pin connector, featuring a distinctive double-cutaway shape. In addition to the 24-pin connector for the special 'hexaphonic' pickup's signal (which outputs individual string signals to control GR synthesizers), the guitar also has a standard guitar output for the sound of its two built-in humbucker pickups. This means the G-303 can also be used as a regular guitar. All metal parts are nickel-plated; the body is mahogany, and the glued-in neck is maple. The G-303 differs from the simultaneously available G-808 only by its glued-in rather than neck-through construction and the wood used. The G-303 was manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (specifically by supplier Fuji Gen Gakki) according to Roland's specifications. Sound control is provided by four gold (plastic) knobs for Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, and Balance between guitar and synthesizer levels, two black knobs for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato intensity, a three-stage mode switch, and another three-stage switch between guitar and synthesizer signals or both. The G-303 is the legendary guitar played by Pat Metheny, as well as Andy Summers (The Police), Adrian Belew, and Robert Fripp (King Crimson). The G-303 was also available in white (G-303W) and as a left-handed model (G-303LH).

Features

  • Controller guitar for Roland guitar synthesizers
  • 24-pin connector
  • Hexaphonic pickup (outputs individual string signals)
  • Standard guitar output
  • Two built-in humbucker pickups
  • Mahogany body
  • Glued-in maple neck
  • Controls for Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, Guitar/Synthesizer balance
  • Controls for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato intensity
  • Three-stage mode switch
  • Three-stage switch for guitar/synthesizer signal or both
  • Dimensions: 322 x 1020 x 66 mm
  • Weight: 3.7 kg
  • No power adapter/cable required
  • Production years: 1980-1985
