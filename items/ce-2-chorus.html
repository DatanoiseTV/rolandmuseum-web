CE-2 Chorus

Compact FX Year: 1979

Description

The CE-2 is the compact version of the famous CE-1 Chorus Ensemble pedal. Like the CE-1, the CE-2 offers the incomparable BOSS Chorus sound. One of the main reasons for the exceptional success of the chorus effect is its wide range of applications, from amplifying guitars, keyboards, and wind instruments to large vocal PAs. The controls on the CE-2 are 'Rate' (speed) and 'Depth' (effect depth). The CE-2 Chorus offers the well-known BOSS chorus effect, which provides a doubling or thickening effect, enriching the sound of guitars, keyboards, woodwind and brass instruments, and even vocals, without being obtrusive or distracting from the instrument's original tone. The CE-2 features controls for the depth of the chorus effect and the rate of its sweep for precise and creative control. The CE-2 Chorus also boasts one of the best signal-to-noise ratios among all compact effects units, over 90 dB, making it ideal for recording. Like other BOSS compact effects units, the CE-2 is equipped with quiet FET switching, LED on/off and battery indicator, a robust and reliable housing, and an AC adapter jack.

Features

  • Rate and Depth controls
  • Doubling or thickening chorus effect
  • 9V battery or AC adapter power
  • 9mA current draw
  • Signal-to-noise ratio over 90dB
  • Maximum input level: 0dBm (100Hz), -10dBm (1KHz)
  • Input impedance: 470k Ohm
  • Output load impedance: over 10k Ohm
  • Quiet FET switching
  • LED On/Off and battery indicator
  • Robust and reliable housing
  • AC adapter jack
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 400 g
