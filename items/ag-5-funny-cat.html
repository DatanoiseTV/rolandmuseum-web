Roland AG-5 'Funny Cat'

Guitar Effect Pedal Year: 1973

Description

The Roland AG-5 'Funny Cat - Harmonic Moover & Soft Distortion Sustainer' is a guitar effects pedal housed in a robust metal enclosure. It offers a type of auto-wah combined with a mild overdrive with sustain. The top left side features a switch for 'HARMONIC OVERDRIVE' and 'NORMAL' (effect off). On the right, the 'Soft Distortion Sustainer' (SDS) can be turned on and off ('NORMAL'). Similar to the AF-100 'Bee Baa', which shares the same enclosure design, the jacks and knobs are located on the narrow back panel. From right to left: 'INSTRUMENT' (mono input), 'OUTPUT', 'SDS' (controls level and sustain), 'MODE' (filter envelope A, B, or C), and a balance control between the dry signal ('STRAIGHT') and the 'HARMONIC M(over)' effect. Like the AF-100, the top of the enclosure can be opened by loosening two rear screws to access the battery compartment. The name 'Funny Cat', according to founder Ikutaro Kakehashi, refers to the pedal's unique sound. The AG-5 was manufactured by the Roland Music Engineering Group (Roland MEG), which later became 'Boss'.

Features

  • Auto-wah
  • Overdrive with sustain
  • Harmonic Mover filter
  • Soft Distortion Sustainer (SDS)
  • Metal enclosure
  • Mode selector (A, B, C)
  • Balance control
