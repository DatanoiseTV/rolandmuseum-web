TU-12P Chromatic Tuner

Utility Year: 1988

Description

The TU-12P is a chromatic tuner with a built-in microphone and pickup, allowing electric guitars to be tuned without a cable connection. It features a liquid crystal display with a bar graph and a tuning range from C1 to B5 with an adjustable reference pitch from 440 to 444 Hz.

Features

  • Chromatic tuner
  • Built-in microphone
  • Built-in pickup for cable-free tuning of electric guitars
  • Liquid crystal display (LCD) with bar graph
  • Tuning range: C1 to B5
  • Adjustable reference pitch: 440 to 444 Hz
  • Power: 1 x CR2032 battery
  • Dimensions: 147 x 18 x 25 mm
  • Weight: 0.045 kg
  • Available in black or white
