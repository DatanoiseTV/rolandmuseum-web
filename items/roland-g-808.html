Roland G-808

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1980

Description

The Roland G-808 is a guitar controller designed for Roland guitar synthesizers with a 24-pin connection. Its distinctive double-cutaway body houses both a 24-pin connection for the hexaphonic pickup (separately outputting string signals) and a standard guitar output for its two humbucker pickups. Therefore, it functions as a standard guitar as well. It features a neck through design made of maple and mahogany with a body of walnut and ash. It differs from the contemporary G-303 primarily in its neck-through construction and wood choice. The G-808 was manufactured by Ibanez/Greco according to Roland's specifications.

Features

  • 24-pin connection
  • hexaphonic pickup
  • standard guitar output
  • two humbucker pickups
  • neck-through design (maple and mahogany neck, walnut and ash body)
  • Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, Balance, Resonance (VCF), Vibrato controls
