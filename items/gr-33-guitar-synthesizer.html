Roland GR-33 Guitar Synthesizer

Guitar Synthesizer Year: 2000

Description

The GR-33 Guitar Synthesizer offers improved sound generation, multi-effects, further improved ultra-short response time, and a revised user interface with a 32-character display and an expression pedal. This gives guitarists an even wider selection of excellent sounds. The sound source of this incredible GK-Ready synthesizer is based on the renowned Roland JV-1080 sound module with first-class sound and studio-quality effects. All of this comes at an extremely musician-friendly price.

Features

  • Enhanced sound generation based on the Roland JV-1080 synthesizer
  • 384 sounds with doubled waveform memory compared to the GR-30
  • Ultra-fast response with Roland GK-2a pickup-equipped guitars
  • Built-in multi-effects: Chorus, Reverb, and 25 effects (including Overdrive, Distortion, Rotary)
  • 40 insert effects with real-time control via expression pedal
  • 128 user patches for custom effects
  • 32-character LCD display and value dial for intuitive editing
  • Programmable expression pedal
  • 'Output Select' switch for amplifier or power amp matching
  • Built-in Intelligent Harmonist and Arpeggiator
  • MIDI I/O for sequencing and controlling other modules
  • 48-voice polyphony
