Roland GS-500 Guitar Controller

Synthesizer Addons Year: 1977

Description

The GS-500 is a controller guitar specifically designed for the GR-500 guitar synthesizer. It features no standard guitar output, relying solely on a 24-pin connector for the control cable to the synthesizer. While it has a built-in humbucker pickup, its signal can only be accessed via the GR-500. Additionally, a special hexaphonic pickup delivers separate control signals per string to the synthesizer. This Les Paul-shaped guitar, constructed with a mahogany body and maple top, was manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (Fuji Gen Gakki) to Roland's specifications. It includes master volume for the pure guitar sound, separate volume and tone controls, a 3-stage equalizer switch, four volume controls with on/off switches for 'Polyensemble', 'Bass', 'Solo-Melody', and 'External-Synthesizer' sections, 'Remote' and 'Portamento' on/off switches, and a three-stage switch for selecting Guitar, Synthesizer, or Dual sound.

Features

  • Controller guitar for Roland GR-500 guitar synthesizer
  • 24-pin connector for control cable (no standard guitar output)
  • Built-in humbucker pickup (signal accessed via GR-500)
  • Hexaphonic pickup for string-separated control signals
  • Les Paul style design with mahogany body and maple top
  • Manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (Fuji Gen Gakki)
  • Controls: Master Volume, Volume, Tone, 3-stage Equalizer switch
  • Four volume controls and on/off switches for Polyensemble, Bass, Solo-Melody, External-Synthesizer sections
  • Remote and Portamento on/off switches
  • Three-stage switch for Guitar, Synthesizer, and Dual sound modes
  • Dimensions: 335 x 1000 x 85 mm
  • Weight: 4.8 kg
  • Includes GR-500 Guitar Synthesizer and C-24G 24-pin cable (5m)
  • Recommended accessories: PC-50 Preset Controller, KS-10A Stand
