Roland G-505

Guitar Year: 1982

Description

The G-505 is a control guitar for Roland guitar synthesizers with a 24-pin connector and has a Strat-shaped body, three single-coil pickups, a typical Strat 5-way switch, and a tremolo system (but a distinctive pickguard). In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal of the special "hexaphonic" pickup, which outputs the string signals separately and can thus control GR synthesizers, the guitar also has a normal guitar output where the sound of the single-coil pickups is output. The G-505 can therefore also be used as a completely normal guitar. The natural-colored maple neck, consistent across all variants, is bolted to an ash body, which was available in three color versions. According to Roland specifications, the following variants were manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (more precisely by the supplier Fuji Gen Gakki): in red, blue, or sunburst with a white pickguard. Four golden (plastic) knobs serve for sound control: Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, and Balance between guitar and synthesizer levels; two black knobs for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato intensity; a three-position mode switch in between; and also a three-position switch between guitar and synthesizer signal or both.

Features

  • Control guitar for Roland guitar synthesizers
  • 24-pin connector
  • Strat-shaped body
  • Three single-coil pickups
  • 5-way switch
