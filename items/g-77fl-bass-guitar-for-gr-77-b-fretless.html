G-77FL Bass Guitar for GR-77 B (Fretless)

Synthesizer Addons Year: 1986

Description

The G-77FL is a fretless controller bass for Roland Bass Synthesizers with a 24-pin connection. It features a very distinctive "handle" design, a fiberglass stabilizer intended to prevent unwanted neck vibrations. In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal of the special individual-string pickup used to control GR synthesizers (GR-33B or GR-77B), the bass also has a normal bass output that outputs the sound of its two standard pickups. The G-77FL can therefore also be used as a regular bass, although its shape may cause it to slip when played sitting down. Besides the fiberglass stabilizer, the bass is made of alder and maple. Sound control is provided by a larger (plastic) Master Volume knob and six smaller black knobs for Pickup Balance (between the two normal pickups), Guitar Tone, and Balance (between normal bass and bass synthesizer), Cutoff, Resonance (VCF) or EDIT, and Vibrato Intensity. Below these is a three-stage mode switch, which has different functions depending on the connected synthesizers. The G-77FL was also available with frets as the G-77.

  • 24-pin connection for synthesizer control
