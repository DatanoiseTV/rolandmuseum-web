Roland JV-1080

Synthesizer Module Year: 1994

Description

The JV-1080 is a 64-voice, 16-part multitimbral rack synthesizer module without a keyboard. Its extensive sound library can be expanded with up to four SR-JV80 series Wave Expansion Boards. It occupies 2U in a 19-inch rack format and offers one insert multi-effect (MFX) in addition to chorus and reverb effects. A sound program (Patch) consists of up to 4 Tones, two of which can be combined via one of 10 structures. Each Tone comprises a PCM oscillator, a 12dB multi-mode filter, a dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, and 2 LFOs. Depending on the chosen structure, boosters and a ring modulator can be added, and with identical settings for two filters, a quality of 24dB can be achieved. Due to Tones being limitable in velocity and note range, split, layer, and crossover sounds are possible within a single patch. The ROM sample library has been significantly expanded compared to its predecessors. A Performance manages the compilation of up to 15 parts (with patches) and one rhythm set, with MIDI channels freely selectable for each individual part.

Features

  • 64-voice polyphony
  • 16-part multitimbral
  • Rack synthesizer module (2U, 19-inch format)
  • No keyboard
  • Expandable with four SR-JV80 series Wave Expansion Boards
  • Effects: 1 Insert MFX, Chorus, Reverb
  • Patches with up to 4 Tones
  • Tones combined via 10 structures
  • Tone components: PCM oscillator, 12dB multi-mode filter, dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, 2 LFOs
  • Optional boosters and ring modulator
  • 24dB filter quality with dual filter settings
  • Split, layer, and crossover sounds within patches
  • Expanded ROM sample library
  • Performance mode for up to 15 parts and 1 rhythm set
  • Freely assignable MIDI channels per part
