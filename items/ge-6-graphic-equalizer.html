Boss GE-6 Graphic Equalizer

Compact Equalizer Pedal Year: 1980

Description

The GE-6 (Graphic Equalizer) is a compact equalizer designed for initial sound control on an instrument. The GE-6 also finds many applications as an effects unit, e.g., for boosting in a specific frequency range. The GE-6 features 6 octave bands with a control range of ± 15 dB per band and a center detent at 0 dB. Compared to other compact equalizers, its silent electronic switch allows the GE-6 to be switched in or out of the signal path. Another excellent feature is its signal-to-noise ratio of more than 90 dB, which makes it quieter than some studio equalizers.

Features

  • 6 octave bands with ±15 dB control range per band
  • Center detent at 0 dB for each band
  • Silent electronic switching
  • S/N ratio over 90 dB
  • Power: 9V Battery or AC Adapter
  • Current draw: DC9V 7mA
  • Frequency centers: 100Hz, 200Hz, 400Hz, 800Hz, 1.6kHz, 3.2KHz
  • Frequency response: 15Hz-25KHz, +2dB
  • Input impedance: 220K Ohm
  • Output load impedance: Over 600 Ohm
  • Dimensions: 2.8(W) x 2.2(H) x 4.9(D) in.
  • Weight: 1 Ib.
