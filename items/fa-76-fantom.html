FA-76 Fantom

Digital Synthesizer Year: 2002

Description

The Fantom (FA-76) is a digital synthesizer workstation with 76 velocity-sensitive synthesizer keys, a sequencer, and a flexible arpeggiator. The sound generation is 16-part multitimbral and based on the XV sound engine, which forms patches from up to four tones. Each tone consists of a stereo PCM oscillator, dynamic filter and amp, 3 envelopes, and LFOs. Two tones can be linked by one of 10 structures. All parameters are fully editable. In addition to programmable patterns, the arpeggiator includes a rhythm section and many complex preset arpeggio/rhythm patterns. The sequencer offers 16 tracks, each capable of recording all 16 MIDI channels, 100 patterns, tempo and time signature tracks, and can hold one song in memory, as well as play songs directly from a 3.5' HD floppy disk. Although the FA-76 already carries the 'Fantom' name, it cannot use its own samples as the basis for the oscillators. This original Fantom still belongs to the XV series. Additional key points include 1024 patches (640 preset, 128 user, 256 GM2 sounds) plus 29 rhythm sets, a variable arpeggiator and rhythm generator with real-time control via rotary knobs, buttons, and D-Beam infrared controller, a high-resolution sequencer with Realtime Phrase Sequencing (RPS function), 24-bit Reverb plus Chorus and 90 multi-effects such as COSM Amp Modeling, expandability with one SR-JV80 and two SRX Wave Expansion Boards, 24-bit D/A converters and S/PDIF digital outputs, and a floppy drive for saving user data.
