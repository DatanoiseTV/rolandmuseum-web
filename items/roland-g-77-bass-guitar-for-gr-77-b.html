Roland G-77 Bass Guitar for GR-77 B

Other Year: 1985

Description

The G-77 is a control bass for Roland bass synthesizers with a 24-pin connection and features a very distinctive 'handle' design, a fiberglass stabilizer designed to prevent unwanted neck vibrations. In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal of the special individual string pickups to control the GR synthesizers (GR-33B or GR-77B), the bass also has a normal bass output that delivers the sound of the two standard pickups. Thus, the G-77 can also be used as a regular bass, although its shape might make it prone to slipping when played sitting down. Besides the fiberglass stabilizer, the bass is made of alder and maple and was available in three colors: (ivory) white, red, and black. Sound control is provided by a larger (plastic) knob for Master Volume and six smaller black knobs for Pickup Balance (between the two normal pickups), Guitar Tone, and Balance (between normal bass and bass synthesizer), Cutoff, Resonance (VCF) or EDIT, and for Vibrato Intensity, plus a three-stage mode switch below them, which has different functions on connected synthesizers. The G-77 was also available fretless as the G-77FL.

Features

  • Control bass for Roland GR-series bass synthesizers (GR-33B or GR-77B)
  • 24-pin connector for synthesizer control signal
  • Special individual string pickups
  • Standard bass output from two regular pickups
  • Can be used as a standalone bass guitar
  • Unique 'handle' design
  • Fiberglass neck stabilizer to prevent unwanted vibrations
  • Body constructed from alder and maple
  • Available in ivory white, red, and black finishes
  • Controls include Master Volume, Pickup Balance, Guitar Tone, Bass/Synthesizer Balance, Cutoff, Resonance (VCF) or EDIT, Vibrato Intensity
  • Three-stage mode switch for connected synthesizers
  • Also available as the fretless G-77FL model
