Roland G-77

Bass Guitar Year: 1985

Description

The G-77 is a controller bass for Roland bass synthesizers with a 24-pin connector. It has a unique design with a "handle" and a fiberglass stabilizer to prevent unwanted neck vibrations. Besides the 24-pin connector for the individual string pickup signals to control GR synthesizers (GR-33B or GR-77B), the bass also has a standard bass output for the sound of its two normal pickups. Thus, it functions as a regular bass guitar, though its shape can make it prone to slipping when playing seated. The G-77 is made of alder and maple (along with the fiberglass stabilizer) and came in three colors: (ivory) white, red, and black. Onboard controls include a large (plastic) master volume knob, six smaller black knobs for pickup balance (between the two normal pickups), guitar tone, balance (between normal bass and bass synthesizer), cutoff, resonance (VCF) or EDIT, vibrato intensity, and a three-way mode switch underneath with varying functions depending on the connected synthesizer.

Features

  • 24-pin connector for GR synthesizers
  • Standard bass output
  • Fiberglass stabilizer
  • Alder and maple body
  • Onboard controls for tone and balance
