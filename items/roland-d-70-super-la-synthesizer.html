Roland D-70 Super LA Synthesizer

Digital Synthesizer Year: 1990

Description

The D-70 is a 6-part multi-timbral, digital MIDI synthesizer with 76 velocity-sensitive synthesizer keys and Channel Aftertouch. The so-called Advanced LA synthesis sound engine of the D-70 is a maximum 30-voice evolution of the U- and D-series, combining up to 4 Tones with PCM oscillators and dynamic filters. Originally, the D-70 was intended to be called U-50, as its sound engine builds upon the U-series and can utilize ROM samples from SN-U110 cards for the U-series. However, the dynamic filters were adopted from the D-series. The keyboard can supply 4 zones with layer and split sounds, with the send channel for each zone being separately enabled and configured.

  • Dimensions: 1197 x 100 x 305 mm
