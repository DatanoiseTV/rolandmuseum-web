Boss RV-2

Digital Reverb Year: 1986

Description

The Boss RV-2 is a digital reverb pedal offering studio-quality reverb in a compact format. It uses BOSS's DSP (Digital Signal Processing) technology to simulate various spaces, from large halls to small clubs. Six reverb modes are available: Room, two types of Hall, Plate, gated reverb, and Panning Delay. Reverb time is adjustable from 0.2 to 10 seconds, and a built-in pre-EQ allows for tonal shaping, from bright and clear to warm and bass-heavy. The RV-2 also includes stereo outputs for enhanced spatial effects.

Features

  • Digital reverb
  • Six reverb modes: Room, Hall1, Hall2, Plate, Gated Reverb, Panning Delay
  • Adjustable reverb time (0.2 - 10 seconds)
  • Built-in pre-EQ
  • Stereo outputs
