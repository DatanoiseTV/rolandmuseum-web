Roland MC-307

Groove Box Year: 2000

Description

The Groove Box MC-307 combines an 8-part multitimbral digital synthesizer and a pattern sequencer. Its sound generation principle is based on the 4-Tone-10 Structure architecture of the JV/XP series, but with ROM samples replaced by dance-oriented sounds. Parameters are fully compatible with the MC-505. Compared to the MC-505, the MC-307 has an additional bank of PCM samples and newer, fresher preset patterns and patches as a basis for custom grooves. However, it lacks the Megamix mode and has only 4 rotary encoders instead of the 8 sliders found on the MC-505 for controlling parts. Tempo changes, like turntable scratching, or pitch and tempo adjustments can be quickly controlled with the Turntable Emulation knob.

Features

  • 8-part multitimbral digital synthesizer
  • Pattern sequencer
  • Sound generation based on 4-Tone-10 Structure architecture (from JV/XP series)
  • ROM samples optimized for dance music
  • Parameters fully compatible with MC-505
  • Additional bank of PCM samples (compared to MC-505)
  • Newer preset patterns and patches
  • 4 rotary encoders for part control
  • Turntable Emulation knob for quick tempo and pitch changes
  • Dimensions: 422 x 98 x 277 mm
  • Weight: 2.2 kg
  • Power supply: 1A AC
  • No Megamix mode
