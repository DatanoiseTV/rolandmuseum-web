Roland GI-10

Guitar-to-MIDI Interface Year: 1995

Description

The Roland GI-10 is a Guitar-To-MIDI interface for guitars with a 13-pin connection and GK-pickup (GK-2, GK-3, etc.) in a 9.5-inch housing (1U 19-inch, half width). It converts signals from the hexaphonic GK-pickup into MIDI notes and other associated MIDI performance data. In addition to the GK IN jack (13-pin) on the front, there is a jack input for connecting a (normal) guitar or a microphone. If a plug is inserted here, the GI-10 no longer converts the GK-pickup signal, but rather monophonically sung or played signals into MIDI data, whereby one must maintain a very precise level and sing or play the notes distinctly. For the main purpose, the conversion of a GK signal, the sensitivity for individual strings can be set in 8 steps, and it can be determined whether the MIDI data should be transmitted polyphonically on an adjustable channel or in mono mode on 6 consecutive MIDI channels. The bend range can be set to 0, 2, 12, or 24 semitones, the note output can be shifted up or down by 1 octave, and for fingerpicking, the output can be optimized in four stages, with a setting without dynamics also available. A tuner is built-in, whose concert pitch can be set to either 440 Hz or 442 Hz. On the rear, an expression pedal (CC#01 or CC#11) and two footswitches can be connected with an insert cable. Footswitch 1 holds the information of the last played string and blocks new performance data; footswitch 2 sends CC#64 (sustain/hold-1) to the connected MIDI sound generator. MIDI IN can loop through program changes, and MIDI OUT outputs the MIDI data from the GI-10. The GI-10 converts guitar signals similar to the GR-09 or GR-1, meaning one must play quite firmly and precisely. The conversion is fast, but with noticeable latency, especially for low notes. The GI-10 only converts notes with pitches within the range of a guitar. The GI-10 cannot be used for converting bass guitars with GK-2B or GK-3B pickups.

Features

  • Guitar-To-MIDI interface
  • 13-pin connection for GK-pickups (GK-2, GK-3)
  • Converts hexaphonic GK-pickup signals to MIDI notes
  • Monophonic input for standard guitar or microphone
  • 8-step sensitivity adjustment for individual strings
  • Polyphonic MIDI output on adjustable channel
  • Mono mode MIDI output on 6 consecutive MIDI channels
  • Bend range settings: 0, 2, 12, or 24 semitones
  • Note output shift: +/- 1 octave
  • Four-stage optimization for fingerpicking (including no dynamics option)
  • Built-in tuner (440 Hz or 442 Hz concert pitch)
  • Expression pedal input (CC#01 or CC#11)
  • Two footswitch inputs (one for holding last played string, one for CC#64 sustain)
  • MIDI IN for program change passthrough
  • MIDI OUT for GI-10 data
  • Noticeable latency, especially on low notes
  • Not compatible with bass GK-pickups (GK-2B, GK-3B)
  • 9.5-inch housing (half-rack width)
