Roland 132 System 100M, Dual C/V Audio Mixer and Voltage Processor

Eurorack / Modular Year: 1979

Description

The Roland 132 is a Dual CV/Audio Mixer and Processor Module for the System-100M. It features two 4-channel mixers with direct and inverted outputs, capable of mixing both audio signals and control voltages. Two channels are equipped with positive and negative fixed voltages, which can also be used as normal mixer channels via patch jacks. A red LED indicates output overload (distortion). The Voltage Processor consists of two sections, one for positive and one for negative variable voltage. The mixer's frequency range extends from DC to 40 kHz AC, and with a signal-to-noise ratio of more than 90 dB, this module offers highest studio quality.

Features

  • Dual CV/Audio Mixer and Processor module
  • Part of the Roland System-100M modular synthesizer series
  • Two independent 4-channel mixers
  • Direct and inverted outputs for each mixer
  • Suitable for mixing both audio signals and control voltages (CV)
  • Two channels with positive and negative fixed voltages (patchable as regular mixer channels)
  • Red LED indicator for output overload/distortion
  • Voltage Processor section with positive and negative variable voltage outputs
  • Mixer frequency range: DC (direct current) to 40 kHz AC (alternating current)
  • Signal-to-noise ratio: > 90 dB (studio quality)
  • Dimensions: 104 x 230 x 199 mm
  • Weight: 1.6 kg
  • Power supplied via Roland 190, 191, or 191J module cases
