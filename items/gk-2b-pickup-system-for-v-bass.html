GK-2B Pickup System for V-Bass

Synthesizer Addons Year: 2002

Description

The Roland GK-2B is a hexaphonic pickup with a 13-pin connector for mounting on a standard bass, designed to control 13-pin bass synthesizers or virtual bass systems. Unlike normal pickups, a hexaphonic pickup captures each string individually, providing 4 to 6 separate signals depending on the number of strings on the bass. The GK-2B is essentially two parts: the pickup itself, which should be mounted about 10mm behind the bridge, and a small control unit connected via a short cable, containing the controls. The control unit, fixed to the strap button and bass surface with a special bracket, has a mini-jack input to connect the signal from the normal pickups, allowing it to also be transmitted via the 13-pin cable. The control unit features two buttons "Up/S2" and "Down/S1", a control for (synth) volume, a switch for BASS to MIX to pure GK signal, and the 13-pin connector for the GK cable. The GK-2B is not a MIDI pickup, as often incorrectly stated, but rather the prerequisite for a 13-pin device (e.g., V-Bass, GR-20, GI-20, VB-99, etc.) to convert its signals into MIDI.

Features

  • Hexaphonic pickup system
  • 13-pin connector for synth/virtual bass systems
  • Individual string signal capture (4-6 signals)
  • Two-part design: pickup and control unit
  • Control unit with mini-jack input for normal bass pickups
  • "Up/S2" and "Down/S1" buttons
  • Synth volume control
  • Switch for BASS / MIX / pure GK signal
  • 13-pin GK cable connection
  • Enables MIDI conversion by compatible 13-pin devices (not a MIDI pickup itself)
