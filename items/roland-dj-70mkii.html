Roland DJ-70MKII

Sampler Year: 1997

Description

The Roland DJ-70MKII is a stereo sampler featuring a 3-octave velocity-sensitive keyboard. It's 24-voice, 31-part multitimbral, and includes an 8-track sequencer with RPS (real-time playback). It uses the same data format as the S-700 series, layering up to 4 samples in partials to form patches of up to 88 samples. Each partial offers dynamic amps and filters with resonance. The DJ-70MKII can load samples while playing and process sound disks from the S-330 and S-550. It has 4MB of sample memory, expandable to 32MB. Storage is via floppy disk drive (DD and HD) and SCSI-connected hard drives. A unique feature is the scratch dial, allowing for turntable-style scratching of samples. Data written by the DJ-70MKII is not readable by other devices in the S-700 series.

Features

  • 3-octave velocity-sensitive keyboard
  • 24-voice
  • 31-part multitimbral
  • 8-track sequencer with RPS
  • 4MB sample memory (expandable to 32MB)
  • Floppy disk drive (DD and HD)
  • SCSI hard drive support
  • Scratch dial
