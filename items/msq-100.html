Roland MSQ-100

Digital MIDI Keyboard Recorder Year: 1984

Description

The MSQ-100 is a simple single-track MIDI sequencer capable of recording data from all 16 MIDI channels. Notes can be recorded in real-time or via step-entry, and overdubs are possible (e.g., to record data from other MIDI channels in subsequent passes). The MSQ-100 can operate as a master or slave (both MIDI and DIN SYNC) and data can be stored on tape/cassette recorders (using an FSK signal, which can nowadays also be recorded with an HD recorder). The MSQ-100 does not have detailed editing functions and can thus be considered the MIDI successor to the CSQ-100 or the DCB version JSQ-60. Switches on the rear allow individual control over whether aftertouch, pitch bend, controller data, or velocity (touch dynamics) are recorded. The MSQ-100 offers storage space for approx. 3000 notes (velocity enabled) or 6100 notes (velocity disabled).

Features

  • Single-track MIDI sequencer
  • Records data from all 16 MIDI channels
  • Real-time and step-entry recording
  • Overdubbing capability
  • Master or slave operation (MIDI and DIN SYNC)
  • Data storage via FSK signal to tape/cassette recorder
  • No detailed editing functions
  • Rear switches to select recording of aftertouch, pitch bend, controller data, or velocity
  • Storage for approximately 3000 notes (velocity enabled) or 6100 notes (velocity disabled)
  • Powered by external BOSS PSA power supply
