Roland Roland MKS-100 MIDI Sampler-Modul

MIDI Sampler Module Year: 1986

Description

The Roland MKS-100 'Digital Sampler' is an 8-voice sampler module housed in a 19-inch, 2U rack-mount format, featuring a built-in 2.8-inch floppy disk drive (Quick-Disk format, or QD). The MKS-100 samples at 12-bit resolution with selectable sampling rates of either 15kHz or 30kHz. Its internal 256kB memory is divided into 4 banks of 64kB each, allowing for a total sample length of up to 4.4 seconds, or approximately 1.1 seconds per bank. For recording 'long' samples, two banks can be combined. Samples can be edited within the device, assigned a dynamic envelope, and looped; however, it lacks a dynamic filter. The maximum of 4 samples can be arranged side-by-side (split) or layered across the note range (from note 24 to 104) when the MKS-100 is controlled in MIDI Poly mode. In Mono mode, different samples can be addressed on four consecutive channels. Unlike its 'big brothers' such as the S-50 and S-330, monitor and mouse operation is not provided; the MKS-100 relies on its front panel controls and a single-line display for operation.

  • 19-inch rack format (2U high)
  • Built-in 2.8-inch floppy disk drive (Quick-Disk format)
  • 12-bit sampling resolution
  • Selectable sampling rates: 15kHz or 30kHz
  • 256kB internal memory, divided into 4 banks of 64kB each
  • Maximum total sample length: 4.4 seconds
  • Maximum sample length per bank: approx. 1.1 seconds
  • Supports combining two banks for longer samples
  • Samples can be edited within the device
  • Features a dynamic envelope
  • Looping capability for samples
  • Does not include a dynamic filter
  • Supports up to 4 samples simultaneously
  • Samples can be split or layered across the note range (N. 24-104) in MIDI Poly mode
  • Mono mode allows addressing different samples on four consecutive channels
  • Operated via front panel controls and a single-line display (no monitor/mouse support)
  • Built-in power supply
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 482 x 90 x 410 mm
  • Weight: 7 kg
  • Power consumption: 19W
  • Includes Roland 2-pin power cable, PJ-1 audio cable (2 x 2.5m), DemoDisk 2.8"
