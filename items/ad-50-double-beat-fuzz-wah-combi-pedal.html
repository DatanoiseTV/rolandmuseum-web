AD-50 'Double Beat' Fuzz-Wah Combi-Pedal

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1973

Description

The Roland AD-50 'DOUBLE BEAT' is a floor effect pedal for guitar housed in a sturdy, black die-cast casing. It combines the AW-10 Wah pedal with a FUZZ effect. The Wah effect can be switched on or off by fully pressing the pedal. Located on the widened base next to the pedal are the controls for the Fuzz: a switch for FUZZ/NORMAL, a tone rotary switch with Sine, Square, and Peaks positions, an OUT LEVEL knob, and a SUSTAIN knob. The 'INSTRUMENT' jack input is on the left side (unlike most other Roland effects), and the mono jack output is on the right, which makes connecting multiple effects more difficult. The battery can be changed by opening the battery compartment cover, which is secured with two knurled screws on the underside. There is no power adapter connection. The AD-50 was manufactured by the Roland Music Engineering Group (Roland MEG), which was later renamed 'Boss'.

Features

  • Fuzz-Wah combo pedal
  • Sturdy, black die-cast housing
  • Wah effect (switchable when fully pressed)
  • Fuzz controls: FUZZ/NORMAL switch, Tone rotary switch (Sine, Square, Peaks), OUT LEVEL, SUSTAIN
  • Instrument input (left side, jack)
  • Mono output (right side, jack)
  • Battery operated (1 x 9V block)
  • No power adapter connection
