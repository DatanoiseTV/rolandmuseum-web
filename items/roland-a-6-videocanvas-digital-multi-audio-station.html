Roland A-6 VideoCanvas Digital Multi Audio Station

Recording Year: 1998

Description

The Roland A-6 'Video Canvas' is a 4-track (stereo) hard disk recorder with an alternative clip section, where audio phrases can be played directly using 16 pads. The A-6 records to a built-in hard drive (6.4GB), has a built-in 5-channel mixer (stereo, 4 HD track pairs, and pad section), and audio effects. An optional expansion board can be installed to add a SCSI connection, digital S/P-DIF connections (In, Out, coaxial), and XLR connections. The phrase player can play as many clips simultaneously as there are inactive hard disk tracks; meaning the maximum number of tracks and clips can only ever be four. It doesn't matter whether a track or a clip is mono or stereo. The hard disk recorder operates non-destructively and offers extensive editing functions. In principle, the A-6 is structured similarly to the phrase sampler SP-808, which, however, uses ZIP media instead of a hard drive for storage. The A-6 was shipped from the factory with a collection of film-typical ambient samples already loaded onto the hard drive.

  • 4-track (stereo) hard disk recorder
  • Alternative clip section with 16 pads for direct playback of audio phrases
  • Built-in 6.4GB hard drive
  • Built-in 5-channel mixer (stereo, 4 HD track pairs, and pad section)
  • Audio effects
  • Optional expansion board for SCSI, digital S/P-DIF (In, Out, coaxial), and XLR connections
  • Phrase player can play a maximum of four tracks and clips simultaneously
  • Non-destructive hard disk recording
  • Extensive editing functions
  • Similar structure to phrase sampler SP-808
  • Shipped with film-typical ambient samples pre-loaded on the hard drive
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 394 mm x 99 mm x 343 mm
  • Weight: 4.1 kg
  • Power consumption: 16W
  • Built-in power supply, IEC power cable
  • Included accessory: IEC power cable
  • Recommended accessories: HDPA6 hard drive, A6-OP1 Multi I/O Expansion, SI-80S/SP Video Synchronizer
