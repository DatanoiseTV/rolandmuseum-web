Roland SH-5

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1975

Description

The SH-5 is a substantial, monophonic analog synthesizer equipped with 44 non-velocity-sensitive keys, permanently integrated into a flight case with an angled control surface. It features two VCOs, each offering selectable triangle, sawtooth, square, and modulatable pulse waveforms, which can be ring-modulated and mixed with adjustable noise. The sound passes through a fixed bandpass filter, a dynamic multi-filter (Hi, Band, or Low), and a VCA, with the envelope shaped by an ADSR and an AR generator. Modulation is provided by Portamento, two LFOs, a Sample & Hold generator, and the innovative pitch bend lever, first introduced with the SH-5. It offers extensive control connections but no built-in audio effects.

Features

  • Monophonic analog synthesizer
  • 44 non-velocity-sensitive keys
  • Built into a flight case with angled control surface
  • 2 VCOs with selectable waveforms (triangle, sawtooth, square, pulse, modulatable)
  • Ring modulation capability
  • Mixer with adjustable noise
  • Fixed bandpass filter
  • Dynamic multi-filter (Hi, Band, Low)
  • VCA
  • ADSR envelope generator
  • AR envelope generator
  • Portamento
  • 2 LFOs
  • Sample & Hold generator
  • Pitch bend lever (first introduced with SH-5)
  • Numerous control connections
  • No audio effects
  • Dimensions: 864 x 257 x 270/335 mm
  • Weight: 22 kg
  • Integrated power supply with fixed power cable
  • Includes PJ-1 audio cables (2 x 2.5m)
  • Recommended accessories: DP-1 Footswitch (Sustain Pedal), FV-1 Volume Pedal, KS-10 Stand
