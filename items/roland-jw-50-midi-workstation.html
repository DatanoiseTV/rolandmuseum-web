Roland JW-50 MIDI-Workstation

Digital Synthesizer Year: 1992

Description

The JW-50 is a 24-voice, 16-part multitimbral, GS-compatible digital synthesizer with 61 velocity-sensitive keys and aftertouch. It features a built-in SMF sequencer with a (DD) floppy drive, capable of recording 8 internal songs and offering a backing function with 30 styles. The sound generation is roughly equivalent to the SC-55 module, and sounds can only be slightly altered (Vibrato Rate/Depth/Delay, Filter Resonance/Cutoff, and Amp Attack/Decay/Release). Chorus and Reverb effects are on board, and 8 faders can be used to control part volumes, among other things.
