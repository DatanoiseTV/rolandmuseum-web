Boss TU-60

Tuner Year: 1978

Description

The TU-60 is a guitar tuner featuring a rotary switch for selecting the 6 strings, a built-in microphone for acoustic guitars, and 8 LEDs for indicating correct tuning. These 8 LEDs function as an interference 'running light', blinking with varying brightness. When the brightest light remains in the same position, the tuning is correct; otherwise, the maximum brightness 'moves' left or right depending on whether the string is tuned too high or too low.

Features

  • Rotary switch for selecting 6 strings
  • Built-in microphone for acoustic guitars
  • 8 LEDs for tuning indication (interference running light)
  • Concert pitch adjustable between 440 and 445 Hz
  • Requires calibration before tuning by setting the CALIBRATION knob with the switch on CHECK
  • Battery operated: 1 x 9V block (6LR61)
  • Power consumption: 10mA
  • Dimensions: 92 (W) x 38 (H) x 154 (D) mm
  • Weight: 0.26 kg
  • Similar models: TU-120
  • Successors: TU-12, TU-100
