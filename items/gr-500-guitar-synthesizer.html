GR-500 Guitar Synthesizer

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1977

Description

The Roland GR-500 is the first polyphonic, analog guitar synthesizer ever, featuring a 24-pin connection and exclusively controllable with the special GS-500 guitar. Its controls are intuitively laid out across five sections on the angled surface of its box-shaped casing. Achieving satisfactory results requires a certain level of playing accuracy and practice.

Features

  • Polyphonic, analog guitar synthesizer
  • 24-pin connection
  • Controllable only with the special GS-500 guitar
  • Five main sections for control
  • Common Threshold control for synthesizer response sensitivity
  • Headphone jack, Master Volume (Level) control, and power switch
  • Section (G) for pure guitar signal, accessible only at the GR-500
  • Tone control (EQ Freq), EQ on/off switch, and output selector switch in guitar section
  • Three output summing buses allowing each section's signal to be routed to a specific sum; three outputs (CH1 to CH3) and a MIX output are on the rear
  • POLYENSEMBLE section (P) converts signals from all 6 guitar strings into synthesizer signals of the same pitch, but without following dynamics. Features Attack, Decay, and Sustain sliders for envelope shaping. Can sustain a note for up to 5 minutes
  • POLYENSEMBLE tone adjustable with four sliders: F (fundamental volume), L (low), M (middle), H (high) for overtones
  • Monophonic Bass section with last-note priority. Can react to all strings, lowest three, or lowest two strings. Tone mixed by three sliders: Percussion, Soft, and Hard; velocity-sensitive, operates in 16-foot range
  • Three envelope sliders in Bass section, with a switch for Short/Long release time
  • Main SOLO section functions as a classic monophonic synthesizer with last-note priority: VCO with 8-foot saw, 8-foot and 16-foot square waves with pulse width modulation are mixed, sent through a low-pass filter (VCF, 24dB/oct) and then a VCA. The filter can be modulated by LFO, envelope, and velocity
  • Control section for an external synthesizer, featuring Portamento and Transpose controls, providing CV/Gate signals on the rear
  • On/off states and volumes for individual sections controlled via the GS-500 control guitar
  • Included accessory: C-24B 24-pin cable
  • Recommended accessories: KS-10A stand, PC-50 Preset Controller, GS-500 Guitar
