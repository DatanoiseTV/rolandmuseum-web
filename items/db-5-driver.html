Boss DB-5 Driver

Guitar/Bass Effects Pedal Year: 1978

Description

From the 1978 Roland 'Perfect Sound Systems' catalog: An effects unit with a 5-band equalizer and selector switch for distortion and attack. Ideal for electric guitars, electric basses, and as an addition for synthesizers.

Features

  • 5-band equalizer
  • Selector switch for distortion and attack
  • Sliders for +/- 12 dB adjustment at 100 Hz, 300Hz, 600 Hz, 1.2 kHz, and 3 kHz
  • Battery or ACA-220 power adapter operation
