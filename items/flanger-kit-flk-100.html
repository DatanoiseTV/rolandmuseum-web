AMDEK Flanger Kit (FLK-100)

Flanger Effect Kit Year: 1982

Description

The four controls (Manual, Depth, Rate, and Resonance) ensure the exceptionally wide effect range of the flanger. Special bucket brigade devices provide maximum stability. AMDEK provides double the enjoyment: the pleasure of assembly and the pride of accomplishment, followed by the satisfaction of using a high-quality device that stands up to professional comparisons. AMDEK offers musicians many opportunities, perhaps previously unrecognized, to engage with their music and the equipment they use. Even if you have never seen the inside of an effect pedal, you can still assemble an AMDEK device.

Features

  • Flanger effect
  • Four controls: Manual, Depth, Rate, Resonance
  • Wide effect range
  • Utilizes Bucket Brigade Devices (BBDs) for stability
  • DIY (Do-It-Yourself) kit
  • Function: Effect changeover switch, Effect indicator
  • Terminals: Input, Output, External power source
  • Delay time: 1 ms - 13 ms
  • LFO speed: 100 ms - 16s
  • Residual noise: -95 dB (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 470 kOhm
  • Output load impedance: Over 10 kOhm
  • Power source: 006P battery or external power source
  • Consumption current: 15 mA (DC 9V)
  • Dimensions: 95 (W) x 64 (H) x 143 (D) mm
  • Weight: 520 g
  • Professional metal casing with pre-drilled holes
  • Crackle-free on/off switch
  • LED indicator for operation status
