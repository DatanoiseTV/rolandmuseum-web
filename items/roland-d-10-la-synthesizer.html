Roland D-10 LA-Synthesizer

Digital Synthesizer Year: 1988

Description

The D-10 is a 9-part multi-timbral, 32-voice digital synthesizer with 61 velocity-sensitive synthesizer keys and MIDI. 8 tonally playable parts (sub-synthesizers) and one rhythm part utilize LA synthesis, a combination of PCM samples without filters and simpler synthesis with filters. A sound program is called 'Timbre' and consists of 2 'Tones', which in turn are formed from one or two 'Partials'. A Partial is either a PCM oscillator (ROM samples) with a subsequent amplifier or it consists of a simple, pulse-width modifiable oscillator (triangle/square wave) with a dynamic filter and amplifier. 13 structures are selectable for connecting the two Partials to a Tone, so a sound program (Timbre) can consist of up to 4 oscillators. The filter offers resonance without self-oscillation and sounds exceptionally 'warm'. The 256 internally available PCM samples are slightly shorter than those of the first LA synthesizer D-50. The sound generation is the same as in the D-5, D-20, and D-110, concluding with a digital reverb offering 8 algorithms. For easier programming, a PG-10 programmer can be connected. The D-10 and other models in the D-series were the first synthesizers to have demo songs in ROM. The D-10 has a built-in drum computer with which one can compose a song from 1- to 2-bar patterns or play sounds manually in Manual mode. There are 32 preset and 32 freely programmable patterns. The D-10 has two operating modes, Performance and Multitimbral. For live performance, two sounds (parts) can be layered or split in Performance mode. In Multitimbral mode, 8 parts can be controlled simultaneously via MIDI. 128 Performances, 64 Tones, and 64 Timbres can be stored internally; an inserted M-128 or M-256 memory card doubles the memory.

Features

  • 9-part multi-timbral
  • 32 voices
  • Digital synthesizer
  • 61 velocity-sensitive keys
  • MIDI connectivity
  • LA synthesis (PCM samples + simple synthesis with filter)
  • 8 tonal parts
  • 1 rhythm part
  • Sound program 'Timbre' (composed of 2 'Tones')
  • 'Tones' from 1 or 2 'Partials'
  • Partial: PCM oscillator (ROM samples) with amp, or simple PWM oscillator (triangle/square) with dynamic filter and amp
  • 13 partial connection structures
  • Filter with resonance (no self-oscillation)
  • 256 internal PCM samples
  • Digital reverb with 8 algorithms
  • Connectable to PG-10 programmer
  • Demo songs in ROM
  • Built-in drum computer
  • 32 preset, 32 user-programmable drum patterns
  • Performance and Multitimbral modes
  • Performance mode: layer or split two sounds
  • Multitimbral mode: 8 parts simultaneous MIDI control
  • 128 Performances, 64 Tones, 64 Timbres internal storage
  • M-128 or M-256 memory card doubles storage
  • Dimensions: 974 x 98 x 301 mm
  • Weight: 8.8 kg
  • Built-in power supply, fixed power cable
