Boss PS-2 Digital Pitch Shifter/Delay

Generated Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1986

Description

The PS-2 Digital Pitch Shifter/Delay provides advanced pitch shifting and delay effects in a compact format. At its heart is an LSI chip, a BOSS proprietary development, enabling pitch shifting over a full octave range from -1 to +1 with high speed, pitch-shifted chorus and octave effects, as well as a variety of harmony effects. For immediate and precise octave effects, the PS-2 is equipped with preset +1 and -1 octave modes. A chromatic tuner, such as the TU-12, can be connected to the Tuner Out jack to check the amount of pitch shifting in manual mode. Metallic-sounding effects are easily adjusted by mixing the direct sound with the pitch-shifted sound of up to +1 octave. To obtain a really dense sound, you must mix the direct sound with the pitch-shifted sound of -1 octave. The PS-2 ensures clear effects even when playing chords. For harmonic effects, mix the direct sound with pitch-shifted thirds or fifths. The PS-2 can also be used as a high-quality digital delay, offering up to 2,000 ms delay. Technical Data: Power Supply: 9V battery, AC adapter (PSA series); Power Consumption: 9V, 60 mA; Pitch Change: -1 to +1 octave; Delay Time: 30 ms to 2,000 ms; Frequency Response (direct): 10 Hz to 30 kHz, +1 dB, -3 dB; (effect): 40 Hz to 6 kHz, +1 dB, -3 dB; Signal-to-Noise Ratio: -90 dBm or less (IHF-A); Input Impedance: 1 MOhm; Output Impedance: over 10 kOhm; Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm; Weight: 450 g
