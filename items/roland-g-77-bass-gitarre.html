Roland Roland G-77 Bass-Gitarre

Bass Guitar Controller Year: 1985

Description

The G-77 is a controller bass for Roland bass synthesizers with a 24-pin connector and features a very distinctive 'with handle' design, a fiberglass stabilizer intended to prevent unwanted neck vibrations. In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal of the special individual string pickups used to control GR synthesizers (GR-33B or GR-77B), the bass also has a normal bass output from which the sound of the two normal pickups is emitted. The G-77 can therefore also be used as a regular bass, although its shape poses a risk of slipping when played sitting down. Besides the fiberglass stabilizer, the bass is made of alder and maple and was delivered in three colors: (ivory) white, red, and black.

Features

  • Controller bass for Roland bass synthesizers
  • 24-pin connector
  • Distinctive 'handle' design
  • Fiberglass stabilizer to prevent unwanted neck vibrations
  • Special individual string pickups for controlling GR synthesizers (GR-33B or GR-77B)
  • Normal bass output for the sound of two regular pickups
  • Can also be used as a regular bass
  • Bass made of alder and maple
  • Available in three colors: (ivory) white, red, and black
  • Controls for Master Volume, Pickup Balance (between two normal pickups), Guitar Tone, Bass/Synthesizer Balance, Cutoff, Resonance (VCF) or EDIT, and Vibrato Strength
  • Three-stage mode switch
  • Also available fretless as G-77FL
