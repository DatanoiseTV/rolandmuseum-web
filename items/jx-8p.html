Roland JX-8P

Polyphonic MIDI Synthesizer Year: 1984

Description

The JX-8P is a 6-voice, digitally controlled analog synthesizer with 61 velocity-sensitive keys, offering 32 preset and 32 user sounds. Each voice includes 2 DCOs with cross-modulation, 1 resonant DCF, 1 dynamic DCA, 2 envelopes, and 1 LFO. The VCA can be switched to Gate mode. It operates in Omni mode or with an adjustable MIDI channel and supports the separately available PG-800 programmer for parameter editing.

Features

  • 6-voice polyphony
  • Digitally Controlled Analog Synthesizer (DCOs, DCF, DCA)
  • 61 velocity-sensitive keys
  • 32 preset sounds
  • 32 user sounds
  • 2 DCOs per voice (with selectable pulse width and cross-modulation; LFO-driven pulse width modulation not available)
  • 1 resonant, dynamic DCF (Digitally Controlled Filter)
  • 1 dynamic DCA (Digitally Controlled Amplifier)
  • 2 envelopes per voice
  • 1 LFO per voice
  • VCA switchable to Gate mode
  • Operates in Omni mode or with adjustable MIDI channel
  • Compatible with separately available PG-800 programmer
  • Dimensions: 977 (W) x 92 (H) x 375 (D) mm
  • Weight: 11.5 kg
  • Power consumption: 25W
  • Built-in power supply
