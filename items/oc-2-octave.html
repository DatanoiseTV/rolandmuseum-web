Boss OC-2 Octave

Octave Effect Pedal Year: 1982

Description

The BOSS OC-2 is a sophisticated octave divider featuring a superior tracking system that accurately follows melodic lines without glitches. Its second key innovation is the high-quality tone it produces, generating two tones: one an octave lower and another two octaves lower than the played note. These tones are deep, bassy, and round, distinctly different from the nasal square wave sounds of earlier octave dividers. The three controls allow mixing the direct signal with Octave 1 and Octave 2. Widely used by guitarists to add bass lines, its high-quality tracking also makes it suitable for keyboards, woodwinds, and brass instruments to enhance solos into an ensemble.

Features

  • Superior tracking system
  • Generates two octaves lower (Octave 1 and Octave 2)
  • Deep, bassy, and round octave tones
  • Three level controls for Direct, Octave 1, and Octave 2 signals
  • Suitable for guitar, keyboards, woodwinds, and brass instruments
  • Power supply: 9V battery, AC adapter (ACA series)
  • Current draw: 9V, 4 mA
  • Maximum input level: -5 dBm
  • Maximum output level: 0 dBm
  • Minimum operating input level: -60 dBm (at 250 Hz)
  • Equivalent input noise: -100 dBm (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 400 g
