Roland VP-70 MIDI Voice Prozessor

Voice Processor Year: 1987

Description

The VP-70 is a 4-channel pitch shifter with a voice-to-MIDI function in a 19-inch rack-mountable (1U) enclosure. The pitch can be shifted statically by specifying intervals or dynamically based on incoming MIDI notes, with a maximum shift of 1 octave.

Features

  • 4-channel pitch shifter
  • Voice-to-MIDI function
  • 19-inch rack-mountable (1U) enclosure
  • Pitch can be shifted statically by specifying intervals
  • Pitch can be shifted dynamically based on incoming MIDI notes
  • Maximum pitch shift: 1 octave
  • Dimensions: 482(W) x 44(H) x 294(D) mm
  • Weight: 4 kg
  • Power consumption: 20W
  • Built-in power supply
  • Recommended accessories: DP-2 Footswitch (Sustain Pedal), FS-5U Footswitch, FC-100 RRC-Foot Controller
