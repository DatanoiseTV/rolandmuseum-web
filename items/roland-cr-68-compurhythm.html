Roland CR-68 CompuRhythm

Drum Machine / Rhythm Year: 1978

Description

The Roland CR-68 rhythm machine, externally named "CompuRhythm CR-68", is an analog rhythm machine with 19 fixed rhythms that can be selected via push buttons. Seven additional fill-in rhythms or a pause can be triggered manually via a button, or automatically every 2, 4, 8, 12, or 16 bars via a rotary switch. An ACCENT knob allows adjusting the intensity of accents, and a BALANCE knob controls the volume proportion of high-frequency instruments in the overall sound. TEMPO and VOLUME knobs, START/STOP button, and power switch are also on the front of the CR-68, which is housed in a rectangular wooden casing. On the rear, there is a mono output (2 jacks for high and low levels), two connections for buttons (Start/Stop and Variation), and a trigger impulse output, switchable between 8th and 16th notes. The tempo can only be adjusted via the rotary knob and is indicated by an LED on the front. External synchronization is not possible. The CR-68 and CR-78 represent the third generation of Roland rhythm machines. The rhythms, each with a variation, include: Rock 1, Rock 2, Rock 3, Rock 4, Disco 1, Disco 2, Waltz, Shuffle, Slow Rock, Swing, Fox Trot (Variation: Tango), Boogie, Enka, Bossa Nova, Samba, Mambo (Variation: Cha Cha), Beguine (Variation: Rhumba).

Features

  • Analog rhythm machine
  • 19 fixed rhythms
  • 7 additional fill-in rhythms
  • Automatic fill-in trigger (2, 4, 8, 12, or 16 bars)
  • ACCENT and BALANCE controls
  • TEMPO and VOLUME controls
  • START/STOP button
  • Rectangular wooden casing
  • Mono output (High/Low level jacks)
  • Connections for external Start/Stop and Variation buttons
  • Trigger impulse output (switchable 8th/16th notes)
  • LED tempo indicator
  • No external synchronization
  • Rhythms include: Rock 1-4, Disco 1-2, Waltz, Shuffle, Slow Rock, Swing, Fox Trot (Tango), Boogie, Enka, Bossa Nova, Samba, Mambo (Cha Cha), Beguine (Rhumba)
