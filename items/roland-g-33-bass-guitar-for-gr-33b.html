Roland G-33 Bass Guitar for GR-33B

Other Year: 1980

Description

The G-33 is a controller bass guitar for the Roland GR-33B bass guitar synthesizer (with a 24-pin connector), featuring the body shape of a typical Fender bass. Alongside the 24-pin connector for its special pickup (which outputs individual signals for the four strings, enabling GR-synthesizer control), it also has a standard bass output for its single-coil pickup with dual magnets. This means the G-33 can also be used as a conventional bass guitar.

Features

  • Dimensions: 342 x 1182 x 66 mm
  • Weight: 4.4 kg
  • No battery or external power supply required
  • 24-pin connector for Roland GR-33B bass guitar synthesizer
  • Special pickup for individual string signal output (four strings)
  • Standard bass output with a single-coil pickup featuring dual magnets
  • Maple neck glued to an ash body
  • Also available as a left-handed model (G-33LH)
  • Controls: Master Volume, Cutoff Frequency, Balance (Bass/Synthesizer level), separate Bass and Treble controls, Resonance (VCF), Vibrato Strength
  • Three-stage selector switch (Bass, Synthesizer, or Both)
  • Manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (Fuji Gen Gakki) to Roland specifications
