AMDEK TAK-100

Other Year: 1982

Description

The AMDEK TAK-100 is a small tuning kit with a headphone jack and input for a mini amp. It's a kit that needed assembly by the user. The kit included high quality components. The device had multiple modes (AMP, 220, 440, 880 Hz) for tuning different instruments.

Features

  • Headphone output
  • Input jack
  • Mini amp compatible
  • Multiple tuning modes
  • High-quality components
  • Requires assembly
