Roland TR-808

Drum Machine / Rhythm Year: 1980

Description

The Roland TR-808 Rhythm Composer is an analog, pattern-based drum computer. Its 16 sounds can be output via individual outputs and sometimes modified in sound, decay, or pitch. The sound is very powerful and should be familiar to most. Unlike earlier Roland rhythm machines, the TR-808 has no preset rhythms; it's purely freely programmable. However, 3 tracks were pre-loaded upon delivery, but these can be deleted. A maximum of 12 songs (called tracks here), each with 64 patterns, can be stored. The TR-808 can be synchronized with other DIN-SYNC devices via DIN-SYNC and also outputs three trigger signals that depend on the programming of the "Cowbell", "Clap", and "Maraca" instruments and the accent.

Features

  • Analog
  • 16 sounds
  • Individual outputs
  • Pattern-based
  • 12 songs with 64 patterns each
  • DIN-SYNC
  • 3 trigger signals
