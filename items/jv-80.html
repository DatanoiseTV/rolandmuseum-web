Roland JV-80

Synthesizer Year: 1992

Description

The JV-80 is a 28-voice, 8-part multitimbral digital synthesizer with 61 velocity-sensitive keys and aftertouch. A sound program (patch) consists of up to 4 tones, two of which can be combined via one of 10 structures. A tone consists of a PCM oscillator, a 12dB multi-mode filter, a dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, and 2 LFOs. Depending on the chosen structure, boosters and a ring modulator can be added, and with the same setting for two filters, a 24dB slope can be achieved. By limiting tones in velocity and note range, split, layer, and crossover sounds are possible within a single patch. The combination of up to 7 parts (with patches) and a rhythm set is controlled by a performance. The sound palette of the JV-80 can be expanded with a Wave Expansion Board, providing additional PCM samples for the oscillators. The JV-80 is the first synthesizer in the JV/XP series, all based on sound generation with 4 tones and 10 structures.

Features

  • 28-voice polyphony
  • 8-part multitimbral
  • Digital synthesizer
  • 61 velocity-sensitive keys with aftertouch
  • Patches consist of up to 4 tones
  • 10 structures for tone combination
  • PCM oscillator
  • 12dB multi-mode filter
  • Dynamic amplifier
  • 3 envelopes
  • 2 LFOs
  • Optional booster and ring modulator
  • Up to 24dB filter slope possible with dual filters
  • Split, layer, and crossover sounds within a single patch
  • Expandable with Wave Expansion Board (SR-JV80 series)
  • First synthesizer in the JV/XP series
