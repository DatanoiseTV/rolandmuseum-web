LM-2 Limiter

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1986

Description

The LM-2 Limiter addresses extreme transients and volume peaks in modern music, such as percussive synthesizer sounds or slapped bass/guitar. It prevents amplifier overload, recording device saturation, or even speaker damage by compressing volume peaks above an adjustable maximum. Utilizing a high-performance VCA, it guarantees an excellent signal-to-noise ratio without compromising sound quality.

Features

  • High-performance VCA for excellent signal-to-noise ratio
  • Prevents overloading by compressing volume peaks
  • Controls: Level, Tone, Threshold, Release
  • Level control determines output volume
  • Tone control influences timbre
  • Threshold control sets the level at which limiting begins
  • Release control determines the decay time after the signal drops below the threshold
  • Power supply: 9V battery, AC adapter (ACA series)
  • Power consumption: 9V, 12 mA
  • Signal-to-noise ratio: -100 dB or less (IHF-A, Level and Tone controls at mid-position)
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70 (W) x 125 (D) x 55 (H) mm
  • Weight: 400 g
