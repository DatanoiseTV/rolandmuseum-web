Roland MKS-7

MIDI Sound Module Year: 1985

Description

The MKS-7 Super Quartet is a multitimbral analog synthesizer module with PCM rhythm sounds. It features four sections: a monophonic bass section with 20 sounds, a two-voice melody section and a four-voice chord section, each with 100 sounds, and a drum section with 11 PCM samples. Sound editing is possible via MIDI SysEx. The MKS-7 can also be switched to a "Whole" mode for 6-voice polyphony on the chord part.

Features

  • Multitimbral (4 parts)
  • Analog synthesis (bass, melody, chord sections)
  • PCM drum sounds (11 samples)
  • 100 sounds for melody and chord sections
  • 20 bass sounds
  • MIDI SysEx editing
  • "Whole" mode for 6-voice polyphony
