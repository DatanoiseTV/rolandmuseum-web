Roland AT-4

Acoustic Trigger Interface Year: 1994

Description

The AT-4 is a four-channel microphone preamplifier that boosts signals from 4 trigger microphones attached to drums to the minimum level required by TD modules. However, the AT-4 does not guarantee that a trigger microphone will work with any TD module (SPD, HPD-15, etc.).

Features

  • Four-channel microphone preamplifier
  • Boosts signals from 4 drum trigger microphones to the minimum level required by TD modules
  • Production period: 1994-1997
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 154 x 44 x 118 mm
  • Weight: 0.46 kg
  • Battery operation: 1 x 9V block (6LR61)
  • Power consumption: 0.04A
  • Power supply: PSA adapter
  • Included accessories: 006P 9V block battery
  • Recommended accessories: PSA power supply, PCS-25 audio cables, TD-7, SPD-11
