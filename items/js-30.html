Roland JS-30

Sampler Year: 1993

Description

The JS-30 Sampling Workstation is a phrase sampler designed for easy real-time operation. It features 12 keypads for triggering samples, a BPM adjustment function, and a SCSI port for connecting external storage devices.

Features

  • Phrase sampler
  • 12 keypads
  • BPM adjustment
  • SCSI port
