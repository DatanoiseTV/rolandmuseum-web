Boss DSD-3

Guitar Effects Year: 1986

Description

The DSD-3 is a budget-friendly digital sampler and delay. It features a 12-bit A/D/A converter with a frequency response of 40 Hz to 7 kHz. The sampling time can be set between 200 and 800 ms. It operates in two modes: Rec/Play (simultaneous record and playback) and Play Only (playback of a stored sample). In Rec/Play mode, overdubbing is possible with the feedback at maximum. The Time knob adjusts the pitch of the sample during playback. The DSD-3 also functions as a digital delay with a delay time of 50 to 800 ms. The delay time is determined by selecting S-mode (50-200ms) or L-mode (200-800ms) and adjusting the Time knob. External trigger signals can also control the delay time.

Features

  • Digital Sampler/Delay
  • 12-bit A/D/A converter
  • 200-800ms Sampling Time
  • 50-800ms Delay Time
  • Rec/Play and Play Only modes
  • External Trigger Input
  • Pitch Shifting
