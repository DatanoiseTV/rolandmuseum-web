Roland MKS-10 PLANET P

Piano Sound Module Year: 1984

Description

The MKS-10 PLANET-P is a 16-voice analog piano sound generation module housed in a 19-inch, 2U rack format. It features exactly 16 immutable presets; 8 of these can be selected directly on the MKS-10 unit, while the remaining 8 are accessible only via MIDI program change messages. The MKS-10 is exclusively polyphonic (not multi-timbral) and is designed to receive MIDI data on a freely selectable MIDI channel. As nothing is storable on the unit, it is equipped only with MIDI IN and MIDI THRU jacks.

Features

  • 16-voice polyphony
  • Analog piano sound generation
  • 19-inch rack format, 2U height
  • 16 immutable presets (8 selectable on unit, 8 via MIDI Program Change)
  • Exclusively polyphonic (not multi-timbral)
  • Receives MIDI data on a freely selectable MIDI channel
  • MIDI IN and MIDI THRU jacks
  • Included accessories: LP-25 Audio Cable, MSC-10 MIDI Cable
  • Dimensions: 482 (W) x 89 (H) x 400 (D) mm
  • Weight: 10 kg
  • Integrated power supply with Roland power cable
  • Pioneered the separation of MIDI data and sound generation alongside modules like MKS-30 and MKS-80
  • Successor: MKS-20
