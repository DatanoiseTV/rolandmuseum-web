Roland AF-60 ´Bee Gee´ Fuzz Box

Guitar/Bass Effect Pedal Year: 1975

Description

The Roland AF-60 is a floor effect pedal for guitar housed in a robust, dark green die-cast casing. It is a fuzz effect that offers a modified distortion sound character compared to the AF-100 ´Bee Baa´. The AF-60 has two controls, TONE and OUT LEVEL, and an effect/bypass switch on the top. TONE controls the tonal character of the fuzz distortion, and OUT LEVEL controls the output level. The input jack (6.3mm mono), labeled INSTRUMENT, is located on the right side, while the mono output jack, labeled OUTPUT, is on the left. To change the battery, the casing must be unscrewed from the bottom; external power supply operation is not provided. The casing shape is the same as that used for the (Roland) AS-1, AP-2, and (Boss) BF-1. The AF-60 was manufactured by the Roland Music Engineering Group, or Roland MEG for short, which was later renamed ´Boss´.

Features

  • Fuzz effect pedal
  • Robust die-cast casing
  • TONE control for fuzz character
  • OUT LEVEL control for output volume
  • Effect/Bypass switch
  • 6.3mm mono input jack
  • 6.3mm mono output jack
  • Battery-powered (1x 9V block)
  • No external power supply option
