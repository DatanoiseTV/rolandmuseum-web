Roland GK-2B Tonabnehmer-System für V-Bass

Hexaphonic Pickup System Year: 2002

Description

The Roland GK-2B is a hexaphonic pickup with a 13-pin connector for mounting on a standard bass guitar, used to control bass synthesizers or virtual bass systems that utilize the 13-pin system. Unlike normal pickups, a hexaphonic pickup individually captures each string, providing 4 to 6 signals depending on the number of strings on the bass. The GK-2B is essentially two-part, consisting of the pickup itself, which should be mounted approximately 10mm behind the bridge, and a small control unit connected via a short cable containing the controls. The control unit, secured to the strap button and bass surface with a special bracket, has a mini-jack input to connect the signal from normal pickups, which is also transmitted via the 13-pin cable. On the control unit, there are two buttons 'Up/S2' and 'Down/S1', a knob for (synth) volume, a switch for selecting BASS, MIX, or pure GK signal, and the 13-pin connector for the GK cable. The GK-2B is not a MIDI pickup, as is often incorrectly stated, but rather the prerequisite for a 13-pin device (e.g., V-Bass, GR-20, GI-20, VB-99, etc.) to convert its signals into MIDI.

Features

  • Hexaphonic pickup
  • 13-pin connector
  • Mounts on standard bass guitars
  • Controls bass synthesizers or virtual bass systems
  • Individually captures each string
  • Two buttons: 'Up/S2' and 'Down/S1'
  • Volume control for synth sound
  • Switch for BASS, MIX, or pure GK signal
  • Mini-jack input for normal pickups
  • Enables 13-pin MIDI devices (not a MIDI pickup itself)
