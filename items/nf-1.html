Boss NF-1

Compact Effect Pedal Year: 1980

Description

The Noise Gate is an electronic device that suppresses unwanted background noise by muting the signal. Compared to other noise gates, the BOSS NF-1 stands out due to its high efficiency without altering or coloring the sound of the connected instrument. The controls on the NF-1 Noise Gate are: Sensitivity and Decay (response time, muting after the last sound signal). The sensitivity setting determines the activation threshold. The second control adjusts the time that should elapse after the last note until muting occurs - the Decay Time. This allows for accommodating any conceivable volume envelope of an instrument and bringing it fully to bear, without being restricted by the noise gate. We don't feel it - but when we're not playing, there's no noise either.

Features

  • Suppresses unwanted background noise
  • High efficiency without altering or coloring the sound
  • Controls: Sensitivity and Decay
  • Sensitivity controls activation threshold
  • Decay controls muting time after the last signal
  • Power: 9V battery or AC adapter
  • Current draw: DC9V 3.9mA
  • Max. output: +9dBm
  • Unity gain
  • Input impedance: 220K Ohm
  • Output load impedance: Over 10K Ohm
  • Attack time: 1ms
  • Release time: 100ms - 1.6 sec
  • Variable gating threshold: -65 to -35dBm
