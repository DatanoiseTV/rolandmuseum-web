Roland Roland MV-30 Studio M

Music Workstation Year: 1990

Description

The MV-30 is a combination of a 30-voice, 8-part multitimbral sound generator and a 16-track sequencer in a console-style chassis. The sound generation is based on that of the U-20, extended with the filters of the D-70. A sound program is called a 'Timbre' and is based on a 'Tone', which is shaped by a dynamic, resonant filter and dynamic amplitude, and modulated by two envelopes and an LFO. A Tone consists of one or two oscillators that play 1 or 2 fixed PCM waveforms, either together, split, or crossfaded via velocity. The MV-30 has 2 slots for PCM cards to expand the internal library of 220 preset tones, and cards for the U-series and D-70 can also be used. There are 7 normal synthesizer parts to which 128 savable Timbres can be assigned, and one rhythm part (for rhythm Timbre 129). The 128 Timbres and the rhythm Timbre can be saved together as a set under an 8-character name on a floppy disk. 8 sets can be saved per diskette. The sequencer is primarily song-oriented but also offers 20 patterns per song. There are a total of 18 tracks, with 1 track for (MIDI-) mixer data and one tempo track not intended for notes. The remaining tracks are divided into 8 single-channel tracks, which are firmly assigned to the internal parts of the sound generator and cannot output data externally. The last 8 tracks can record mixed data from all 16 MIDI channels and either output it via MIDI or route it to the internal sound generator. Each of the 16 note tracks can optionally also be declared as a pattern track and then calls up a pattern sequence. The up to 20 patterns of a song can be assigned as RPS phrases to individual keys (note numbers) and then manually started at any time with the RPS function. Realtime and step input are available as recording methods, and individual events can be edited in detail. Internally, approximately 50,000 events can be recorded; on a diskette, approximately 70,000-100,000 events can be saved. The maximum number of songs is 20 (internal) or 64 songs (diskette). In addition to its own format, the MV-30 can load songs from W-30 diskettes, MC-sequencers (MC-300, MC-500, MC-500MKII, and MC-50), S-samplers (SYS-503, SYS-333, SYS-553), and DOS-formatted SMF files. Songs can be saved as MV-30, W-30, S-MRC, and SMF songs. The sequencer can operate as a master or as a slave to MIDI Clock and understands Song Position Pointer. The MV-30 can generate an FSK signal for tape synchronization and also run as a slave to tape with FSK.

Features

  • 30-voice sound generator
  • 8-part multitimbral
  • 16-track sequencer
  • Console-style chassis
  • Sound generation based on U-20 with D-70 filters
  • 'Timbre' sound programs based on 'Tone'
  • Dynamic, resonant filter
  • Dynamic amplitude
  • Two envelopes and one LFO for modulation
  • Tones composed of one or two oscillators
  • Play 1 or 2 fixed PCM waveforms (together, split, or velocity crossfaded)
  • 2 slots for PCM cards (compatible with U-series and D-70 cards)
  • Internal library of 220 preset tones
  • 7 normal synthesizer parts (128 savable Timbres)
  • 1 rhythm part (for rhythm Timbre 129)
  • 8 sets can be saved per floppy disk (8-character name)
  • Song-oriented sequencer with 20 patterns per song
  • 18 tracks total (1 mixer data, 1 tempo, 8 internal synth parts, 8 MIDI tracks)
  • 8 internal single-channel tracks (no external MIDI output)
  • 8 MIDI tracks (record mixed data from 16 MIDI channels, output via MIDI or to internal synth)
  • Note tracks can be declared as pattern tracks
  • RPS (Realtime Phrase Sequence) function (assign patterns to keys for manual triggering)
  • Recording methods: Realtime and Step input
  • Detailed event editing
  • Internal storage: approx. 50,000 events
  • Floppy disk storage: approx. 70,000-100,000 events
  • Max songs: 20 (internal), 64 (diskette)
  • Loads songs from W-30, MC-sequencers (MC-300, MC-500, MC-500MKII, MC-50), S-samplers (SYS-503, SYS-333, SYS-553), and DOS-formatted SMF
  • Saves songs as MV-30, W-30, S-MRC, and SMF
  • Sequencer functions as MIDI master or slave (MIDI Clock, Song Position Pointer)
  • FSK signal generation for tape synchronization
  • FSK slave for tape synchronization
  • Dimensions: 390 x 77 x 310 mm
  • Weight: 3.46 kg
  • Power consumption: 1.2A, 9VAC
