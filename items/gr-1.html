Roland GR-1

Guitar Synthesizer Year: 1992

Description

The GR-1 Guitar Synthesizer is a self-contained unit that connects and operates as simply as an effect pedal. By connecting your favorite guitar with the GK-2 Guitar Driver to the GR-1, a whole new world of sound synthesis will open up to you. All functions of the GR-1 have been specifically tailored to the needs of guitarists, from editing possibilities and effects to the built-in 4-track sequencer. The GR-1 is easy to use and offers guitarists all possibilities of sound synthesis and MIDI. It features Roland's latest digital technology and an incredible array of sounds. With the GR-1's multitimbral capabilities and built-in sequencer, you have access to a whole range of sounds directly from your guitar. You can enter the world of synthesizers without losing your connection to the guitar. With the GR-1, you can create sounds previously exclusive to keyboards without changing your playing habits. The sensitivity of the divided GK-2 pickup can be individually adjusted for each string on the GR-1's control surface. Optionally, a Wave Expansion Board (SR-GR1-01) with 200 additional sounds can be installed in the GR-1 to increase its capacity to 400 sounds. Editing is easily done in the familiar way with the 7 rotary knobs of the Macro Edit Section. Two of the 200 sounds can be selected, and their application can be pre-programmed in 4 different ways: the first sound alone, the second sound alone, both together, and muting for each individual string. For each sound, envelope (Attack, Decay, Release), filter (Cut off/Resonance), and vibrato (Rate/Depth) parameters can be edited separately. For the two selected sounds, reverb and chorus can be switched on or off, and volume balance, detune, stereo panning, and vibrato can be individually controlled. Note shift is adjustable for each string. The GR-1 can store 64 individually designed patches with all parameter settings in its internal memory. An additional 64 patches can be stored on the separately available M-256 E Memory Card. Simply attach the Roland GK-2 Synthesizer Driver (sold separately) to your guitar, connect it to the GR-1 Guitar Synthesizer, and start playing. The GK-2 fits easily on most electric and acoustic steel-string guitars. The compact and lightweight GR-1 connects to an amplifier like an effect unit and is just as easy to operate. It has built-in footswitches for changing parameters and switching sounds. Its MIDI In/Out jacks can connect to other sound sources, effects units, or other MIDI devices.

Features

  • Guitar Synthesizer
  • Designed for use with Roland GK-2 Guitar Driver (sold separately)
  • 4-track sequencer
  • Multitimbral capabilities
  • Digital sound synthesis
  • Individual string sensitivity adjustment for GK-2 pickup
  • Expandable with SR-GR1-01 Wave Expansion Board (adds 200 sounds, total 400)
  • Macro Edit Section with 7 rotary knobs
  • Programmable sound application (single sounds, layered, individual string mute)
  • Editable parameters: Envelope (Attack, Decay, Release), Filter (Cut off/Resonance), Vibrato (Rate/Depth)
  • Built-in Reverb and Chorus effects
  • Volume Balance, Detune, Stereo Panning, Vibrato control
  • Individual string Note Shift
  • 64 internal patch memory locations
  • Expandable with M-256 E Memory Card (adds 64 patches)
  • Built-in footswitches for parameter and sound changes
  • MIDI In/Out ports
