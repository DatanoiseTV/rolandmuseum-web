Roland MC-307 GrooveBox

Groovebox Year: 2000

Description

The MC-307 GrooveBox consists of an 8-part multitimbral digital synthesizer and a pattern sequencer. The sound generation principle is based on the 4 Tone-10 Structure architecture of the JV/XP series, with ROM samples exchanged for dance-ready sounds. Parameters are fully compatible with the MC-505, but the MC-307 has an additional bank of PCM samples and newer, fresher preset patterns and patches as a basis for creating your own grooves. However, the MC-307 does not have a Megamix mode and only 4 rotary encoders instead of the 8 sliders on the MC-505 for controlling parts. Tempo changes like record scratching, or just pitch and tempo changes, can be quickly controlled with the Turntable Emulation knob.

Features

  • 8-part multitimbral digital synthesizer
  • Pattern sequencer
  • Sound generation based on 4 Tone-10 Structure architecture (JV/XP series)
  • Dance-oriented ROM samples
  • Parameters fully compatible with MC-505
  • Additional bank of PCM samples
  • Newer, fresher preset patterns and patches
  • 4 rotary encoders for part control
  • Turntable Emulation knob for quick tempo/pitch changes and scratching
  • Dimensions: 422 x 98 x 277 mm
  • Weight: 2.2 kg
  • Power supply: 1A AC adapter
