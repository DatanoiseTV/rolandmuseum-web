GR-100 Guitar Synthesizer

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1981

Description

The Roland GR-100 is an electronic effects unit for guitars with a 24-pin connection. Unlike the Roland GR-300, which is a true guitar synthesizer, the GR-100 is essentially a hexaphonic fuzz box with a subsequent filter and a chorus. Roland wisely called the GR-100 "Electronic Guitar" instead of "Guitar Synthesizer". Three built-in footswitches are used to turn the chorus or filter modulation on/off and to invert the filter envelope. Filter modulation can be adjusted with controls for Peak and Sensitivity (Sens), one control determines Chorus intensity (Sens), and another controls the Vibrato rate (intensity is controlled by a knob on the G-Guitar). There are no more controls, but there is a 24-pin connector on the top and, on the rear, connections for a VCF control pedal and three audio outputs: Chorus, Mix, and Guitar, as well as the power switch.

Features

  • Electronic effects unit for guitars
  • 24-pin connection
  • Hexaphonic Fuzz Box
  • Post-fuzz filter
  • Chorus effect
  • Three built-in footswitches for Chorus On/Off, Filter Modulation On/Off, Filter Envelope Inversion
  • Controls for Peak and Sensitivity (Filter Modulation)
  • Control for Chorus Intensity (Sens)
  • Control for Vibrato Rate
  • Connections for VCF control pedal
  • Three audio outputs: Chorus, Mix, Guitar
  • Built-in power supply
  • Included accessories: C-24D 24-pin cable, PJ-1 audio cable
