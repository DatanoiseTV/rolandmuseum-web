Boss AD-3 Acoustic Processor

Compact FX Year: 1999

Description

Specifically developed for live performances, the AD-3 is ideal for giving electro-acoustic guitars a lively, full sound. Thanks to special tone shaping controls and effective feedback suppression, problems with thin sound or feedback are a thing of the past. High-quality reverb and chorus effects are also built-in.

Features

  • Compact pedal, especially for stage use
  • Switchable bi-stereo chorus with separate processing for low and high frequencies for a particularly natural and full chorus effect
  • Clear, dense studio-quality reverb, optimized for acoustic guitar
  • Specially developed 'Bottom' and 'Top' controls to transform cold, dry piezo sound into a warm, natural sound with rich lows and clear highs
  • Special guitar amplifier output (acoustic-electric guitar amplifier simulation) with optimal impedance and sound preset for guitar amplifiers
  • Balanced stereo outputs for direct connection to PA systems
  • Effective dual anti-feedback function, adjustable manually or switched to 'Auto' mode with a footswitch
  • Power supply via AC adapter or battery
