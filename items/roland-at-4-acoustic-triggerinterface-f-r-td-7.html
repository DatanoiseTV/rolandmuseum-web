Roland Roland AT-4 Acoustic Triggerinterface für TD-7

Drum Machines Year: 1994

Description

The AT-4 is a quad microphone preamplifier that boosts signals from 4 trigger microphones attached to drums to the minimum level required by TD modules. The AT-4 does not guarantee that a trigger microphone will work with any TD module (SPD, HPD-15, etc.).

Features

  • 4-channel microphone preamplifier
  • Boosts trigger microphone signals
  • For use with Roland TD-7 modules
