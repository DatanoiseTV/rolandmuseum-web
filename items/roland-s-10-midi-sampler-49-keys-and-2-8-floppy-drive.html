Roland S-10 MIDI SAMPLER, 49 keys and 2.8" Floppy Drive

Sampler Year: 1986

Description

The Roland S-10 'Digital Sampling Keyboard' is an 8-voice sampler with 49 velocity-sensitive keys, an arpeggiator, and a 2.8-inch floppy drive (Quick Disk format, or QD). The S-10 samples at 12-bit resolution and either 15 or 30kHz. The internal memory of 256kB is divided into 4 banks of 64kB each, so all samples combined can be a maximum of 4.4 seconds long, approximately 1.1 seconds per bank. To record a 'long' sample, two banks can be combined. Samples can be edited on the device, assigned a dynamic envelope, and looped, but there is no dynamic filter. A maximum of 4 samples can be arranged side-by-side (split) or layered across the keyboard. The S-10 has only one (MIDI) part. Monitor and mouse operation, like on its 'big brother' S-50, is not provided; the S-10's controls and single-line display must suffice here.

Features

  • Digital Sampling Keyboard
  • 8-voice polyphony
  • 49 velocity-sensitive keys
  • Arpeggiator
  • 2.8-inch Quick Disk (QD) floppy drive
  • 12-bit sampling resolution
  • Selectable sampling rates: 15 kHz or 30 kHz
  • Internal memory: 256KB (4 banks of 64KB)
  • Maximum total sample time: 4.4 seconds
  • Maximum sample time per bank: 1.1 seconds
  • Ability to combine two banks for longer samples
  • Onboard sample editing, dynamic envelope assignment, looping
  • No dynamic filter
  • Up to 4 samples can be split or layered on the keyboard
  • Single MIDI part
  • Single-line display
  • Power consumption: 19W
  • Power supply: Built-in, Roland power cable
  • Dimensions: 945(W) x 77(H) x 271(D) mm
  • Weight: 9.5 kg
