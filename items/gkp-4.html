GKP-4

Utility Year: 2002

Description

The Roland GKP-4, labeled 'GK Parallel Box', is a quadruple distributor for the GK signal of a guitar to four 13-pin devices, such as GR synthesizers (GR-09, GR-1, GR-20, GR-30, GR-33, GR-50), Guitar-to-MIDI interfaces (GI-10, GI-20), Virtual Guitar Systems (VG-8, VG-88, VG-99) and other GK devices (OC-20G, WP-20G). On the front side of the elongated bar is the 13-pin input, the four outputs are arranged next to each other on one side, and two sets of four associated switches are on the opposite side. With these switches, you can individually determine for each output A to D whether the control signals 'S1/Down' and 'S2/Up' from the GK pickup are output (ON) or not (OFF). The other switch determines whether the synthesizer volume setting from the GK-2 should be passed to the respective output (ON) or not (OFF). The GKP-4 is powered via the GK device connected to output A.

Features

  • Quadruple distributor for GK signals
  • 13-pin input
  • Four 13-pin outputs
  • Individual switches for S1/S2 control signals per output
  • Individual switches for GK-2 synthesizer volume setting per output
  • Powered via connected GK device (output A)
  • Compatible with GR synthesizers, Guitar-to-MIDI interfaces, Virtual Guitar Systems
