Boss OS-2 OverDrive/Distortion

Effect Pedal Year: 1990

Description

The OS-2 is an effect pedal that combines two of the most popular effects, Overdrive and Distortion, providing guitarists with an exceptionally wide range of distortion possibilities. Thanks to BOSS's 'asymmetrical overdrive circuit', the OS-2 accurately reproduces the characteristic sound of a tube amplifier. In the distortion section, a specially developed circuit ensures the powerful sustain that defines the sound of today's guitarists. A tone control allows for shaping a variety of sounds, be it the soft, full overdrive sound of a tube amp, extremely harsh distortion effects, or a sound within the extensive tonal spectrum between these two extremes. Fine tonal adjustments and the balance of the output signal can be made with Drive and Level controls.

Features

  • Combination of Overdrive and Distortion effects
  • BOSS-developed 'asymmetrical overdrive circuit' for tube amp sound
  • Specially developed circuit for powerful sustain in distortion mode
  • Tone control for versatile sound shaping
  • Drive and Level controls for fine-tuning
  • Power supply: 9V battery or AC adapter (PSA series)
  • Current draw: 9V, 12 mA
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 400 g
