Roland DJ-70MKII

DJ Sampling Workstation Year: 1997

Description

The Roland DJ-70MKII is a stereo sampler workstation with a 3-octave velocity-sensitive keyboard. It's 24-voice polyphonic, 31-part multitimbral, and features an 8-track sequencer with RPS. The sampler uses the same data format as the S-700 series, layering up to 4 samples into partials and up to 88 partials into a patch. Dynamic amps and filters with resonance are available per partial. The DJ-70MKII can load samples while playing and handles sound disks from the S-330 and S-550. It has 4MB of sample memory, expandable to 32MB, and uses floppy disks and SCSI hard drives for storage. A unique feature is the Scratch Dial for scratching samples like vinyl records.

Features

  • 24-voice polyphony
  • 31-part multitimbral
  • 8-track sequencer
  • Scratch Dial
  • 4MB sample memory (expandable to 32MB)
  • Floppy disk and SCSI storage
