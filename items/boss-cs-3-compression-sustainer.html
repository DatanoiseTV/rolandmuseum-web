Boss CS-3 Compression Sustainer

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1986

Description

The CS-3 Compressor/Sustainer can apply compression to any electronic or electric instrument without unwanted distortion. Equipped with a specially developed compression circuit, the CS-3 offers excellent sound quality and versatility for all applications from live performance to studio recording. Four controls are available for a variety of effects. The Sustain control determines the sustain time. The further it is turned to the right, the longer the sustain time. Turning it to the left, on the other hand, increases the compression. This allows the CS-3 to be used as a limiter. The Tone control allows fine-tuning of the timbre of the effect sounds, thus enabling a greater variety of sounds and improving sound projection. The Attack time can be adjusted over a wide range with the Attack control, so that every sound, even notes in fast passages, can be clearly compressed. Finally, the Level control regulates the volume balance between the effect and the normal signals.
