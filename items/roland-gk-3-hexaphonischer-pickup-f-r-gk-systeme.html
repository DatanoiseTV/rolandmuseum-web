Roland GK-3 Hexaphonischer Pickup für GK-Systeme

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 2004

Description

The Roland GK-3 is a hexaphonic pickup with a 13-pin connector for mounting on standard guitars to control guitar synthesizers of the 13-pin system. It picks up each string individually, providing six separate signals. The GK-3 consists of the pickup itself, mounted about 10mm behind the bridge, and a small control unit connected by a short cable. This control unit, attached to the strap button and guitar surface with a bracket, has a mini-jack input for the regular guitar pickups, allowing their signal to be transmitted through the 13-pin cable alongside the hexaphonic signals. The control unit has two buttons ('Up/S2' and 'Down/S1'), a volume knob for the synth sound, a switch to toggle between Guitar, Mix, and pure GK signal, and the 13-pin connector for the GK cable.

Features

  • Hexaphonic pickup
  • 13-pin connector
  • Separate string signals
  • Control unit with volume, mix switch, and two buttons
  • Mini-jack input for regular guitar pickups
