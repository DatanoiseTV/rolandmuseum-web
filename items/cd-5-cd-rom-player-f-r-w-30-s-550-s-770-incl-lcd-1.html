Roland CD-5 CD-ROM Player für W-30, S-550, S-770 incl LCD-1

CD-ROM Drive for Samplers Year: 1989

Description

A CD-ROM drive with a SCSI connection designed for Roland samplers. This drive is compatible with the Apple standard of the early 90s and was delivered with the LCD-1 CD-ROM, which contains sounds in the S-550 format. At the time, CD-ROM drives were extremely rare.

Features

  • CD-ROM drive
  • SCSI connection
  • Compatible with Roland W-30, S-550, S-770 samplers
  • Compatible with early 90s Apple standard
  • Includes LCD-1 CD-ROM with S-550 format sounds
  • Built-in power supply
  • Dimensions (355 x 61 x 337 mm)
  • Weight (5.6 kg)
