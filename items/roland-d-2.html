Roland D-2

Groovebox Year: 2001

Description

The Roland D-2 is a groovebox featuring an 8-part multitimbral digital synthesizer and a pattern sequencer. It uses the 4-Tone-10Structure architecture of the JV/XP series but with dance-oriented sounds. The sounds are largely compatible with the MC-505, although not all parameters can be directly edited on the D-2. It offers a 2-D touch-sensitive D-Field controller for filter cutoff and resonance, tempo changes, and note input.

Features

  • 8-part multitimbral digital synthesizer
  • Pattern sequencer
  • 4-Tone-10Structure architecture
  • Dance-oriented sounds
  • 2D touch-sensitive D-Field controller
  • MC-505 compatibility
