Boss PSM-5

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1990

Description

The PSM-5 is a stabilized power supply for up to five 9V Boss (or similar) effects pedals, and functions as a master switch. It offers two loops; one through the effects and another direct. The footswitch selects between these loops.

Features

  • Stabilized power supply for up to five 9V pedals
  • Master switch functionality
  • Two loops (effects on/off)
  • Footswitch
