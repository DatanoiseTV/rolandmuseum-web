FT-2 Dynamic Filter

Compact FX Year: 1990

Description

The FT-2 is equipped with a low-pass filter with adjustable slope (resonance). Unlike other wah effects, the FT-2 only influences the overtones of a sound, leaving the fundamental tone unchanged, ensuring excellent sound projection for any instrument, from guitar to keyboard to bass guitar. The FT-2 offers three operating modes: Up, Down, and Manual. In Up and Down modes, automatic upward or downward wah effects can be generated. By integrating BOSS's high-speed envelope circuit, the FT-2 can produce automatic wah effects faster and with higher accuracy than any conventional wah effect unit. The Manual mode allows the FT-2 to be used as a low-pass filter with adjustable cutoff frequency and resonance, or as a wah pedal by connecting the optional EV-5 expression pedal to the EXP input jack. When used as a wah pedal, the FT-2 never produces the scratching and popping noises associated with ordinary wah pedals. A Cutoff-Frequency control allows precise adjustment of the filter point, so the FT-2 can be adapted to any instrument. A "Q" control varies the wah effect over a wide range from subtle to extremely intense.

Features

  • Low-pass filter with adjustable slope (resonance)
  • Affects only overtones, fundamental tone remains unchanged
  • Three operating modes: Up, Down, Manual
  • Automatic wah effects (Up/Down)
  • BOSS High-Speed Envelope Circuit
  • Manual low-pass filter function (adjustable cutoff frequency and resonance)
  • Wah pedal function with optional EV-5 expression pedal
  • No scratching or popping noises as a wah pedal
  • Cutoff-Frequency control
  • "Q" control for wah intensity
  • Power supply: 9V battery, AC adapter (ACA series)
  • Power consumption: 16 mA
  • S/N ratio: -85 dBm
  • Dimensions: 70 x 125 x 55 mm
  • Weight: 400 g
