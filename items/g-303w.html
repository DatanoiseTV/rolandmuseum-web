Roland G-303W

Guitar Synthesizer Controller Year: 1982

Description

The Roland G-303W is a controller guitar designed for use with Roland guitar synthesizers featuring a 24-pin connector. Its distinct double-cutaway shape and white finish ('w' designation) set it apart. Beyond the 24-pin connector for the hexaphonic pickup, which sends individual string signals to control GR-series synthesizers, the G-303W includes a standard guitar output for its two humbucker pickups. This allows it to function as a regular guitar as well. Constructed with nickel-plated metal parts, a mahogany body, and a maple neck, the G-303W differs from the G-808 primarily in its set neck construction and wood choice. Manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (specifically Fuji Gen Gakki) to Roland's specifications, the G-303W offers controls for master volume, guitar tone, cutoff frequency, guitar/synth balance, resonance (VCF), vibrato depth, and three-way mode and signal switching. Famously played by Pat Metheny, Andy Summers (The Police), Adrian Belew, and Robert Fripp (King Crimson), the G-303W was also available in a left-handed version (G-303LH).

Features

  • 24-pin hexaphonic pickup
  • Dual humbuckers
  • Mahogany body
  • Maple neck
  • Synthesizer control
  • Standard guitar output
  • Nickel-plated hardware
