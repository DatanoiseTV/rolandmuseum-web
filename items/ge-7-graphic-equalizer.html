GE-7 Graphic Equalizer

Compact FX Year: 1982

Description

The GE-7 Graphic Equalizer is a small, compact equalizer designed for initial sound control on an instrument. It offers extensive tone control for any musical signal, from creative sound shaping to compensating for room acoustics or system deficiencies. It can also be used as an effect for boosting specific frequency ranges.

Features

  • 7 octave bands with a control range of ±15dB per band and a center detent at 0dB
  • Built-in 'Level' control to adjust overall volume to match bypass level
  • Silent electronic switch for noise-free on/off operation
  • S/N ratio of more than 90dB
  • Power: 9V Battery, AC Adapter
  • Current draw: DC9V, 7mA
  • Frequency Centers: 100Hz, 200Hz, 400Hz, 800Hz, 1.6kHz, 3.2kHz, 6.4kHz
  • Control range: ±15dB
  • Residual noise: -100dBm (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 220 kOhm
  • Output impedance: Over 600 Ohm
  • Dimensions: 2.8(W) x 2.2(H) x 4.9(D) inches
  • Weight: 0.99 lbs
  • Extended frequency range suitable for fundamental and overtones of electric bass and guitar, keyboards, and vocals
  • Standard features of BOSS compact pedals including silent FET switching, LED on/off and battery check indicator, non-slip feet, and easy access to the battery compartment
