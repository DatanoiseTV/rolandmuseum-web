Roland GS-500

Guitar FX Year: 1977

Description

The GS-500 is a guitar controller for the GR-500 guitar synthesizer. It lacks a standard guitar output, instead featuring a 24-pin connector for the control cable to the synthesizer. The guitar's signal from the humbucker pickup is only accessible through the GR-500. A hexaphonic pickup provides individual string signals for the synthesizer. This Les Paul-style guitar has a mahogany body and maple top, manufactured by Ibanez/Greco to Roland's specifications. It includes a master volume, tone and volume controls, a 3-band EQ, four volume controls for synth sections, and switches for Polyensemble, Bass, Solo-Melody, External-Synthesizer, Remote, and Portamento.

Features

  • 24-pin connector for GR-500
  • Humbucker pickup
  • Hexaphonic pickup
  • Mahogany body
  • Maple top
  • 3-band EQ
  • Master Volume
  • Individual Synth Section Controls
