CSQ-600 Digital Sequencer, 600 Steps for CV/Gate

Sequencer Year: 1980

Description

The CSQ-600 is a digitally controlled sequencer for analog CV/Gate signals. It offers 4 sequences, each with a maximum of 150 steps (notes), which are retained even after power-off thanks to the built-in battery. The four sequences can be combined in any order. Separate recording of CV and control time in two passes is possible, as is external (slave) synchronization of the CSQ-600. Recorded sequences are retained by a battery for a period after being switched off; there is no option to save the sequences permanently.

Features

  • Digitally controlled sequencer
  • For analog CV/Gate signals
  • 4 sequences, each with a maximum of 150 steps
  • Sequences retained by built-in battery (after power-off)
  • Sequences can be combined in any order
  • Separate recording of CV and control time in 2 passes
  • External (slave) synchronization
  • No permanent sequence storage option
  • Dimensions: 450 x 95 x 305 mm
  • Weight: 3.9 kg
  • Built-in power supply, fixed power cable
  • Production years: 1980-1982
