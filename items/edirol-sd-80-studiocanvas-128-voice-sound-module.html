Edirol SD-80 StudioCanvas, 128-Voice Sound Module

Digital Synthesizer Year: 2004

Description

The SD-80 is a 128-voice MIDI synthesizer module that provides global effects such as Reverb and Chorus, as well as up to three insert effects (90 algorithms) simultaneously. Two pairs of Line Out and S/P DIF Out are available as outputs. The SD-80 has 32 parts, and when connected via USB to a PC or Macintosh, the additional MIDI interfaces provide access to another 32 MIDI channels. The sound library includes 30 drum kits and 1050 GM2/GS/XGlite compatible sounds (6 sound maps: Classical, Contemporary, Solo, Enhanced, Special1, Special2). The SD-80 comes with a powerful software editor and USB drivers for Windows Me/98/2000/XP Home/XP Professional, as well as MacOS 8.5, 8.6, and 9.x.
