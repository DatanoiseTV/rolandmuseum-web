Roland JP-8080 Analog modeling Synthesizer Modul

Virtual Analog Synthesizer Module Year: 1998

Description

The JP-8080 is a 10-voice, virtual analog synthesizer module with a voice modulator, arpeggiator, RPS sequencer, and many real-time controls (16 sliders, 24 knobs). It is maximally 2-part multitimbral. The digital emulation of analog sound generators includes per voice: 2 VCOs (each with 7 waveforms), 1 multi-function filter (HPF, BPF, LPF), 1 VCA, 3 envelopes, and 2 LFOs. Chorus and Delay effects are available. External signals can be fed in and processed with the voice modulator, which includes a vocoder, among other things. The user memory holds 128 patches and 64 performances, with three times as many presets available.
