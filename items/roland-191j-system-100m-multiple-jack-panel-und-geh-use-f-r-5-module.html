Roland 191J System 100M, Multiple Jack Panel und Gehäuse für 5 Module

Eurorack / Modular Year: 1979

Description

The Multiple Jack Panel 191J is essentially just a patchbay (signal distributor) that could be inserted into the lower panel of the 191 housing (for five System-100M modules). This patchbay offers nine sets, each with four parallel-connected jacks for signal distribution. In Germany, this patchbay, designated 191J, was sold from November 1979 only installed in and together with the 191 housing.

Features

  • Patchbay (signal distributor)
  • Designed for Roland System-100M modular synthesizers
  • Fits into 191 housing (for 5 modules)
  • 9 sets of 4 parallel-connected jacks for signal distribution
