TR-66 Rhythm Arranger

Drum Machine / Rhythm Year: 1975

Description

The TR-66 rhythm device is externally named 'Rhythm Arranger 66' and is an analog rhythm machine with 9 fixed rhythms selectable via push buttons and 18 additional rhythms selectable via a rotary switch, with each rhythm available in Variation A and B. Rhythms from the push-button group and the rotary switch group can be combined, and you can switch between Variation A, B, and an alternating AB sequence (called Auto), hence the nickname 'Arranger'. The Rhythm-Arranger 66 belongs to the second series of Roland rhythm devices, alongside the TR-330 and TR-700, and is built into a rectangular wooden case with controls on the front. On the back, there is a mono output for high and low levels and a connection for a start button, but no others. Tempo is adjusted via a rotary knob; synchronization is not possible. Selectable rhythms include Waltz, Jazz Waltz, Slow Rock, Bossa Nova, Samba, Mambo, Cha Cha, Beguine, Rhumba, Rock 1-6, Bass&Snare, Foxtrot 1-2, Swing 1-3, March, Parade, Habanera, Shuffle, and Tango.

Features

  • Analog rhythm device
  • 9 fixed rhythms via push buttons
  • 18 additional rhythms via rotary switch
  • Rhythms available in A and B variations
  • Rhythm groups can be combined
  • Switch between A, B, or Auto (alternating AB) sequences
  • Rectangular wooden case
  • Mono output (high/low level)
  • Start button connection
  • Tempo adjusted by rotary knob
  • No synchronization possible
