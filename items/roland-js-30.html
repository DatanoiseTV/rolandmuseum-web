Roland JS-30

Sampler

Description

A new type of sampler for today's music. Phrase sampling is an indispensable part of dance and pop music production today. Now Roland presents the JS-30 Sampling Workstation: A high-quality phrase sampling instrument for every musician and DJ at a very interesting price. With its sophisticated design and really simple operation, even complex phrase sampling becomes child's play. You don't even have to be able to play an instrument. These features make the JS-30 the ideal instrument for club DJs, professional studios and home studios. It opens up completely new, virtually limitless possibilities for music creation.

Features

  • phrase sampling
  • 16-bit sampling
  • 44.1kHz
  • 12 keypads
  • sample editing
  • time x2 function
  • SCSI port
  • CD-ROM reading
