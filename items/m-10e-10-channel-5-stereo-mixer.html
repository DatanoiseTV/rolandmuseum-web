Edirol M-10E 10 Channel (5 Stereo) Mixer

Portable Mixer Year: 2003

Description

Portable 10-channel mixer for 5 stereo channels, which can be battery operated. Four mono signals from microphone level to line level can be connected, as well as other instruments with line level.

Features

  • Portable 10-channel mixer
  • 5 stereo channels
  • Battery-operable
  • Four mono signals with microphone to line level input
  • Line-level instrument input
  • Dimensions: 218 x 60 x 170 mm
  • Weight: 0.75 kg
  • Battery operation: 1 x 9V block (6LR61)
  • Power consumption: 45mA
  • PSA power adapter compatible
  • PSA power supply included
