Roland US-20

Unit Selector for GK-System Year: 1995

Description

The Roland US-20 is a switch and splitter that allows a GK-guitar (guitar with GK-2, GK-2A(H), or GK-3) to control two 13-pin devices, such as GR synthesizers like GR-09, GR-1, GR-20, GR-33, Guitar-to-MIDI converters like GI-10, GI-20, or Virtual Guitar Systems like VG-8, VG-88, VG-99. On the rear, there is one 13-pin input and two such outputs, and on the top, two switches are located. The left switch selects between device A or B, and the right switch adds the other device if both are to be played simultaneously. LEDs indicate the switching status. Power is supplied via the 13-pin cable from one of the connected GK devices. The US-20 can also be used for a bass with a GK-2B or GK-3B pickup and two GK-bass devices (e.g., GI-20, V-Bass, or VB-99).

Features

  • Switch and splitter for GK-guitars (GK-2, GK-2A(H), GK-3 pickups)
  • Controls two 13-pin devices simultaneously or individually
  • Compatible with GR synthesizers (GR-09, GR-1, GR-20, GR-33)
  • Compatible with Guitar-to-MIDI converters (GI-10, GI-20)
  • Compatible with Virtual Guitar Systems (VG-8, VG-88, VG-99)
  • One 13-pin input
  • Two 13-pin outputs
  • Two switches for device selection (A/B, A+B)
  • LED indicators for switching status
  • Power supplied via 13-pin cable from connected GK device
  • Compatible with bass guitars with GK-2B / GK-3B pickups and GK-bass devices (GI-20, V-Bass, VB-99)
