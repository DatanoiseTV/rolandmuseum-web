Boss DB-5 Driver

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1978

Description

An effects unit with a 5-band equalizer and a selector switch for distortion and attack. Ideal for electric guitar, electric bass, and as an addition to synthesizers.

Features

  • 5-band equalizer
  • Selector switch for distortion and attack
  • Slider controls for +/- 12 dB adjustment at 100 Hz, 300Hz, 600 Hz, 1.2 kHz, and 3 kHz
  • Battery or ACA-220 power supply operation
