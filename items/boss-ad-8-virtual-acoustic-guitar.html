Boss AD-8 Virtual Acoustic Guitar

Guitar FX Year: 2004

Description

The Boss AD-8 converts the harsh, metallic sound of piezo pickups into a warm, round sound similar to that of microphone recording. It also models six famous acoustic guitars without the need for special pickups. A blend control allows mixing the original piezo sound with the simulated sound. Additional features include a preamp, 4-band parametric equalizer, three studio-quality reverb types, a stereo DI box with ground lift, four memory slots, a chromatic tuner, and automatic feedback suppression.

Features

  • Piezo to mic sound conversion
  • Modeling of six famous acoustic guitars
  • Blend control for original and modeled sound
  • Preamp
  • 4-band parametric EQ
  • 3 types of studio-quality reverb
  • Stereo DI box with ground lift
  • 4 memory slots
  • Chromatic tuner
  • Automatic feedback suppression
