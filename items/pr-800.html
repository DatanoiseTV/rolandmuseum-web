Roland PR-800

MIDI Digital Recorder / Sequencer Year: 1984

Description

The PR-800 is a simple 1-track MIDI sequencer designed as a MIDI recorder for MIDI pianos. Recording is exclusively in real-time. If a time signature of 3/4 or 4/4 is set beforehand, the recording can be corrected measure by measure; otherwise, there are no further editing options. For playback, a repetition of either the entire sequence or a specific range can be set. Tempo is adjusted via a rotary knob. The PR-800 can act as a MIDI Clock master or slave, and recorded data can be saved to tape/cassette via an FSK signal. No information on storage capacity is available.

Features

  • Simple 1-track MIDI sequencer
  • MIDI recorder for MIDI pianos
  • Real-time recording only
  • Measure-by-measure correction with 3/4 or 4/4 time signatures
  • Looping of entire sequence or a section
  • Tempo adjustable via rotary knob
  • MIDI Clock master or slave
  • Data savable to tape/cassette via FSK signal
