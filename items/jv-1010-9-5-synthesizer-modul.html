Roland JV-1010 9,5" Synthesizer-Modul

Synthesizer Module Year: 1999

Description

The JV-1010 is a 64-voice, 16-part multitimbral rack synthesizer without a keyboard, whose sound library can be expanded with SR-JV80 series wave expansion boards. It occupies 1U at half width in the 19" format (9.5"), offers 1 (insert) MFX in addition to chorus and reverb effects, and largely foregoes controls. An editor program is required for editing. A sound program (patch) consists of up to 4 Tones, two of which can be combined via one of 10 structures. A Tone consists of a PCM oscillator, a 12dB multi-mode filter, a dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, and 2 LFOs; by selecting the structure, boosters and ring modulators can optionally be added, and with the same setting of two filters, a quality of 24dB can be achieved. Because Tones can be limited in velocity and note range, split, layer, and crossover sounds are possible within a single patch. In addition to the ROM samples of the JV-1080, those of the Session Board are also included here. The combination of up to 15 parts (with patches) and a rhythm set is managed by a Performance, with MIDI channels freely selectable for each individual part.

Features

  • 64-voice polyphony
  • 16-part multitimbral
  • Rack synthesizer module (without keyboard)
  • Expandable with SR-JV80 wave expansion boards
  • 1U half-width (9.5-inch) rack format
  • 1 insert multi-effect (MFX)
  • Chorus and Reverb effects
  • Minimal physical controls; requires editor software for deep editing
  • Patches consist of up to 4 Tones
  • Tones can be combined via 10 structures
  • Each Tone includes PCM oscillator, 12dB multi-mode filter, dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, 2 LFOs
  • Optional boosters and ring modulators via structure selection
  • 24dB filter quality possible with dual filter setup
  • Supports split, layer, and crossover sounds within a patch
  • Includes ROM samples from JV-1080 and Session Board
  • Performances manage up to 15 parts and one rhythm set
  • Freely assignable MIDI channels per part
