TAK-100 Tuning Amp Kit

Utility Year: 1982

Description

An incredibly small tuner with a headphone jack and an input jack for a mini-amp. AMDEK provides double the fun: the fun of assembly and the pride of one's own achievement, and after assembly, the enjoyment of a high-quality device that does not shy away from professional comparisons. AMDEK offers musicians many, perhaps previously unrecognized, opportunities to engage with their music and the devices used. Even if you've never seen the inside of an effect pedal before, you can still assemble an AMDEK device. The selection is easy; the program includes a whole range, from small metronomes and tuners to programmable rhythm machines and percussion synthesizers. We aim to transform passive customers into active musicians who know more about their equipment than just the model designation and aren't stumped by the smallest technical problem. Musicians who understand and appreciate the inner workings of their electronics, and who enjoy not only playing but also building their electronic devices. The excellent sound: we know there are many kits that fulfill one point: the fun of assembly; but completely disregard another: the sound, i.e., what comes out of an effect device. And we are very confident that an AMDEK device's sound can measure up to any other effect device. This is due to our experience in building effect devices and using high-quality components. High-quality components mean tried-and-tested resistors, capacitors, ICs, etc., which have proven their reliability in use. All components, up to the fully assembled circuit board, are computer-tested elements that have undergone rigorous inspection. But components alone do not make a functional device. An AMDEK device also includes professional design. This means an attractive metal casing with precisely pre-drilled holes for controls, footswitches, and threads. All AMDEK products, unless they have their own power supply, also have a connection option for a power adapter and an absolutely click-free on/off switch. For all effect devices, the operating status is indicated by an LED lamp. AMDEK has a functional design that convinces even the most demanding musician. The instruction manual: the greatest fun can, of course, immediately turn into its opposite if you realize during assembly that the instructions are incomplete or, even worse, too complicated and confusing. We believe we have found a way with the AMDEK instruction manual to enable everyone to easily build an AMDEK effect device. AMDEK offers modification options for some devices, making their application adaptable to individual musicians.

Features

  • DIY kit for a tuner and mini-amplifier
  • Headphone output
  • Input jack
  • Reference oscillator accuracy: ± 0.035 cent
  • Amplifier output level: 200 mW
  • Headphone output: 100 mW x 2 (16 Ohm)
  • Max. input level: -20 dB (1 kHz)
  • Input impedance: 50 kOhm
  • Consumption current: 8.5 mA (no signal)
  • Controls: Mode, Volume
  • Mode options: AMP, 220, 440, 880 (Hz)
  • Terminals: Input, Output, External Power source
  • Power source: 006P battery or external power adapter
  • Dimensions: 68 (W) x 55 (H) x 121 (D) mm
  • Weight: 385 g
  • High-quality components
  • Professional metal casing with pre-drilled holes
  • LED status indicator
  • Click-free on/off switch
  • Modification possibilities
