Edirol SD-20

Sound Module Year: 2004

Description

The SD-20 is a 64-voice MIDI sound module from the Studio Canvas series, powered via USB, serving as a compact, portable complete solution for laptops. The SD-20 offers 32 parts, 660 GM2/GS/XGlite compatible sounds, and 23 drum kits for a wide range of musical genres such as classical, pop, dance, or world music. An optical digital output (S/P DIF) is also available, as is the option to operate the SD-20 with a 9V power adapter and serial connections without USB.

Features

  • 64-voice MIDI sound module
  • Studio Canvas series
  • USB powered (can also use 9V adapter)
  • Compact and portable
  • 32 parts
  • 660 GM2/GS/XGlite compatible sounds
  • 23 drum kits
  • Optical Digital Output (S/P DIF)
  • Can be operated with 9V power adapter and serial connections (without USB)
  • Dimensions: 203 x 35 x 159 mm
