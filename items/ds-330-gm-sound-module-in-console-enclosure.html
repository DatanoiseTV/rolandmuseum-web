DS-330 GM Sound Module in Console Enclosure

Digital Synthesizer Year: 1993

Description

The Boss DS-330 is a 28-voice GM digital synthesizer housed in a drum computer style enclosure. The sound generation is 16-part multitimbral, and the sounds are only very limitedly modifiable, as the DS-330 is based on Roland's SC-55 II sound module.

Features

  • 28-voice GM digital synthesizer
  • Housed in a drum computer style enclosure
  • Sound generation is 16-part multitimbral
  • Sounds are only very limitedly modifiable
  • Based on Roland SC-55 II sound module
  • Power consumption/supply: BRA power supply
  • Dimensions: 215 x 57 x 165 mm
