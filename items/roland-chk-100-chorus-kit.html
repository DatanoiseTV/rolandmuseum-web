Roland CHK-100 Chorus Kit

Compact FX Year: 1982

Description

A stereo chorus effect kit offering control over rate and intensity, also capable of tremolo effects. It's known for its clean chorus sound and was part of Amdek's DIY electronic kit series, emphasizing both build quality and sound quality.

Features

  • Stereo Chorus Effect
  • Tremolo
  • Rate and Depth Control
  • Input Impedance: 470 kOhm
  • Output Load Impedance: Over 10 kOhm
  • Power Source: 006P Battery or External
  • Dimensions: 95 x 64 x 143 mm
  • Weight: 500g
