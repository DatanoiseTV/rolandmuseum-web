R-09HR High Resolution WAVE/MP3 Recorder

Recorder Year: 2008

Description

The Edirol R-09 is a 2-channel WAVE or MP3 recorder, a device type that Roland calls a field recorder. It records to an SD card at 24-bit with 44.1 or 48kHz. SD cards from 64MB to 2GB or SDHC cards from 4 to 8GB are supported. The R-09 has a built-in, decent stereo microphone, but can also record from an external mic or the line input. Data exchange with a computer occurs via a Mini-USB cable, with the recorder appearing as a removable disk.

Features

  • 2-channel WAVE or MP3 recording
  • High Resolution (HR)
  • Recording at 24-bit, 44.1kHz or 48kHz
  • Records to SD card (64MB-2GB) or SDHC card (4-8GB)
  • Built-in stereo microphone
  • External microphone input
  • Line input
  • Mini-USB for data exchange (appears as removable disk)
  • Dimensions: 62 x 27 x 113 mm
  • Weight: 0.174 kg
  • Power: 2 x 1.5V AA/LR6 batteries or PSB-6U AC adapter
  • Current consumption: 0.36A
