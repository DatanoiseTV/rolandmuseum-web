Roland SCC-700 Sound Control Center

Effects Control Unit Year: 1983

Description

The Boss SCC-700 Sound Control Center allows musicians to manage multiple effects units. It can connect up to seven effects devices (pedals, rack units, etc.) and store their settings in 32 programmable patches. Each patch can control the on/off state, order, and individual levels of the connected effects. The SCC-700C is the central unit, while the SCC-700F foot controller allows for on-the-fly patch changes. The SCC-700B is a carrying case designed to house the SCC-700C and up to seven compact pedals.

Features

  • Controls up to 7 effects units
  • 32 programmable patches
  • Controls effect on/off, order, and levels
  • Foot controller available (SCC-700F)
  • Carrying case available (SCC-700B)
