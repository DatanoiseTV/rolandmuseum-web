Boss TU-12HB Chromatic Tuner, black

Utility Year: 1997

Description

The TU-12HB is a chromatic tuner with a built-in microphone and guitar input. It is structurally identical to the bright TU-12H but features a black casing. The tuning range extends from C1 to B6, with tuning displayed via a needle meter in Hz and root notes indicated by LEDs. A switch shifts the display/tuning range by one octave from L (Low, C1-B5) to H (High, C2 to C?).

Features

  • Chromatic tuner
  • Built-in microphone
  • Guitar input
  • Black casing
  • Tuning range: C1 to B6
  • Needle meter display for Hz
  • LEDs for root notes
  • Octave switch (L: C1-B5, H: C2-C?)
  • Power: 1 x 9V Block (6LR61) battery, 7 to 20mA current draw
  • Optional PSA AC adapter
  • Dimensions: 145 x 35 x 53 mm
  • Weight: 0.17 kg
