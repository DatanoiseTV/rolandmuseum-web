Roland US-2

Unit Selector Year: 1982

Description

The Roland US-2 is a switch and splitter used to control two GR synthesizers (GR-100, GR-300, GR-700) individually or together with a G-guitar (G-202, G-303, G-505, G-707, G-808, guitar with GK-1). On the rear, there is one 24-pin input and two such outputs, and on the top, there are two switches for outputs A and B, which can be turned on and off individually. LEDs indicate the switching status. Power is supplied via the 24-pin cable from one of the connected synthesizers. The US-2 can also be used for a G-33 or G-88 and two of the bass synthesizers GR-33B or GR-77B. The US-2 only serves to distribute a bass or guitar signal to two synthesizers and cannot, conversely, distribute two guitars/basses to one synthesizer. Mixed configurations of guitar and bass components are not intended, nor is a combination with the BC-13. Thus, it is not possible to control both the 13-pin and 24-pin systems simultaneously from one guitar via BC-13 and US-2.

Features

  • Switch and splitter for Roland G-Guitars (G-202, G-303, G-505, G-707, G-808, GK-1)
  • Controls two GR Synthesizers (GR-100, GR-300, GR-700) individually or together
  • Compatible with G-33 / G-88 and bass synthesizers (GR-33B, GR-77B)
  • One 24-pin input
  • Two 24-pin outputs
  • Individual switches for outputs A and B
  • LED indicators for switching status
  • Power supplied via 24-pin cable from connected synthesizer
  • Cannot distribute two guitars/basses to one synthesizer
  • Not compatible with mixed guitar/bass components
  • Cannot combine 13-pin and 24-pin systems simultaneously
