Boss PSM-5 Power Supply & Master Switch

Effects Accessory Year: 1990

Description

The PSM-5 is a stabilized power supply that can power up to five BOSS 9-V effects pedals (or other compatible effects pedals). It operates on AC mains power. An AC adapter from the PSA series is included for connection to a power outlet, ensuring trouble-free operation without the risk of depleted batteries. A parallel DC cable with five outputs is included, connecting to the rear of the PSM-5 and the power adapter jacks of individual BOSS effects pedals. But the PSM-5 is much more than a regular power supply; it can also be used as a master switch. The Send jack on the left side of the PSM-5 is connected to the first effects pedal in the chain. Then, the output of the first pedal is connected to the input of the second, and so on. Finally, the last effects pedal in the chain is connected to the Return jack on the right side of the PSM-5. The guitar (or another instrument) is connected to the Guitar jack, and the amplifier to the Amp jack of the PSM-5. These connections create two separate loops – one through the effects pedals, the other directly from the guitar to the amplifier, bypassing the effects pedals. The loops can be easily selected by pressing the footswitch. An LED indicator illuminates when the effects loop is active. The remarkable PSM-5 enhances the performance of your effects pedals and controls them simultaneously – all in one unit.

Features

  • Can power up to five 9V effects pedals
  • Functions as a master A/B switch with footswitch
  • LED indicator for effect loop status
  • Power supply: AC adapter (PSA series)
  • Input impedance (Guitar): 1 MOhm
  • Input impedance (Return): 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance (Amplifier): over 10 kOhm
  • Output load impedance (Send): over 10 kOhm
  • Gain: Unity
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 340g
