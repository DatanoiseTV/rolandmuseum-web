Roland R-8 Human Rhythm Composer

Drum Machine Year: 1989

Description

The R-8 is a 32-voice drum computer with 68 samples, 10 songs, 32 preset, and 100 user patterns. 10 Feel-Patches simulate realistic deviations from the rigid basic pattern of computers. The sounds are widely programmable.

Features

  • 32-voice drum computer
  • 68 samples
  • 10 songs
  • 32 preset patterns
  • 100 user patterns
  • 10 Feel-Patches for realistic rhythm variations
  • Widely programmable sounds
  • Dimensions: 410 x 70 x 290 mm
  • Weight: 3.1 kg
  • Power consumption: 7W
  • Includes AC adapter
  • Recommended accessories: M-256E RAM memory card, SN-R8 series PCM sound cards, DP-2 footswitch (sustain pedal), FS-5U footswitch, EV-5 expression pedal, EV-10 expression pedal
