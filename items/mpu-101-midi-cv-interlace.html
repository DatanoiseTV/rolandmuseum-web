Roland MPU-101 MIDI/CV-Interlace

MIDI-to-CV Interface Year: 1985

Description

The MPU-101 is a MIDI-to-CV interface with CV, Dynamics, and Gate outputs for 4 channels, and an additional 4 control outputs. The MIDI receive channel can be freely set; depending on the polyphony setting, the remaining CV/Gate channels are supplied by the next higher MIDI channels. The MPU-101 can also be operated in Time-Code mode, in which case only the Gate outputs function, providing MIDI Clock-synchronous trigger pulses for quarter notes, eighth notes, and triplets to control old rhythm machines or arpeggiators of older synthesizers, as well as for synchronizing analog sequencers. The MPU-101 was announced as the MI-10 but was never released under that name.

Features

  • MIDI-to-CV interface
  • 4 channels of CV, Dynamics, and Gate outputs
  • 4 additional control outputs (Bender, Modulation, Aftertouch, Volume)
  • Freely assignable MIDI receive channel (1 to 16)
  • Polyphony setting
  • Time-Code mode for MIDI Clock-synchronous trigger pulses (quarter notes, eighth notes, triplets)
  • Octave transposition: High/Medium/Low
  • 3 assignment modes
  • Operating modes: Mono, 2-voice, 3-voice, 4-voice, and special
  • Tune knob (± 100 cents)
  • Output voltage ranges: CV (-3 to +7.58V), Dynamics (0 to +10V), Gate (Off: 0V, On: +14V), Bender (-1 to +1V), Modulation (0 to +10V), Aftertouch (0 to +10V), Volume (0 to +10V)
  • LED indicators: Power, Gate x 4, Hold
  • MIDI jacks: In, Out, Thru
  • Built-in power supply with fixed power cable
