Boss TU-15 Chromatic Tuner

Utility Year: 2003

Description

The TU-15 is a chromatic tuner with a built-in microphone for tuning acoustic instruments and a jack input for electric instruments, which emits a beep when correctly tuned. Tuning is displayed with a combined needle meter and backlit LCD display, along with two LEDs. Besides chromatic mode, the string number can also be displayed in bass or guitar mode instead of the fundamental note, allowing for open tunings and tuning down the guitar by up to 5 semitones. The tuning range is between E0 and C8 with an adjustable concert pitch from 430 to 449Hz.

Features

  • Chromatic tuner
  • Built-in microphone for acoustic instruments
  • Jack input for electric instruments
  • Beep sound for correct tuning
  • Combined needle meter and backlit LCD display with two LEDs
  • Chromatic mode, string number display in bass or guitar mode
  • Open tunings and up to 5 semitones lower tuning possible
  • Tuning range between E0 and C8
  • Adjustable concert pitch from 430 to 449Hz
  • Dimensions: 149 x 40 x 58 mm
  • Weight: 0.19 kg
  • Power: 2 x 1.5V AA (LR6) batteries
  • Power consumption: 15-55mA
  • PSA power adapter compatible
