Roland AP-2 ´Phase II´

Phaser Pedal Year: 1975

Description

The Roland AP-2 'Phase II' is a floor effects pedal for guitar, housed in a sturdy, medium-brown die-cast casing. It functions as a phaser with two controls, RATE and RESONANCE, and an effect/bypass switch on the top. RATE adjusts the modulation speed, while RESONANCE controls the phaser's tonal character. Unlike most other Roland effects, the 'INSTRUMENT' jack input is on the left side, and the mono jack output is on the right, which can complicate multi-effect setups. To replace the two 9V block batteries, the underside of the housing must be unscrewed, as there is no provision for AC adapter operation. The casing shares its design with the Roland AS-1, AF-60, and Boss BF-1. The AP-2 'Phase II' was manufactured by the Roland Music Engineering Group (Roland MEG), which later became known as 'Boss'.

Features

  • Floor effects pedal for guitar
  • Sturdy, medium-brown die-cast housing
  • Phaser with two controls: Rate and Resonance
  • Effect/Bypass switch
  • Instrument jack input on the left, output on the right
  • Battery-powered (2 x 9V block), no AC adapter operation
  • Housing design identical to Roland AS-1, AF-60, Boss BF-1
