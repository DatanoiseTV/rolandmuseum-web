Roland A-70 MIDI Masterkeyboard

Utility Year: 1998

Description

MIDI master keyboard with 76 lightly weighted keys, adjustable velocity sensitivity, and channel aftertouch. 8 zones (4 internal and 4 external) can be assigned to 4 independent MIDI outputs, and 2 footswitches and 2 expression pedals can be connected. It features a performance memory with 64 slots and sequencer remote control (Song Select, Tempo, Reset, Start/Stop). A voice expansion board can be installed, transforming the A-70 from a pure master keyboard into an RD-Piano or JV-Synthesizer.

Features

  • MIDI Master Keyboard
  • 76 lightly weighted keys
  • Adjustable velocity sensitivity
  • Channel aftertouch
  • 8 zones (4 internal, 4 external)
  • 4 independent MIDI outputs
  • Connectors for 2 footswitches and 2 expression pedals
  • 64 performance memory slots
  • Sequencer remote control (Song Select, Tempo, Reset, Start/Stop)
  • Expandable with Voice Expansion Boards (e.g., VE-GS1, VE-RD1, VE-JV1) to function as a digital piano or JV-series synthesizer
  • Built-in power supply
