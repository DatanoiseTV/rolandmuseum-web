Roland TD-8KV-Dum-Kit

Electronic Drum Kit Year: 1999

Description

The TD-8K is an almost complete drum kit, assembled from the following individual components: TD-8 trigger module, KD-80 kick trigger (mesh), PD-80R snare trigger (meshhead), 3 x PD-80 tom triggers (meshhead), 3 x PD-7 hi-hat and cymbal triggers (rubber pads), 2 x MDY-7U cymbal holders, 1 x MDH-7U pad holder, and FD-7 hi-hat control pedal. For a ready-to-play set, a bass drum pedal, a drum stool, and a drum stand (rack with pad and cymbal holders from the MDS series or individual stands) are additionally required.

Features

  • TD-8 trigger module
  • KD-80 kick trigger (mesh)
  • PD-80R snare pad (dual-trigger, mesh)
  • 3 x PD-80 single-trigger tom pads (mesh)
  • 3 x PD-7 dual-trigger hi-hat and cymbal pads (rubber)
  • FD-7 hi-hat control pedal
  • Includes cymbal and pad holders (MDY-7U, MDH-7U)
  • Requires additional bass drum pedal, drum stool, and drum stand
