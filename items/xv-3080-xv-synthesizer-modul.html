XV-3080 XV-Synthesizer-Modul

Digital Synthesizer Year: 2000

Description

A digital synthesizer module from the Roland XV series, designed for rack mounting.

Features

  • Digital synthesizer module
  • Rack-mountable (Dimensions: 482 x 88 x 281 mm, 2U rack)
  • Expandable with SRX Sound Expansion Boards
  • Expandable with SR-JV80 Series Sound Expansion Boards
  • Internal power supply
  • Weight: 4.4 kg
  • Sales period: 2000-2001
