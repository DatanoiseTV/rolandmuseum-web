Roland GK-3B Hexaphonic Bass Pickup

Synthesizer Addons Year: 2004

Description

The Roland GK-3B is a hexaphonic pickup with a 13-pin connector for mounting on a standard bass, used to control bass synthesizers or virtual bass systems of the 13-pin system. Unlike normal pickups, the hexaphonic pickup captures each string individually, providing 4 to 6 signals depending on the number of strings on the bass. The GK-3B is virtually two-part, consisting of the pickup itself, which should be mounted approximately 10mm behind the bridge, and a small control unit connected via a short cable containing the controls. The control unit, which is secured to the strap button and bass surface with a special bracket, has a jack input to connect the signal of the normal pickups, so that this is also transmitted via the 13-pin cable. On the control unit, there are two buttons 'Up/S2' and 'Down/S1', a knob for the (synth) volume, a switch from BASS to MIX to pure GK signal, and the 13-pin connector for the GK cable. The GK-3B is not a MIDI pickup, as is often incorrectly claimed, but rather the prerequisite for a 13-pin device (e.g., V-Bass, GR-20, GI-20, VB-99, etc.) to convert these into MIDI signals. Compared to the GK-2B, the shape of the control unit and the pickup have slightly changed.

Features

  • Hexaphonic pickup with 13-pin connector
  • For mounting on a standard bass
  • Controls bass synthesizers or virtual bass systems (13-pin system)
  • Captures each string individually, providing 4 to 6 signals
  • Two-part design: pickup and small control unit
  • Pickup mounted approximately 10mm behind the bridge
  • Control unit connects via short cable, contains controls
  • Control unit secures to strap button and bass surface with special bracket
  • Jack input on control unit for normal pickup signals, transmitted via 13-pin cable
  • Two 'Up/S2' and 'Down/S1' buttons
  • Knob for synthesizer volume
  • Switch for BASS / MIX / pure GK signal
  • 13-pin connector for GK cable
  • Prerequisite for 13-pin devices to generate MIDI signals (not a MIDI pickup itself)
  • Changed shape compared to GK-2B
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 87/138 mm x 74/13 mm x 23/9 mm
  • Weight: 0.07 kg
  • Power supply via GK cable
