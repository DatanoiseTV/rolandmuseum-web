Roland Roland VP-330

Vocoder Synthesizer Year: 1979

Description

The VP-330 is an 11-band vocoder with a keyboard, featuring both a Voice and a String sound engine that can be used as a carrier signal or independently. Its primary purpose is to analyze sung or spoken vocals and modulate them onto another musical (carrier) signal, essentially making the other signal 'speak.' Ten fixed-frequency filter bands analyze the vowels, while the 11th band analyzes the consonants. In contrast to the rack vocoder SVC-350, the 10 vowel analysis bands here are fixed. Both an external signal and the internal Voice section can be used as the carrier signal.

Features

  • 11-band vocoder
  • Integrated keyboard
  • Internal Voice sound engine
  • Internal String sound engine
  • Voice and String engines usable as carrier signals or standalone
  • Analyzes sung or spoken vocals for modulation
  • 10 fixed-frequency filter bands for vowel analysis
  • 1 filter band for consonant analysis
  • Supports external signals as carrier input
  • Built-in power supply
