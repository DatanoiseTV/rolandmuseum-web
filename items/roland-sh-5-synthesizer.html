Roland SH-5 Synthesizer

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1975

Description

The SH-5 is a heavy, monophonic analog synthesizer with 44 non-velocity-sensitive keys built into a flight case with an angled control panel. It features two VCOs with selectable waveforms (triangle, sawtooth, square, pulse), ring modulation, a noise generator, a fixed bandpass filter, a dynamic multi-filter, and a VCA. It also includes ADSR and AR envelopes, portamento, two LFOs, a sample and hold generator, and a pitch bend lever. No audio effects are present, but multiple control inputs are available.
