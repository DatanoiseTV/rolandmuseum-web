Amdek CHK-100 Chorus Kit

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1982

Description

The Amdek CHK-100 Chorus Kit is a stereo chorus effect unit. It offers tempo and intensity control, allowing for tremolo effects as well. It's notable for being a kit, requiring assembly by the user.

Features

  • Stereo chorus effect
  • Tempo control
  • Intensity control
  • Tremolo effect
  • Input impedance: 470 kOhm
  • Max. input level: 0 dBm (100 Hz) -10 dBm (1 kHz)
  • Output load impedance: Over 10 kOhm
  • Power source: 006P battery or external power source
  • Consumption current: 9.5 mA (DC 9V)
