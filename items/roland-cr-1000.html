Roland CR-1000

Drum Machine / Rhythm Year: 1986

Description

The Roland CR-1000 Digital Drummer is a flat, digital rhythm machine with 24 fixed rhythms and MIDI. Rhythm patterns are grouped into 3 selectable groups of 8 patterns each. Each rhythm offers a variation, 4 fills, an intro, and an ending, all selectable via illuminated buttons. An additional button adds handclaps. Larger buttons start/stop and trigger fill-ins. Eight mini-sliders and a 3-digit LED display (showing tempo) are on the front panel. Sliders control accent strength, groove (straight to heavily shuffled), and individual volume for bass drum, snare/rim/clap, timbale/toms, cymbals/hi-hat, conga, and claves/cowbell. Tempo is set via a large knob (28-249 BPM) or external MIDI clock sync. A smaller volume knob is also present. The rear panel has three footswitch jacks (Start/Stop, Fill-In, Restart), mono output, MIDI In and Thru, power jack and on/off switch. It can run on AC adapter or 6 AA batteries.

Features

  • 24 Fixed Rhythms
  • MIDI
  • 3 Selectable Rhythm Groups
  • Variations, Fills, Intro, Ending
  • Handclap sound
  • Tempo Control (28-249 BPM)
  • External MIDI Clock Sync
  • Individual Volume Sliders
  • Accent and Groove Controls
  • Footswitch Jacks
  • Mono Output
  • MIDI In/Thru
  • AC Adapter or Battery Power
