Boss PH-1R Phaser

Phaser Pedal Year: 1982

Description

The PH-1R is an enhanced version of the PH-1 phaser pedal, featuring all its electronic refinements plus an additional 'Resonance' control for deeper phasing effects and an improved signal-to-noise ratio of over 90dB. Like the PH-1, it generates phase shifts up to 720°, delivering classic phase effects with clarity, brilliance, and depth. Its controls include Speed (LFO range 100ms to 16 seconds) and Depth, making it a flexible phaser suitable for guitar, bass, keyboards, PA systems, and studio use.

Features

  • 9V battery or AC adapter power
  • DC9V, 7mA current draw
  • 4-stage phaser
  • LFO speed range: 100ms to 16 seconds
  • Signal-to-noise ratio: >90dB (for PH-1R)
  • Maximum input: -20dB
  • Phase shift: 720°
  • Unity gain
  • Dimensions: 2.8(W) x 2.2(H) x 4.9(D) inches
  • Weight: 1.1 lbs
  • Additional Resonance control for deeper effects
  • Speed and Depth controls
  • Versatile for guitar, bass, keyboards, PA, and studio applications
