Boss DD-3 Digital Delay

Compact Effects Pedal Year: 1986

Description

The DD-3 Digital Delay is extremely compact but offers the excellent sound quality and versatility of an advanced digital delay unit. The delay time can be continuously adjusted between 12.5 and 800 ms. The frequency response ranges from 40 Hz to 7 kHz. Three different delay ranges can be selected with the mode selector switch: S-range (12.5 to 50 ms), M-range (50 to 200 ms), and L-range (200 to 800 ms). The delay time for each range is set with the D. Time knob. The E. Level knob adjusts the balance between the direct sound and the delayed sound, and the number of repetitions can be selected with the F. Back knob. Stereo effects can be created due to the two separate output jacks. A hold function allows setting the hold time between 200 and 800 ms with the D. Time knob when the mode selector is set to hold mode. The hold function can be switched on and off with an effect pedal. Like similar BOSS products, the DD-3 features silent FET switching, an LED on/off indicator, and a battery status display.

Features

  • Digital delay effect
  • Compact design
  • Adjustable delay time: 12.5 to 800 ms
  • Frequency response: 40 Hz to 7 kHz
  • Three delay ranges: S (12.5-50 ms), M (50-200 ms), L (200-800 ms)
  • Controls: D. Time, E. Level, F. Back
  • Two separate output jacks for stereo effects
  • Hold function (200-800 ms), switchable via effect pedal
  • Silent FET switching
  • LED on/off and battery status indicator
  • Power supply: 9V battery or AC adapter (PSA series)
  • Power consumption: 9V, 55 mA (delay time knob in middle)
  • A/D/A converter: 12-bit logarithmic compression
  • S/N ratio: -96 dBm (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm (FET input)
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 450g
