Roland SH-7

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1978

Description

The SH-7 is a two-voice analog synthesizer with 44 non-velocity-sensitive keys, built into a flight case lower section and featuring an angled control panel. It has two oscillators, both independently adjustable in octave (2, 4, 8, 16, 32) and waveform (triangle, sawtooth, square, and pulse width modulation). Only VCO1 also provides a freely adjustable 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 octave mixture of square waves. VCO2 can be synchronized to VCO1 or used when playing two-voice on the keyboard. Mixable noise and a ring modulator complete the sound generation, which is shaped by a fixed high-pass filter and the dynamic VCF and VCA blocks. Two envelopes are available. Modulation is possible using the pitch bend lever, LFO, and a random generator. CV/Gate inputs and outputs are also available, as well as an external audio input, headphone jack and control input for the filter frequency.
