PH-1 Phaser

Compact FX Year: 1980

Description

Phase shift effects are among the most useful effects developed recently. The PH-1 produces phase shifts of up to 720°, creating all classic phase effects with clarity, brilliance, and depth. The two controls on the PH-1 are Speed and Depth. The speed control covers a wide LFO range from 100 ms to 16 seconds. The depth control regulates the effect intensity, allowing for the setting of any desired phasing effect. Combining all the above features with the technical 'BOSS Standard', the PH-1 is a uniquely flexible phaser suitable not only for guitar, bass, or keyboards but also ideally applicable in PA systems or studios due to its high signal-to-noise ratio.

Features

  • 9V Battery or AC Adapter Power
  • 7mA Current Draw
  • 4-Stage Phase
  • LFO Speed: 100ms - 16 Seconds
  • S/N Ratio: Over 80dB
  • Maximum Input: -20dB
  • Phase Shift: 720°
  • Unity Gain
  • Suitable for Guitar, Bass, Keyboards, PA, and Studio applications
