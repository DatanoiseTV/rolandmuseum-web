DSD-3 Digital Sampler/Delay

Compact FX Year: 1986

Description

The DSD-3, with an extremely favorable price-performance ratio, can be used either as a digital sampling device or as a digital delay device. It contains a 12-bit A/D/A converter, achieving a frequency response of 40 Hz to 7 kHz. The DSD-3 enhances your creative possibilities for both recording and live performance. The sampling time can be set between 200 and 800 ms. There are two operating modes: Rec/Play and Play Only. In Rec/Play mode, recording and replay occur simultaneously. When the feedback control is set to maximum, it's possible to overdub another sample sound onto a previously recorded sound. In Play Only mode, the DSD-3 plays back the stored sample sound that was recorded in Rec/Play mode. Playback of the recorded sample sound is possible by pressing the pedal or by feeding an external trigger signal into the DSD-3. With the Time knob, the pitch of the recorded sample sound can be changed during playback. Additionally, the DSD-3 can be used as a digital delay device with a delay time of 50 to 800 ms. The delay time is determined by selecting S-mode (50 to 200 ms) or L-mode (200 to 800 ms) and turning the Time knob. The delay time can also be controlled with external trigger signals.

Features

  • 12-bit A/D/A converter
  • Frequency response: 40 Hz to 7 kHz
  • Sampling time: 200 to 800 ms
  • Two operating modes: Rec/Play and Play Only
  • Overdub capability in Rec/Play mode (with max feedback)
  • Playback via pedal or external trigger signal
  • Pitch change of sample sound during playback (via Time knob)
  • Digital delay function with 50 to 800 ms delay time
  • Delay time adjustable via S-mode (50-200ms) or L-mode (200-800ms) and Time knob
  • Delay time controllable via external trigger signals
  • Power supply: 9V (battery or AC adapter PSA-series)
  • Power consumption: 9V, 45 to 60 mA
  • Signal-to-noise ratio: -95 dBm (IHF-A)
  • Gain: Unity
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm (FET input)
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 450 g
