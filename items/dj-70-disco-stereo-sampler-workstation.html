DJ-70 Disco Stereo Sampler Workstation

Sampler Year: 1992

Description

The stereo sampler DJ-70 features a velocity-sensitive 3-octave keyboard, is 24-voice polyphonic, 31-part multitimbral, and includes an 8-track sequencer with RPS. The sampler unit uses the same data format as the S-700 series, layering up to 4 samples in partials, of which up to 88 form a patch. Each partial includes dynamic amplifiers and filters with resonance. The DJ-70 can reload samples during playback and also process sound floppy disks from the S-330 and S-550. The DJ-70 comes with 2MB of sample memory, which can be upgraded to 4MB. The only storage medium used is the floppy disk drive (DD and HD floppies). A special feature is the Scratch-Dial, which allows samples to be scratched like records.

Features

  • Stereo sampler workstation
  • Velocity-sensitive 3-octave keyboard
  • 24-voice polyphony
  • 31-part multitimbral
  • 8-track sequencer with RPS (Realtime Phrase Sequencing)
  • S-700 series data format compatible
  • Up to 4 samples layered in partials, up to 88 partials form a patch
  • Dynamic amplifiers and resonant filters per partial
  • Ability to reload samples during playback
  • Compatible with S-330 and S-550 sound floppy disks
  • 2MB sample memory (expandable to 4MB)
  • Floppy disk drive (DD and HD)
  • Scratch-Dial for record-style scratching effects
