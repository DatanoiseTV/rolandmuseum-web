Roland JV-880

Synthesizer Module Year: 1992

Description

The JV-880 is a 28-voice, 8-part multitimbral digital synthesizer module. A sound program (Patch) consists of up to 4 Tones, two of which can be combined via one of 10 structures. A Tone consists of a PCM oscillator, a 12dB multi-mode filter, a dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, and 2 LFOs. Depending on the chosen structure, a booster and ring modulator can optionally be added, and with the same setting of two filters, a quality of 24dB can be achieved. Because Tones can be limited in velocity and note range, split, layer, and crossover sounds are possible within a single patch. The combination of up to 7 parts (with patches) and a rhythm set is managed by a Performance. The sound library of the JV-880 can be expanded with a Wave Expansion Board, providing additional PCM samples to the oscillators. The JV-880 is the module version of the JV-80.

Features

  • 28-voice polyphony
  • 8-part multitimbral
  • Digital synthesizer module
  • Patches consist of up to 4 Tones
  • 10 structures for combining Tones
  • Each Tone includes a PCM oscillator, 12dB multi-mode filter, dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, and 2 LFOs
  • Optional booster and ring modulator (structure-dependent)
  • Achieves 24dB filter slope with two filters
  • Supports split, layer, and crossover sounds within a single patch
  • Performances manage up to 7 parts and one rhythm set
  • Sound library expandable with Wave Expansion Boards
  • Integrated power supply
