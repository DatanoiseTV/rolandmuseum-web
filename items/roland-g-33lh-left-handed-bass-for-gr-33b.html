Roland G-33LH Left-handed Bass for GR-33B

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1982

Description

The G-33 is a controller bass for the Roland GR-33B bass guitar synthesizer (with a 24-pin connector) and has the body shape of a typical Fender bass. In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal of the special pickup, which outputs the signals of the four strings separately and can thus control GR synthesizers, the bass also has a normal bass output from which the sound of the single-coil pickup with double magnets is output. The G-33 can therefore also be used as a normal bass. The bass has a maple neck glued to an ash body and was manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (more precisely by the supplier Fuji Gen Gakki), with a left-handed model G-33LH also available.

Features

  • 24-pin connector for special pickup signal
  • Outputs individual string signals for GR synthesizer control
  • Standard bass output for single-coil pickup with double magnets
  • Can be used as a regular bass
  • Maple neck
  • Ash body
  • Left-handed model (G-33LH) available
  • Master Volume control
  • Cutoff Frequency control
  • Balance control between bass and synthesizer level
  • Separate bass and treble controls
  • Resonance (VCF) control
  • Vibrato intensity control
  • Three-stage switch for Bass/Synthesizer/Both signal selection
