PW-1 Rocker Wah

Compact FX Year: 1980

Description

This Wah-Wah pedal features some innovations that cannot be overlooked. The effect width of the Wah-Wah is pre-adjustable with a knob, meaning the pedal travel always remains the same, only the Wah-Wah range changes. Switching between effect and bypass is done with a FET switch, as known from compact effects. The operating status is indicated by an LED, making the status visible even on a dark stage. This LED also serves as a battery check. The pedal naturally also has an AC adapter connection, volume adjustment, rubber feet, and a secure compartment for the battery. When we decided to develop real effect pedals, we faced a problem that many effect pedals had failed to solve: the controller for pedal control. Previously, normal potentiometers were used, but they started to crackle and scratch after a few hours of operation. These noises made the pedal unusable. Then optoelectronic control was invented, which is very sensitive to knocks and impacts. Often, the right replacement bulb(s) were missing at the crucial moment. To build a pedal like this Rocker Pedal free from any wear and tear, the invention of a new type of controller was necessary. With the new magnetic field controller, we achieved this optimum. This revolutionary controller design contains no touching mechanical parts and no sensitive or wear-prone elements. This controller works silently and cleanly for a lifetime.

Features

  • Wah-Wah effect pedal
  • Adjustable effect width (Wah-Wah range)
  • FET switch for effect/bypass switching
  • LED status indicator (also for battery check)
  • AC adapter connection
  • Volume adjustment
  • Rubber feet
  • Secure battery compartment
  • Revolutionary magnetic field controller (wear-free, silent operation)
