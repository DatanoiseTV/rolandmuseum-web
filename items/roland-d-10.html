Roland Roland D-10

Digital Synthesizer Year: 1988

Description

The D-10 is a 9-part multitimbral, 32-voice digital synthesizer with 61 velocity-sensitive synthesizer keys and MIDI. 8 tonally playable parts (partial synthesizers) and one rhythm part utilize LA synthesis, a combination of PCM samples without filters and simpler synthesis with filters. A sound program is called 'Timbre' and consists of 2 'Tones', which in turn are formed from one or two 'Partials'. A Partial is either a PCM oscillator (ROM samples) with a subsequent amp or consists of a simple, pulse-width-modulatable oscillator (triangle/square) with a dynamic filter and amp. 13 structures can be selected for combining the two Partials into a Tone, so a sound program (Timbre) can consist of up to 4 oscillators. The filter offers resonance without self-oscillation and sounds exceptionally 'warm'. The internally available 256 PCM samples are somewhat shorter than those of the first LA synthesizer D-50. The sound generation is the same as in the D-5, D-20, and D-110 and concludes with a digital reverb offering 8 algorithms. A PG-10 programmer can be connected for easier programming. The D-10 and other models of the D-series were the first synthesizers to have demo songs in ROM. The D-10 has a built-in drum machine, which allows composing a song from 1- or 2-bar patterns or playing the sounds manually in manual mode. There are 32 preset and 32 freely programmable patterns. The D-10 has two operating modes: Performance and Multitimbral. For live performance, two sounds (parts) can be layered or split in Performance mode. In Multitimbral mode, 8 parts can be controlled simultaneously via MIDI. 128 Performances, 64 Tones, and 64 Timbres can be stored internally; an inserted M-128 or M-256 memory card doubles the memory.

Features

  • 9-part multitimbral
  • 32-voice polyphony
  • 61 velocity-sensitive synthesizer keys
  • MIDI connectivity
  • LA synthesis (PCM samples + synthesis)
  • Sound program 'Timbre' composed of 2 'Tones', each made of 1-2 'Partials'
  • Partials: PCM oscillator (ROM samples) with amp or PWM oscillator (triangle/square) with dynamic filter and amp
  • 13 structures for combining Partials
  • Filter with resonance (non-self-oscillating)
  • 256 internal PCM samples
  • Digital reverb with 8 algorithms
  • PG-10 Programmer connectivity
  • Demo songs in ROM
  • Built-in drum machine
  • 32 preset and 32 user-programmable drum patterns
  • Performance mode (layering/splitting)
  • Multitimbral mode (8 parts via MIDI)
  • 128 performances, 64 tones, 64 timbres storable internally
  • M-128/M-256 memory card expands memory
