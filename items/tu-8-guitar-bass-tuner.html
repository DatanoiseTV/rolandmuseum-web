TU-8 Guitar/Bass Tuner

Utility Year: 1994

Description

The TU-8 is a guitar/bass tuner with a needle meter for precise tuning indication and LEDs for string status. In addition to standard tuning, the TU-8 allows for open tunings (D, E, G, A major) or guitar tunings 1 or 2 semitones lower than normal. The tuning range extends from A0 to C8, with a selectable reference pitch from 438 to 445 Hz. An integrated microphone is included for tuning acoustic guitars.

Features

  • Guitar/Bass tuner
  • Needle meter for precise tuning
  • LEDs for string pitch indication
  • Standard tuning
  • Open tunings (D, E, G, A major)
  • Guitar tunings 1 or 2 semitones lower than normal
  • Tuning range from A0 to C8
  • Adjustable reference pitch from 438 to 445 Hz
  • Built-in microphone for tuning acoustic guitars
  • Battery operation (1 x 9V block)
  • PSA AC adapter compatible
