Boss CE-1 Chorus Ensemble

Effects Pedal Year: 1976

Description

The BOSS Chorus Ensemble is the originator of the chorus effect and quickly became a renowned effect pedal, widely copied by other manufacturers. The CE-1 produces a rich and intense sonic texture that gives solo instruments a thick, deep sound, comparable to doubling a voice – a 'chorus' of voices. Additionally, the CE-1 creates a vibrato effect. These effects are activated by two footswitches: one toggles between Normal and Effect, the other switches between Chorus and Vibrato effects.

Features

  • Adjustable chorus effect intensity
  • Independent controls for vibrato: speed and depth
  • Stereo outputs for a large and wide chorus effect when connected to two amplifiers
  • Power consumption: 3W
  • Input sensitivity: 80mV/10mV
  • Output level: 200mV
  • S/N ratio: 60dB (Over 75dB with noise reduction)
  • Indicators: Peak level, Chorus/Vibrato/Normal
  • Dimensions: 10.2(W) x 2.5(H) x 7.1(D) in.
  • Weight: 3.96lbs.
