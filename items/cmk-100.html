Roland CMK-100

Compressor Kit Year: 1982

Description

A low-noise compressor that guarantees a beautiful, clean, and long tone. It was part of the Amdek line, aimed at providing musicians with the enjoyment of assembling their equipment and the pride of accomplishment. Amdek highlighted the dual benefits of learning about the equipment's inner workings and enjoying high-quality sound comparable to professional devices. Amdek emphasized the use of high-quality, computer-tested components and a professional design. The kit includes a well-written manual, and some devices offer modification options to tailor the device to individual musicians.

Features

  • Low-noise compressor
  • Sustain and Level controls
  • Effect changeover switch
  • Effect indicator
  • External power source option (006P battery or DC 9V adapter)
  • Dimensions: 62 x 56 x 128 mm
  • Weight: 360g
  • 4 modification points
