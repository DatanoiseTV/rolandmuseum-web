Roland MV-30 Studio M, Sequencer with RS-PCM Sound Generation

Sequencer Year: 1990

Description

The MV-30 is a combination of a 30-voice, 8-part multitimbral sound engine and a 16-track sequencer in a console-style housing. The sound generation is based on that of the U-20, expanded with the filters of the D-70. A sound program is called a 'Timbre' and is based on a 'Tone,' which is shaped by a dynamic, resonant filter and dynamic amplitude, and modulated by two envelopes and an LFO. A Tone consists of one or two oscillators that play 1 or 2 fixed PCM waveforms, either together, split, or crossfaded by velocity. The MV-30 has 2 slots for PCM cards to expand the internal library of 220 preset tones; cards for the U-series and D-70 can also be used. There are 7 normal synthesizer parts to which 128 storable Timbres can be assigned, and one rhythm part (for rhythm Timbre 129). The 128 Timbres and the rhythm Timbre can be saved together as a set under an 8-character name on a floppy disk. 8 sets can be stored per floppy disk. The sequencer is mainly song-oriented but also offers 20 patterns per song. There are a total of 18 tracks, with 1 track for (MIDI) mixer data and one tempo track not intended for notes. The remaining tracks are divided into 8 single-channel tracks, which are fixed to the internal sound engine parts and cannot output data externally. The last 8 tracks can record mixed data from all 16 MIDI channels and either output them via MIDI or route them to the internal sound engine. Each of the 16 note tracks can also optionally be declared as a pattern track and then call up a pattern sequence. Up to 20 patterns of a song can be assigned as RPS phrases to individual keys (note numbers) and then manually started at any time using the RPS function. Realtime and step input are available as recording methods, and individual events can be edited in detail. Internally, approximately 50,000 events can be recorded; on floppy disk, approximately 70,000-100,000 events can be stored. The maximum number of songs is 20 (internal) or 64 songs (floppy disk). In addition to its own format, the MV-30 can load songs from W-30, MC sequencers (MC-300, MC-500, MC-500MKII, and MC-50), S-samplers (SYS-503, SYS-333, SYS-553), and DOS-formatted SMF disks. Songs can be saved as MV-30, W-30, S-MRC, and SMF songs. The sequencer can operate as a master or as a slave to MIDI Clock and understands the Song Position Pointer. The MV-30 can generate an FSK signal for tape synchronization and can also run as a slave to a tape with FSK.

Features

  • 30-voice polyphonic, 8-part multitimbral sound engine
  • 16-track sequencer in console-style housing
  • Sound engine based on U-20 with D-70 filters
  • Dynamic, resonant filter and amplitude
  • Two envelopes and one LFO for modulation
  • Tones use one or two oscillators playing PCM waveforms (together, split, or velocity-crossfaded)
  • 2 slots for PCM expansion cards (compatible with U-series and D-70 cards)
  • 220 internal preset tones
  • 7 synthesizer parts, 1 rhythm part
  • 128 storable Timbres + 1 rhythm Timbre
  • Save 8 'Sets' of Timbres/rhythm on floppy disk (8-character name)
  • Song-oriented sequencer with 20 patterns per song
  • 18 tracks total (1 mixer data, 1 tempo, 8 internal synth/rhythm, 8 multi-MIDI/internal)
  • 16 note tracks can be pattern tracks
  • RPS (Realtime Phrase Sequence) function
  • Realtime and step input recording with detailed event editing
  • Internal memory: ~50,000 events; Floppy disk: ~70,000-100,000 events
  • Max songs: 20 (internal), 64 (floppy disk)
  • Loads songs from W-30, MC sequencers, S-samplers, and DOS-formatted SMF disks
  • Saves songs as MV-30, W-30, S-MRC, and SMF songs
  • MIDI Master/Slave operation with MIDI Clock and Song Position Pointer
  • FSK signal generation for tape synchronization
