Roland G-303W Guitar for GR-100/GR-300

Other Year: 1982

Description

The G-303 is a controller guitar designed for Roland guitar synthesizers with a 24-pin connector, featuring a distinctive double-cutaway shape. The 'W' suffix simply denotes the option of a white finish. In addition to the 24-pin connector for its special 'hexaphonic' pickup (which outputs individual string signals for controlling GR-synthesizers), it also includes a standard guitar output for its two built-in humbucker pickups, allowing it to function as a regular electric guitar.

Features

  • Dimensions: 322 x 1020 x 66 mm
  • Weight: 3.7 kg
  • No battery or external power supply required
  • Includes guitar case
  • 24-pin connector for Roland GR-100/GR-300 guitar synthesizers
  • Special hexaphonic pickup for individual string signal output
  • Standard guitar output with two built-in humbucker pickups
  • Nickel-plated metal parts
  • Mahogany body with a glued-in maple neck
  • Controls: Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, Balance (Guitar/Synthesizer level), Resonance (VCF), Vibrato Strength
  • Three-stage mode switch
  • Three-stage selector switch (Guitar, Synthesizer, or Both)
  • Also available as a left-handed model (G-303LH)
  • Manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (Fuji Gen Gakki) to Roland specifications
