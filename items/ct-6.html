Boss CT-6

Tuner Year: 1997

Description

The CT-6 is a guitar/bass tuner with a built-in microphone for acoustic instruments and a jack input for electric instruments. It has an 18-LED display and a tuning range from A0 to B7, with adjustable calibration from 438 to 445 Hz and an accuracy of +/-3 cents.

Features

  • Built-in microphone
  • 1/4" jack input
  • 18-LED display
  • A0-B7 tuning range
  • Adjustable calibration (438-445 Hz)
  • +/- 3 cent accuracy
