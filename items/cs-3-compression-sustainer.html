Boss CS-3 Compression Sustainer

Compact Effect Pedal Year: 1986

Description

The CS-3 Compressor/Sustainer can apply compression to any electronic or electric instrument without unwanted distortion. Equipped with a specially developed compression circuit, the CS-3 offers excellent sound quality and versatility for all applications, from live performance to studio recording. Four controls are available for a variety of effects: Sustain determines the sustain time and can also function as a limiter; Tone allows fine-tuning of the effect sound's timbre, enhancing sound variety and projection; Attack adjusts the attack time over a wide range for clear compression, even on fast passages; Level controls the volume balance between effected and normal signals.

Features

  • Specially developed compression circuit
  • Four controls: Sustain, Tone, Attack, Level
  • Sustain control for sustain time and limiter function
  • Tone control for fine-tuning timbre
  • Attack control for wide-range attack time adjustment
  • Level control for volume balance
  • Power: 9V battery or AC adapter (ACA series)
  • Current draw: 10 mA
  • Compression range: 38 dB
  • Maximum input level: +5 dBm (at 1 kHz)
  • Maximum output level: -10 dBm
  • Equivalent input noise: -110 dBm (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 400g
