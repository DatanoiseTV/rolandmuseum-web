TU-120 Tuner

Utility Year: 1979

Description

The TU-120 is a chromatic tuner with a rotary switch for the 12 fundamental tones and an LED chain to indicate tuning accuracy. The display range covers C2 to B6, with the concert pitch adjustable to 440, 442, 443, or 444 Hz. The light-emitting diodes (LEDs) form a kind of interference 'running light,' meaning they flash with varying brightness. When the brightest illumination remains in the same position, the tuning is correct; otherwise, the brightness maximum 'runs' to the right or left, depending on whether the string is tuned too high or too low. A built-in microphone allows tuning of acoustic instruments. Before tuning, the tuner must be calibrated by setting the FUNCTION switch to CHECK and turning the CALIBRATION knob until the brightest light stops in the center. Afterward, switch to TUNE to begin tuning. You can also switch to TONE mode to output the concert pitch. The TU-120 can be powered by a 9V block battery or an ACA AC adapter.

Features

  • Chromatic tuner
  • Rotary switch for 12 fundamental tones
  • LED chain for tuning accuracy display (interference running light)
  • Display range C2 to B6
  • Concert pitch adjustable to 440, 442, 443, or 444 Hz
  • Built-in microphone for acoustic instruments
  • CHECK function for calibration
  • Calibration knob
  • TUNE mode
  • TONE mode (outputs concert pitch)
  • 9V block battery or ACA AC adapter power
  • Dimensions: 97(W) x 40(H) x 194(D) mm
  • Weight: 0.5 kg
  • Current consumption: 13.3mA
