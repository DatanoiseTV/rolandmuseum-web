JV-2080 Expandable Synthesizer Module

Digital Synthesizer Year: 1997

Description

The JV-2080 is a 64-voice, 16-part multitimbral rack synthesizer without a keyboard, whose sound library can be expanded with eight SR-JV80 series Wave Expansion Boards. It occupies 2U in a 19-inch rack format and offers 3 insert MFX effects in addition to chorus and reverb. A sound program (Patch) consists of up to 4 Tones, two of which can be combined via one of 10 structures. A Tone consists of a PCM oscillator, a 12dB multi-mode filter, a dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, and 2 LFOs. By choosing the structure, optional booster and ring modulator can be added, and with identical settings of two filters, a quality of 24dB can be achieved. Because Tones can be limited in velocity and note range, split, layer, and crossover sounds are possible within a single patch. The ROM sample library is identical to the JV-1080. The compilation of up to 15 parts (with patches) and a rhythm set is managed by a Performance, with MIDI channels freely selectable for each individual part.

Features

  • 64-voice polyphony
  • 16-part multitimbral
  • Rack synthesizer module (2U, 19-inch)
  • Expandable with 8 SR-JV80 series Wave Expansion Boards
  • 3 insert MFX effects, plus chorus and reverb
  • Patches with up to 4 Tones
  • Tones can be combined via 10 structures
  • Each Tone: PCM oscillator, 12dB multi-mode filter, dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, 2 LFOs
  • Optional booster and ring modulator
  • Filter quality up to 24dB (when combining two filters)
  • Split, layer, and crossover sounds within a patch
  • ROM sample library identical to JV-1080
  • Performance mode for up to 15 parts and one rhythm set
  • Freely selectable MIDI channels for each part
