Roland AF-100 ´Bee Baa´ Treble Booster

Guitar/Bass Effect Pedal Year: 1972

Description

The Roland AF-100 'Bee Baa' is a floor effect pedal for guitar housed in a sturdy metal casing. It is a combined TREBLE BOOSTER and FUZZ effect. A switch on the top, labeled identically, allows switching between the two effects. The 'FUZZ TONE SELECT' switch to its left changes the timbre of the Fuzz effect. The switch on the right is used to toggle between EFFECT and NORMAL signals. The placement of the jacks and controls on the narrow back is unusual. On the far right is the mono jack input labeled 'TO GUITAR', followed by three controls for the Fuzz: SUSTAIN, TONE, and VOLUME, then the 'ADJ.' control for the BOOSTER, and on the far left, the jack output 'TO AMP'. Turning the Fuzz SUSTAIN control fully counter-clockwise to the 'OFF' position noticeably disengages the Fuzz. To change the battery, the two rear, side screws can be slightly loosened, allowing the top part of the casing to be folded up. The silver casing was also used for the AG-5 'Funny Cat'. According to company founder Ikutaro Kakehashi, the name 'Bee Baa' can be understood by paying attention to the sound. The AF-100 was manufactured by the Roland Music Engineering Group (Roland MEG), which was later renamed 'Boss'.

Features

  • Combined Treble Booster and Fuzz effect
  • Switch to select between Treble Booster and Fuzz effects
  • Fuzz Tone Select switch for timbre adjustment
  • Switch for Effect/Normal signal selection
  • Controls for Fuzz: Sustain, Tone, Volume
  • Control for Booster: Adj.
  • Mono jack input ('TO GUITAR')
  • Jack output ('TO AMP')
  • Battery-operated (1 x 9V block)
  • Sturdy metal casing
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 230 x 65 x 165 mm
  • Weight: 1.05 kg
