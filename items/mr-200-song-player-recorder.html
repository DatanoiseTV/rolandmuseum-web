MR-200 Song Player/Recorder

Sequencer Year: 2004

Description

The MR-200 is an SMF recorder/player, which is a MIDI sequencer with a 3.5" floppy drive. The MR-200 has 16 tracks, where each track is permanently assigned to a MIDI channel of the same number, and each track can be output from either MIDI OUT 1 or MIDI OUT 2, or not at all. A repeat function and overall transposition are adjustable, otherwise, there are no extensive editing functions. The MR-200 can act as a MIDI Clock Master or Slave and its design strongly resembles its ancestor PR-100.

Features

  • SMF Recorder/Player Functionality
  • MIDI Sequencer with 3.5" Floppy Drive
  • 16 Tracks (each assigned to a dedicated MIDI Channel)
  • Tracks Routable to MIDI OUT 1, MIDI OUT 2, or Off
  • Adjustable Repeat Function
  • Global Transposition
  • MIDI Clock Master/Slave Capability
  • Powered by PSB-1U
