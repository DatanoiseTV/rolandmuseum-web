Boss Boss PS-2

Compact Effects Pedal Year: 1986

Description

The PS-2 Digital Pitch Shifter/Delay provides advanced pitch-shifting and delay effects in a compact format. At the heart of the PS-2 is an LSI chip, a BOSS proprietary development, which enables high-speed pitch-shifting over a full octave range from -1 to +1, pitch-shifted chorus and octave effects, as well as a variety of harmony effects. For immediate and precise octave effects, the PS-2 is equipped with preset +1 and -1 octave modes. A chromatic tuner, such as the TU-12, can be connected to the Tuner Out jack to check the amount of pitch shift in manual mode. Metallic-sounding effects can be easily set by mixing the direct sound with the pitch-shifted sound of up to +1 octave. To achieve a truly thick sound, you must mix the direct sound with the pitch-shifted sound of -1 octave. The PS-2 ensures clear effects even when playing chords. For harmonic effects, mix the direct sound with pitch-shifted thirds or fifths. The PS-2 can also be used as a high-quality digital delay, offering up to 2,000 ms of delay.

Features

  • Digital Pitch Shifter and Delay effects
  • BOSS proprietary LSI chip
  • Pitch-shifting range: -1 to +1 octave
  • High-speed pitch-shifting
  • Pitch-shifted chorus and octave effects
  • Variety of harmony effects (thirds, fifths)
  • Preset +1 and -1 octave modes
  • Tuner Out jack for chromatic tuner
  • Mix direct sound with pitch-shifted sound for various effects
  • Clear effects even with chords
  • High-quality digital delay function
  • Delay time: 30 ms to 2,000 ms
  • Power supply: 9V battery or AC adapter (PSA series)
  • Power consumption: 9V, 60 mA
