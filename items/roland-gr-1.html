Roland GR-1

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1981

Description

The Roland GR-1 guitar synthesizer is a self-contained unit that's as easy to connect and use as an effects pedal. You can connect your favorite guitar to the GR-1 using the GK-2 Guitar Driver, opening up a whole new world of sound synthesis. All the features of the GR-1 have been tailored to the needs of guitarists, from the editing options and effects to the built-in 4-track sequencer. It's easy to use and offers guitarists all the possibilities of sound synthesis and MIDI. It features Roland's latest digital technology and incredible sounds. With the multitimbral capabilities and the built-in sequencer, you have access to a whole range of sounds directly from your guitar. You can enter the world of synthesizers without losing your connection to the guitar. With the GR-1, you can produce sounds that were previously only available to keyboards without changing your playing habits. The sensitivity of the divided GK-2 pickup can be individually adjusted for each string on the GR-1's control surface. An optional Wave Expansion Board (SR-GR1-01) can be installed to increase capacity to 400 sounds. Editing can be done easily with the seven knobs on the Macro Edit section. You can select two of the 200 sounds and pre-program their application in four different ways: the first sound alone, the second sound alone, both together, and mute for each individual string. The envelope (attack, decay, release), filter (cutoff/resonance), and vibrato (rate/depth) parameters can be edited separately for each sound. For the two selected sounds, reverb and chorus can be switched on or off, and volume balance, detune, stereo panning, and vibrato can be adjusted individually. Note shift is individually adjustable for each string. The GR-1 can store 64 individually designed patches with all parameter settings in its internal memory. An additional 64 patches can be stored on the optionally available M-256 E memory card.

Features

  • Guitar Synthesizer
  • GK-2 compatible
  • 4-track sequencer
  • Wave Expansion Board (optional)
  • MIDI In/Out
  • Effects: Reverb, Chorus
  • Foot switches for parameter changes and sound selection
  • 200 sounds (expandable to 400)
