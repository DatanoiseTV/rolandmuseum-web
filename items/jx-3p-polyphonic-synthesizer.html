JX-3P Polyphonic Synthesizer

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1983

Description

The JX-3P is a 6-voice, digitally controlled analog synthesizer with 61 (non-velocity-sensitive) keys, a 128-step sequencer, 32 presets, and 32 user sounds. Per voice, it features 2 DCOs, 1 resonant VCF, and 1 VCA (both dynamic), a static high-pass filter, and only 1 envelope and 1 LFO; the VCA could be switched to Gate mode. The oscillators produce square, sawtooth, and pulse waves, with the second oscillator also capable of generating noise. Hard sync and Metal (a type of cross-modulation) provide further sound variety. The JX-3P originated in the early days of MIDI and could only be operated in Omni mode by default. The JX-3P did not support Control Changes (except for modulation and sustain) or SysEx data. Data could be saved to tape, and for programming, the separately available PG-200 programmer could be connected as an alternative to MIDI, but not simultaneously.

Features

  • 6-voice polyphony
  • Digitally controlled analog synthesizer
  • 61 non-velocity-sensitive keys
  • 128-step sequencer
  • 32 presets and 32 user sounds
  • Per voice: 2 DCOs, 1 resonant VCF, 1 dynamic VCA
  • Static high-pass filter
  • 1 envelope, 1 LFO
  • VCA switchable to Gate mode
  • Oscillator waveforms: square, sawtooth, pulse
  • Second oscillator also generates noise
  • Hard Sync and Metal (cross-modulation)
  • Early MIDI implementation (Omni mode only)
  • Limited MIDI CC support (modulation, sustain)
  • Tape data storage
  • External programmer support (PG-200)
