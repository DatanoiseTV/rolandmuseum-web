Boss PN-2 Tremolo/Pan

Effects Pedal Year: 1990

Description

The outstanding features of the PN-2 are an automatic panning function, which continuously changes the balance of sounds within their sound fields, and a tremolo effect that simulates the sound of classic guitar amplifiers. The stereo input of the PN-2 allows any configuration with other stereo effect devices or instruments such as keyboards equipped with stereo outputs. When using mono instruments, the panning effect of the PN-2 simulates stereo effects. Both panning and tremolo effects can be modulated by triangle and square waves, providing a total of four effect possibilities. In conjunction with triangle wave modulation, a natural tremolo effect is created, as used in traditional rock, for example. If, on the other hand, the panning effect is modulated with square waves, sound effects are created that are particularly suitable for electric piano and vibraphone voices. The modulation rate of the low-frequency oscillator (LFO) can be read on an LED display.

Features

  • Automatic panning function
  • Tremolo effect (simulates classic guitar amplifiers)
  • Stereo input
  • Mono instrument stereo simulation
  • Panning and tremolo modifiable by triangle and square waves (4 effect types)
  • LED display for LFO modulation rate
  • Power supply: 9V battery or AC adapter (PSA series)
  • Power consumption: 9V, 20 mA
  • S/N ratio: -90 dBm or less (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance A/B: 1 MOhm (Mono 500 kOhm)
  • Output load impedance A/B: over 10 kOhm
  • LFO Rate: 130 ms to 14 s
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 400 g
