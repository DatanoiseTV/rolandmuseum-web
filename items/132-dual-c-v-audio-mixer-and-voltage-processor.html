Roland 132 Dual C/V Audio Mixer and Voltage Processor

Synthesizer Module Year: 1979

Description

The Dual CV/Audio Mixer and Processor Module 132 is designed for the System-100M. It features two independent 4-channel mixers with both direct and inverted outputs. This module can be used to mix either audio signals or control voltages. Two of its channels are equipped with positive and negative fixed voltages, which can also be utilized as standard mixer channels via switchable jacks. A red LED indicates overload (distortion) at the output. The integrated voltage processor consists of two sections, providing a positive and a negative variable voltage. The mixer's frequency range extends from DC voltage up to 40 kHz AC voltage. With a signal-to-noise ratio exceeding 90 dB, this module delivers top-tier studio quality.

Features

  • Dual CV/Audio Mixer and Voltage Processor module
  • Designed for Roland System-100M
  • Two independent 4-channel mixers
  • Direct and inverted outputs for each mixer
  • Mixes audio signals or control voltages
  • Two channels with switchable positive and negative fixed voltages
  • Red LED overload indicator
  • Voltage processor with positive and negative variable voltage sections
  • Frequency range: DC to 40 kHz AC
  • Signal-to-noise ratio: > 90 dB (studio quality)
  • Powered via 190, 191, or 191J module enclosures
