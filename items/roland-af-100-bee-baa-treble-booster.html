Roland AF-100 'Bee Baa' Treble Booster

Compact FX Year: 1972

Description

The Roland AF-100 'Bee Baa' is a floor effect pedal for guitar housed in a sturdy metal casing. It is a combined treble booster and fuzz effect. A switch on the top allows switching between both effects. The 'FUZZ TONE SELECT' switch to its left changes the timbre of the fuzz effect. The switch on the right toggles between the effect and normal signal. Unusually, the jacks and controls are located on the narrow rear side. On the far right is the mono jack input labeled 'TO GUITAR', three controls for the fuzz: SUSTAIN, TONE, and VOLUME, the 'ADJ.' control for the booster, and on the left, the jack output labeled 'TO AMP'. Turning the Fuzz SUSTAIN control all the way to the left (OFF position) audibly deactivates the fuzz. To change the battery, loosen the two rear-side screws and lift the top of the housing. The silver casing was also used for the AG-5 'Funny Cat'. According to company founder Ikutaro Kakehashi, the name 'Bee Baa' is understood by listening to the sound. The AF-100 was manufactured by the Roland Music Engineering Group, or Roland MEG, which was later renamed 'Boss'.

Features

  • Floor effect pedal for guitar in sturdy metal casing
  • Combined Treble Booster and Fuzz effect
  • Top-mounted switch for toggling between effects
  • Fuzz Tone Select switch for changing fuzz timbre
  • Switch to toggle between effect and normal signal
  • Jacks and controls located on the narrow rear panel
  • Mono jack input 'TO GUITAR'
  • Three Fuzz controls: SUSTAIN, TONE, and VOLUME
  • Booster control 'ADJ.'
  • Jack output 'TO AMP'
  • Fuzz SUSTAIN control can deactivate fuzz when turned to OFF
  • Battery replacement by loosening two screws and tilting up the housing top
  • Battery operated (1 x 9V block (6LR61))
  • Dimensions: 230 x 65 x 165 mm
  • Weight: 1.05 kg
