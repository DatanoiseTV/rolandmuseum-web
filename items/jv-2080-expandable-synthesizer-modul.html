Roland JV-2080 Expandable Synthesizer Modul

Synthesizer Module Year: 1997

Description

The JV-2080 is a 64-voice, 16-part multitimbral rack synthesizer without a keyboard, whose sound library can be expanded with eight SR-JV80 series wave expansion boards. It occupies 2U in a 19" rack format and offers 3 (Insert) MFX in addition to Chorus and Reverb as effects. A sound program (Patch) consists of up to 4 Tones, two of which can be combined via one of 10 structures. A Tone consists of a PCM oscillator, a 12dB multi-mode filter, a dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, and 2 LFOs. Depending on the chosen structure, booster and ring modulator can be added, and with identical settings of two filters, a quality of 24dB can be achieved. Because Tones can be limited in velocity and note range, split, layer, and crossover sounds are possible within a single patch. The ROM sample library is identical to that of the JV-1080. The combination of up to 15 parts (with patches) and a rhythm set is managed by a Performance, with MIDI channels freely selectable for each individual part.

Features

  • 64-voice polyphony
  • 16-part multitimbral
  • Rack synthesizer module, no keyboard
  • Sound library expandable with eight SR-JV80 series wave expansion boards
  • 2U, 19" rack format
  • 3 Insert Multi-Effects (MFX)
  • Chorus and Reverb effects
  • Patches comprise up to 4 Tones
  • Tones combinable via 10 structures
  • Each Tone: PCM oscillator, 12dB multi-mode filter, dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, 2 LFOs
  • Booster and Ring Modulator optional based on structure
  • 24dB filter quality with identical settings of two filters
  • Split, layer, and crossover sounds possible within a patch (Tones limited by velocity/note range)
  • ROM sample library identical to JV-1080
  • Performance mode manages up to 15 parts and one rhythm set
  • Freely assignable MIDI channels per part
  • Dimensions: 482 x 88 x 281 mm
  • Weight: 4.9 kg
  • Power consumption: 13W
  • Integrated power supply, IEC power cable
