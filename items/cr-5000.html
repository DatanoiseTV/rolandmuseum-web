Roland CR-5000

Drum Machine Year: 1981

Description

The Roland ´CompuRhythm CR-5000 Micro Computer Controlled Rhythm Machine´ is an analog rhythm machine with fixed, pre-programmed rhythms housed in a console-style casing. The front part of the casing is sloped and contains the controls for rhythm selection: With three group and 8 selection buttons, one of 24 basic rhythm patterns can be chosen. A rotary switch allows selecting one of eight fill-patterns and another determines whether the fill-pattern is played at all, or every 2, 4, 8, 12, or 16 measures. Another button serves to manually trigger the fill-pattern. With 6 additional buttons, a cymbal (quarter or 8th notes), a hi-hat (eighth or 16th notes), open hi-hat (16th notes) and/or conga can be added. A CRASH button adds an analog crash cymbal sound after a fill or intro. START/STOP buttons, a tempo control (40 to 320 BPM) and a tempo-syncing ´pilot lamp´ (LED) as well as a SHUFFLE button are also located on the slanted main control panel. Behind the slanted main control surface are the power switch and 7 volume controls: Five small knobs adjust the mix ratio of 1. Bass, 2. Tom, Rim, Snare, 3. Conga, 4. Claves and Cowbell, 5. Cymbal and Hi-Hat. Two larger controls are for adjusting ACCENT (strength of accents) and overall volume. On the rear, there is a mono audio output (OUTPUT in mono!), 4 button connections (REGISTER, INTRO/FILL, RESTART, START/STOP) and a trigger output, whose impulses can be selected via a slide switch between Accent, 8th or 16th notes. The CR-5000 can only be used as a master device. The CR-5000 does not allow programming custom rhythm patterns; for that, the CR-8000 was available concurrently, which is nearly identical except for the user rhythms and programming functions.

Features

  • Analog rhythm machine
  • Fixed, pre-programmed rhythms
  • 24 basic rhythm patterns
  • 8 fill-patterns (selectable frequency: every 2, 4, 8, 12, or 16 measures)
  • Manual fill-pattern trigger
  • Individual instrument additions (Cymbal, Hi-Hat, Open Hi-Hat, Conga)
  • Analog Crash Cymbal sound
  • Tempo control (40-320 BPM)
  • Shuffle function
  • 7 volume controls for instrument mixing
  • Accent control
  • Overall volume control
  • Mono audio output
  • 4 external button connections (Register, Intro/Fill, Restart, Start/Stop)
  • Trigger output (Accent, 8th, or 16th note selectable)
  • Master synchronization capability
