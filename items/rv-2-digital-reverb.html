RV-2 Digital Reverb

Compact FX Year: 1986

Description

The RV-2, the first digital reverb in a foot pedal, offers the same professional sound quality typically found only in larger rack units. Its compact design makes the RV-2 particularly suitable for guitarists. With BOSS's developed DSP (Digital Signal Processing) technology in the RV-2's highly integrated components, it's possible to encapsulate the spatial ambiance of large halls down to small clubs in such a compact device. Six reverb modes - Room, two different Halls, Plate, Gated Reverb, and Panning Delay - are available for creating various effects. An adjustable reverb time of 0.2 - 10 seconds allows the player to choose the exact desired effect. A built-in pre-equalizer allows for suppressing lows and emphasizing highs to achieve a bright sound, or creating a rich, warm sound with deep bass and a dense mid-range. The RV-2 can produce the most popular reverb effects with superior sound quality and low noise. Stereo output jacks are provided, allowing you to enrich the reverb sounds with additional spatiality or take advantage of the panning effects in delay mode.

Features

  • First digital reverb in a foot pedal
  • Professional sound quality
  • Compact design
  • BOSS DSP technology
  • 6 Reverb Modes: Room, Hall 1, Hall 2, Plate, Gated Reverb, Panning Delay
  • Adjustable Reverb Time: 0.2 - 10 seconds
  • Built-in Pre-Equalizer (for tone shaping)
  • Low noise
  • Stereo output jacks
  • Power supply: 9V battery or AC adapter (PSA series)
  • Power consumption: 9V, 130 mA
  • Frequency response (direct): 10 Hz to 30 kHz ±1 dB
  • Frequency response (effect): 30 Hz to 10 kHz +1 dB, -3 dB
  • Sampling rate: 31.25 kHz
  • Signal-to-noise ratio: -85 dBm or less (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 30 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 420 g
