Boss BF-2 Flanger

Generated Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1982

Description

The BOSS BF-2 Flanger offers quiet, clean operation and full functional flexibility, and has all the advantages of the BOSS compact format. The BF-2 contains a special BBD circuit (bucket-brigade memory) that greatly reduces the noise associated with most electronic flangers. The controls of the BF-2 are Manual, Depth, Rate and Resonance. The Manual control determines the delay time of the sound, the control range is 1 to 13 ms. By turning the Manual control all the way to the left, the BF-2 has the effect of a non-sweep filter with adjustable equalization and ambient tonality. The Depth control sets the sweep range when the Manual control is in the mine position. The sweep speed can be adjusted with the Rate control from 100 ms to 16 s. With the Resonance control, the flange effect can be intensified by recreating the signal. The input impedance of the BF-2 of 470 kOhm makes it ideally suited for guitar, bass, keyboards as well as mixing on stage and in the studio. Combined with unsurpassed quality and flexibility, the BF-2 is one of the most versatile effect devices on the market.

Features

  • Manual control (Delay Time: 1-13ms)
  • Depth control
  • Rate control (100ms-16s)
  • Resonance control
  • BBD circuit for reduced noise
  • Input Impedance: 470kOhm
  • Suitable for guitar, bass, keyboards
