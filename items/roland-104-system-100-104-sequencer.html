Roland 104 System 100, 104 Sequencer

Sequencer Year: 1976

Description

The Roland 104 is an analog step sequencer for the System-100. It features two channels with up to 12 steps each, adjustable step count, and selectable voltage ranges (2.5V, 5V, 10V). Gate time and clock rate are globally adjustable. This sequencer is notable for its directly adjustable control voltages for each step.

Features

  • Analog Step Sequencer
  • Two channels with up to 12 steps each
  • Adjustable step count
  • Selectable voltage ranges (2.5V, 5V, 10V)
  • Adjustable gate time and clock rate
