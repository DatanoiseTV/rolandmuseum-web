AMDEK TAK-100

Tuning Amp Kit Year: 1982

Description

An incredibly small tuner with a headphone jack and input for a mini amplifier. From the 1982 Amdek catalog: AMDEK brings double the fun! The joy of assembly and pride in one's own achievement, and after assembly, the pleasure and enjoyment of a high-quality device that does not shy away from professional comparisons. AMDEK thus offers musicians many, perhaps previously unrecognized, opportunities to engage with their music and the devices used for it. Even if you haven't seen the inside of an effects unit before, you can still assemble an AMDEK device. The selection will be easy for you. The program includes a whole range, from a small metronome and tuner to a programmable rhythm machine and percussion synthesizer. We want to turn passively buying customers into active musicians who know more about their equipment than just the type designation and aren't stumped by the slightest technical problem. Musicians who know and appreciate the inner workings of their electronics. Musicians who not only enjoy playing but also building their electronic devices. The excellent sound and high-quality components ensure reliability and professional performance. All components are computer-tested, and a professional design with a durable metal housing and pre-drilled holes for controls and switches completes the package. Clear assembly instructions make building easy, and some devices offer modification options.

Features

  • Assembly process: 22 steps
  • Reference oscillator accuracy: ", " ± 0.035 cent
  • Amp. Output level: 200 mW
  • Headphone output: 100 mW x 2 (16 Ohm)
  • Max. Input level: -20 dB (1 kHz)
  • Input impedance: 50 kOhm
  • Consumption current: 8.5 mA (with no Signal)
  • Controls: Mode, Volume
  • Modes: AMP, 220, 440, 880 (Hz)
  • Terminals: Input, Output, External Power source
  • Power source: 006P battery or external Power source
  • Dimensions: 68 (W) x 55 (H) x 121 (D) mm (2.68" x 2.17" x 4.76")
  • Weight: 385 g (0.85 Ibs.)
  • Modifiable points: 1
  • Professional design with metal housing
  • High-quality, tested components
  • Clear and easy-to-follow assembly instructions
