Rodgers MR-200 Song Player/Recorder

Sequencer Year: 2004

Description

SMF recorder/player, MIDI sequencer with a 3.5" floppy disk drive. It has 16 tracks, each track is assigned a fixed MIDI channel number, and each track can be output from MIDI OUT 1 or MIDI OUT 2 or not at all. Repeat and overall transposition are adjustable; there are no other editing functions. The MR-200 can be a MIDI clock master or work as a clock slave. The design is reminiscent of its ancestor, the PR-100.

Features

  • SMF Recorder/Player
  • MIDI Sequencer
  • 3.5" Floppy Disk Drive
  • 16 Tracks
  • MIDI Clock Master/Slave
