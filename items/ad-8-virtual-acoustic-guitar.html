AD-8 Virtual Acoustic Guitar

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 2004

Description

The BOSS AD-8 offers two main features: it can transform the harsh, metallic sound of piezo pickups into a warm, round sound similar to microphone miking. Additionally, the AD-8 provides modeling of six of the world's most famous and expensive acoustic guitars – without special pickups! The BODY knob allows mixing the original sound with the guitar simulation: the piezo pickup of the real guitar provides the attack, while the guitar/microphone simulation provides the warm, full tone. To play pure guitar simulations, the BODY knob is turned to the middle position. Even the sound quality of the strings can be virtually altered by blending in the typical overtones of fresh strings. The AD-8 also offers further practical features: a preamplifier, 4-band parametric equalizer, 3 studio-quality reverb types, stereo DI box with ground lift (XLR and jack), four memory slots (switchable via footswitch), chromatic tuner (activatable via footswitch), and automatic feedback suppression (activatable via footswitch).

Features

  • Transforms piezo pickup sound to warm, mic-like tone
  • Modeling of six famous acoustic guitars (Martin, Gibson, Guild, etc.)
  • Blend control for original piezo tone and mic/guitar modeling
  • Virtually modifiable string sound quality (fresh string overtones)
  • Preamplifier
  • 4-band parametric equalizer
  • 3 types of high-quality reverb, specifically for acoustic guitar
  • Stereo DI box (2x XLR and 2x jack) with Ground Lift
  • Frequency-corrected output for electric guitar amplifiers
  • Chromatic tuner (footswitch activatable)
  • Automatic feedback suppression (footswitch activatable)
  • 4 memory slots
