TU-2 Tuner

Utility Year: 1998

Description

The TU-2 is a chromatic tuner in a compact pedal enclosure. The tuning range extends from C0 to C8 with an adjustable reference pitch from 438 to 445 Hz. Tuning accuracy is displayed via LEDs, and the fundamental note is shown on a 7-segment display. No microphone is built in for tuning acoustic instruments, but the TU-2 can, if a PSA power supply is connected, power other compact pedals.

Features

  • Chromatic tuner
  • Compact pedal enclosure
  • Tuning range: C0 to C8
  • Adjustable reference pitch: 438 to 445 Hz
  • LED display for tuning accuracy
  • 7-segment display for fundamental note
  • No built-in microphone
  • Can power other compact pedals (with PSA power supply)
  • Power consumption/supply: 55mA, PSA power supply
  • Dimensions: 70 x 55 x 125 mm
  • Weight: 0.42 kg
