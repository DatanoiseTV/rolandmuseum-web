Roland 104 System 100, 104 Sequenzer

Analog Sequencer Year: 1976

Description

The analog step sequencer 104 for the System-100 has two channels with a maximum of 12 steps, whose number of steps is adjustable. These channels can run sequentially or be output in parallel to control two synthesizer voices. The voltage range adjustable by the controls can be selected per channel between 2.5V, 5V, and 10V. Gate time and clock rate (tempo) are globally adjustable. The individual stages are referred to as steps. This sequencer initiated Roland's short series of sequencers with directly adjustable control voltages for individual steps, essentially the series of step sequencers. At the same time, the MC-8 was available, a sequencer whose steps were only programmable, not adjustable via controls.

Features

  • Analog step sequencer
  • Two channels with up to 12 steps each
  • Adjustable number of steps per channel
  • Channels can run sequentially or in parallel
  • Selectable voltage range per channel: 2.5V, 5V, or 10V
  • Globally adjustable gate time and clock rate (tempo)
  • Part of the Roland System-100 modular synthesizer
  • Dimensions: 280 x 145 x 355 mm
  • Weight: 5 kg
  • Internal power supply
