Roland G-33

Guitar Synthesizer Controller Bass Guitar Year: 1980

Description

The G-33 is a controller bass for the Roland GR-33B bass guitar synthesizer (with a 24-pin connector) and features the body shape of a typical Fender bass. In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal from the special pickup, which outputs the signals of the four strings separately and can thus control GR synthesizers, the bass also has a normal bass output where the sound of the single-coil pickup with double magnets is output. Thus, the G-33 can also be used as a regular bass. The bass has a maple neck glued to an ash body and was manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (specifically by the supplier Fuji Gen Gakki); a left-handed model, the G-33LH, was also available. Sound control is provided by three larger, gold-colored knobs and four smaller, black (plastic) knobs: Master Volume, Cutoff Frequency, and Balance between bass and synthesizer levels (gold), separate controls for Bass and Treble, and controls for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato intensity. A three-position switch located between the Resonance and Vibrato knobs toggles between bass, synthesizer, or both signals.

Features

  • Controller bass for Roland GR-33B bass guitar synthesizer
  • 24-pin connector for synthesizer control
  • Special pickup for separate string signals
  • Normal bass output for single-coil pickup with double magnets
  • Can be used as a regular bass
  • Maple neck, ash body
  • Manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (Fuji Gen Gakki)
  • Left-handed model G-33LH available
  • Controls: Master Volume, Cutoff Frequency, Balance (Bass/Synth Level)
  • Separate controls for Bass and Treble
  • Controls for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato Intensity
  • Three-position switch: Bass signal, Synthesizer signal, or both
  • Production period: 1980-1984
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 342 x 1182 x 66 mm
  • Weight: 4.4 kg
  • No power supply/cable needed
