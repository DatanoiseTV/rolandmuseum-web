Boss MZ-2 Digital Metalizer

Guitar/Bass Effect Pedal Year: 1990

Description

The MZ-2 is a stereo distortion pedal specifically developed for heavy metal and hard rock genres. It effortlessly creates complex effects that previously required significant effort and multiple expensive devices. It features six different operational modes that generate effects ranging from harsh and spatial distortions to short delays with selective filters. The Single (SGL) mode provides unembellished distortion, similar to a turbo distortion. For more complex and spatial distortions, three Doubling (DOUB I-III) modes combine digital delay with the distortion sound. These Doubling settings sound even more expansive when the MZ-2's stereo outputs are connected to two amplifiers. Additionally, two Chorus (CHO I, II) modes generate a digital chorus effect alongside the distortion for a full and spacious sound. The MZ-2 is an ideal pedal for musicians seeking hard and heavy rock tones.

Features

  • Stereo distortion pedal
  • Designed for Heavy Metal and Hard Rock
  • Six operational modes
  • Single (SGL) mode for unembellished distortion (similar to Turbo Distortion)
  • Three Doubling (DOUB I-III) modes combining digital delay with distortion
  • Two Chorus (CHO I, II) modes for distortion with digital chorus
  • Stereo outputs for enhanced spatial sound when connected to two amplifiers
  • Power supply: 9V battery or optional AC adapter (PSA Series)
  • Current draw: 70 mA
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
