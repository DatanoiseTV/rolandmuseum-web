Roland GR-100

Guitar Effects Unit Year: 1981

Description

The Roland GR-100 is an electronic effects unit for guitars with a 24-pin connection. Unlike the Roland GR-300, which is a true guitar synthesizer, the GR-100 is essentially a hexaphonic fuzz box with a subsequent filter and a chorus. Roland wisely did not call the GR-100 a 'Guitar Synthesizer' but rather 'Electronic Guitar'. Three built-in footswitches are used to turn the chorus or filter modulation on/off and to invert the filter envelope. Filter modulation can be adjusted with Peak and Sens (Sensitivity) controls, one control determines chorus strength (Sens), and another controls the vibrato rate. On the rear, there are connections for a VCF control pedal and three audio outputs: Chorus, Mix, and Guitar.

Features

  • 24-pin guitar connection
  • Hexaphonic fuzz effect
  • Built-in filter
  • Chorus effect
  • Three footswitches for chorus on/off, filter modulation on/off, and filter envelope inversion
  • Controls for filter Peak and Sensitivity
  • Control for chorus strength
  • Control for vibrato rate
  • Connections for VCF control pedal
  • Three audio outputs (Chorus, Mix, Guitar)
  • Dimensions: 300 x 93 x 250 mm
  • Weight: 3.1 kg
  • Built-in power supply
  • Included accessories: C-24D 24-pin cable, PJ-1 audio cable (2 x 2.5m)
