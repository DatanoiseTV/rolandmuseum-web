Roland Roland PK-25

MIDI Pedalboard Year: 2000

Description

The PK-25 full pedal is a MIDI keyboard for the feet without its own sounds, featuring 25 pedals. The pedal sends MIDI notes in the fixed range of note numbers 36 to 60 (C2 to C4) on a fixed MIDI channel 2. The pedal is not velocity-sensitive and sends notes with a fixed velocity of 120. The PK-25 includes an expression pedal with two side buttons, as well as an additional HOLD button. The buttons output MIDI CC messages with fixed CC numbers (18, 19, and 64), and the expression pedal is fixed to Expression (CC#11). For each of these controls, a separate output jack is also provided to connect the buttons or pedal to the pedal inputs of a master keyboard or organ. If a cable is inserted into one of the jacks, the corresponding MIDI message is no longer sent. Via a special PK connection, the PK-25 can be linked to a VK-77, allowing all MIDI communication and power supply for the PK-25 to be handled through it.
