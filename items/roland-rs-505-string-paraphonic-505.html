Roland RS-505 String Paraphonic 505

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1978

Description

49-key keyboard with 3 sound generation sections and two keyboard zones (Upper and Lower), split point at middle C. Bass section: monophonic synthesizer with 3 switchable sounds (Contra Bass 16, Tuba 8, Cello 8) and adjustable AR envelope. Polyphonic Synthesizer section: fixed registers (Bass 16, Bass 8, Lower 8, Lower 4, Upper 8, Upper 4), filter with adjustable cutoff frequency and resonance, modulation by LFO or ADSR envelope. Polyphonic String section: per zone (Upper/Lower), slider for adjusting the mix ratio of 8' and 4' strings and a rocker switch for on/off. Effects include Ensemble, Attack, Vibrato, Autobend, and more. Outputs: Stereo, Synth-Bass, Trigger, Gate. Inputs: Ext IN, Pitch, Sustain, VCF.
