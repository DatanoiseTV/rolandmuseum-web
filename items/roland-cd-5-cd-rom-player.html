Roland CD-5 CD-ROM Player

Other Year: 1989

Description

This CD-ROM drive with SCSI connection is designed for Roland Samplers. It is compatible with the Apple standard of the early 90s and was shipped with the LCD-1 CD-ROM, which contains sounds in S-550 format. CD-ROM drives were extremely rare at the time.

Features

  • CD-ROM drive with SCSI connection
  • Compatible with Apple standard of the early 90s
  • Designed for Roland Samplers (W-30, S-550, S-770)
  • Shipped with LCD-1 CD-ROM containing S-550 format sounds
  • Rare technology for its time
