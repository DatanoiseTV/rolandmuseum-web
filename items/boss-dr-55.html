Boss DR-55

Drum Machine / Rhythm Year: 1979

Description

The Boss Dr. Rhythm DR-55 is a small, analog, and programmable drum machine designed for battery operation. It was the first rhythm machine without preset rhythms. Users could program six patterns with 16 steps and two with 12 steps (sixteenth notes), each with a variation. Programmable instruments included bass drum, snare, rim, and accent. Sliders controlled hi-hat settings (off, eighth notes, sixteenth notes), variation selection (A, B, or both alternating), volume, tempo, tone, and accent level. A large rotary switch selects the rhythm pattern, while buttons or a footswitch (connected to the FOOT SW jack) start and stop playback. It features a mono output and two sync jacks (DBS sends a pulse for each step; CSQ for each programmed accent).

Features

  • Analog
  • Programmable
  • 6 Patterns (16 steps), 2 Patterns (12 steps)
  • Battery-powered
  • Bass Drum, Snare, Rim, Accent
  • Hi-hat control (off, 8th, 16th)
  • Variation A/B selection
  • Volume, Tempo, Tone, Accent controls
  • Foot Switch input
  • DBS/CSQ sync output
