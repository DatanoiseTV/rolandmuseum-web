SH-09 Monophonic Synthesizer

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1978

Description

The SH-09 is a compact, monophonic analog synthesizer with 32 (non-velocity-sensitive) keys. One VCO can alternatively produce square, pulse, sawtooth, or noise waveforms, with a sub-oscillator mixable in one or two octaves lower. The sound is shaped by a dynamic 24dB low-pass filter with resonance (up to self-oscillation) and a dynamic amplifier, which share a single ADSR envelope. The VCA can also be controlled by Gate or Hold. For modulation, an LFO, portamento, and pitch bend lever are available. There are no audio effects, but CV/Gate inputs and outputs, as well as an external audio input, are present.

Features

  • Compact, monophonic analog synthesizer
  • 32 non-velocity-sensitive keys
  • 1 VCO (square, pulse, sawtooth, noise waveforms)
  • Sub-oscillator (1 or 2 octaves lower)
  • Dynamic 24dB low-pass filter with resonance (up to self-oscillation)
  • Dynamic amplifier
  • Shared ADSR envelope for filter and VCA
  • VCA controllable by Gate or Hold
  • LFO for modulation
  • Portamento
  • Pitch bend lever
  • No built-in audio effects
  • CV/Gate inputs and outputs
  • External audio input
