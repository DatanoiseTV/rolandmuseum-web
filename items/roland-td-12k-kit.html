Roland TD-12K Kit

Drum Sets Year: 2005

Description

The TD-12K is an almost complete electronic drum set, comprising the TD-12 trigger module, KD-85 kick trigger, PD-105 snare trigger, three PD-85 tom triggers, two CY-12R/C cymbal pads, and a VH-11 Hi-Hat. For a fully playable setup, additional components like a hi-hat stand, kick pedal, drum stool, and a drum rack/stands are required. Initially, the TD-12K was supplied with black pads, with later versions (TD-12K-WT) featuring white pads, and the black version subsequently named TD-12K-BK.

Features

  • Includes TD-12 module, VH-11 V-HiHat, KD-85 Kick Trigger (Mesh), PD-105 Snare Pad (Dual, Mesh), 3x PD-85 Tom Pads (Dual, Mesh), 2x CY-12R/C Cymbal Pads
  • Approximate dimensions (setup): 1450 x 1500 x 1200 mm
  • Integrated power supply with IEC power cable
  • Available with black (TD-12K-BK) or white (TD-12K-WT) pads
  • Requires additional hi-hat stand, kick pedal, drum stool, and drum stand/rack
