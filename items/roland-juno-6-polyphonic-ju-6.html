Roland JUNO-6 Polyphonic JU-6

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1982

Description

The Juno-6 is a 6-voice analog synthesizer with 61 keys and an arpeggiator. Each voice features an oscillator (DCO), high-pass filter, dynamic low-pass filter, dynamic amplifier, and an envelope generator. Sound programs are not storable. The connections are straightforward: headphone and main outputs (L/Mono, R) alongside inputs for sustain pedal, arpeggio clock, and filter cutoff frequency control. A rear-panel knob for adjusting the master tuning is also present.
