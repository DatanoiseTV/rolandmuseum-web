Roland JV-1000

Digital Synthesizer Workstation Year: 1993

Description

The JV-1000 is a 28-voice, 8-part multitimbral digital synthesizer with 76 velocity-sensitive keys, aftertouch, and a sequencer. The JV-1000 has a slot for a Voice Expansion Board, which can add 28 voices with specialized (GM or JV-80) sounds. The sound engine of the JV-1000 is identical to that of the JV-90; a patch (sound program) consists of up to 4 Tones, two of which can be combined via one of 10 structures. A Tone consists of a PCM oscillator, a 12dB multi-mode filter, a dynamic amplifier, 3 envelopes, and 2 LFOs. Depending on the chosen structure, a booster and ring modulator can also be added, and with identical settings for two filters, a quality of 24dB can be achieved. By allowing Tones to be limited by velocity and note range, split, layer, and crossover sounds are possible within a single patch. The combination of up to 7 parts (with patches) and one rhythm set is managed by a Performance. The JV-1000's sound library can be expanded with a Wave Expansion Board and/or an SO-PCM1 card, providing additional PCM samples for the oscillators. The JV-1000 has a built-in 8-track sequencer with a floppy drive and a second display (!), which matches the functionality of the MC-50. There are 8 internal songs available, and each track can record data from all 16 MIDI channels. One tempo track and a pattern-oriented rhythm track complete the sequencer.

Features

  • 28-voice polyphony (expandable by 28 voices with Voice Expansion Board)
  • 8-part multitimbral
  • 76 velocity-sensitive keys with aftertouch
  • Digital sound synthesis (PCM oscillator)
  • 12dB multi-mode filter (can achieve 24dB with two combined filters)
  • 3 envelopes, 2 LFOs per tone
  • Booster and ring modulator options
  • Split, layer, and crossover capabilities within patches
  • Expandable sound library with Wave Expansion Boards or SO-PCM1 cards
  • Built-in 8-track sequencer with floppy drive
  • Second display for sequencer
  • 8 internal songs
  • Each track records data from all 16 MIDI channels
  • Tempo track
  • Pattern-oriented rhythm track
  • MIDI In/Out/Thru
