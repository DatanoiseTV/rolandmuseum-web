Roland Roland DB-770

Digital Audio Booster Year: 1993

Description

The DB-770 is a 9.5" device with a coaxial S/P-DIF input and output, between which a digital audio signal can be boosted by +3, +6, or +x dB. The DB-770 has no other functions.

Features

  • 9.5" form factor
  • Coaxial S/P-DIF input
  • Coaxial S/P-DIF output
  • Digital audio signal boosting: +3 dB, +6 dB, or variable (+x dB)
