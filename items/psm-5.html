Boss PSM-5

Power Supplies Year: 1990

Description

The PSM-5 is a stabilized power supply that can power up to five Boss 9V effects pedals (or other compatible devices). It includes a PSA-series AC adapter. A parallel DC cable with five outputs is included. The PSM-5 can also act as a master switch. Connect the Send jack to the first effect in the chain, then connect each effect's output to the next effect's input, and finally connect the last effect's output to the Return jack. Connect the guitar to the Guitar jack and the amplifier to the Amp jack. This creates two separate signal paths, one through the effects and one bypassing them. A footswitch toggles between the two paths. An LED indicates when the effects loop is active.

Features

  • Powers up to 5 Boss 9V pedals
  • Stabilized power supply
  • Master switch functionality
  • LED indicator
  • PSA-series AC adapter included
