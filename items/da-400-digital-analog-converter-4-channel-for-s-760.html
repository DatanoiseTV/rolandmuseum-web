DA-400 Digital-Analog Converter, 4-channel for S-760

Utility Year: 1995

Description

The DA-400 is a 4-channel Digital/Analog Converter in a 9.5" format. It features 2 digital S/P-DIF inputs (one switchable between optical and coaxial, the other coaxial only) and 4 jack outputs.

Features

  • 4-channel Digital/Analog Converter
  • 9.5" format
  • 2 digital S/P-DIF inputs (one optical/coaxial switchable, one coaxial only)
  • 4 jack outputs
  • Built-in power supply
  • Designed for use with Roland S-760 sampler
