Boss DSD-3

Generated Sampler Year: 1986

Description

The DSD-3, with its extremely favorable price/performance ratio, can be used either as a digital sampling device or as a digital delay device. It contains a 12-bit A/D/A converter, with which a frequency response of 40 Hz to 7 kHz is achieved. The DSD-3 increases your creative possibilities for both recording and live playing. The sampling time can be set between 200 and 800 ms. There are two operating modes - Rec/Play and Play Only. In Rec/Play mode, recording and replay occur simultaneously. With the feedback control set to maximum, it is possible to overdub another sample sound onto a previously recorded sound. In Play-Only mode, the DSD-3 plays back the stored sample sound that was recorded in Rec/Play mode. Playback of the recorded sample sound is possible by pressing the pedal or by supplying an external trigger signal to the DSD-3. With the time control, the pitch of the recorded sample sound can be changed during playback. In addition, the DSD-3 can be used as a digital delay device with a delay of 50 to 800 ms. The delay time is determined by selecting the S-mode (50 to 200 ms) or the L-mode (200 to 800 ms) and turning the time control. The delay time can also be controlled with external trigger signals.

Features

  • 12-bit A/D/A converter
  • 40 Hz to 7 kHz frequency response
  • 200-800ms sampling time
  • Rec/Play and Play Only modes
  • Digital delay (50-800ms)
  • External trigger input
  • Pitch control
