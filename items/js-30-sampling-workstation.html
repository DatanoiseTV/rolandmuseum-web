JS-30 Sampling Workstation

Sampler Year: 1997

Description

A new type of sampler for today's music. Phrase sampling is an indispensable part of dance and pop music production today. Now Roland introduces the JS-30 Sampling Workstation: A high-quality phrase sampling instrument for every musician and DJ at a very attractive price. With its thoughtful design and truly simple operation, even complex phrase sampling becomes child's play. You don't even have to be able to play an instrument. These features make the JS-30 the ideal instrument for club DJs, professional studios, and home studios. It opens up completely new, practically limitless possibilities for music creation. With a single JS-30, you immediately become a remix artist, no prior experience with musical instruments is necessary. Simply combine phrases as you like and compose new songs this way. This is how music is made today! The JS-30 was specifically developed for phrase sampling. It has a clear and logically structured control panel for the simplest real-time handling, distinguishing it pleasantly from conventional rackmount samplers. There is a dedicated button for each function, making it as easy as possible. The perfect sampler: The design of the JS-30 Sampling Workstation builds on Roland's extensive experience in building digital samplers. The excellent sound results from the use of sampler chips, which were already used in the Roland S-770 and S-760 digital samplers. The JS-30 offers practically the same sound quality as the S-series samplers with 16-bit sampling at 44.1kHz. Record, play, and mix "off the cuff": All you have to do to record samples is connect a CD player, a tape recorder, or a microphone and press the Start/Stop button. That's all! The sample is automatically assigned to one of the 12 keypads and is ready for playback. You don't have to worry about presets or assignments at all. In case you do want to intervene yourself, the JS-30 offers extensive editing options including Truncate and Loop. Double sample time: The internal memory has a sample time of 22.5 seconds at a sampling rate of 44.1kHz. With the useful Time x 2 function, the time can be extended to 45 seconds. By installing additional SIMMs, the sample time can be extended to a full 45 seconds (90 seconds with the Time x 2 function). Even more possibilities through the built-in SCSI port: The JS-30 has a SCSI port, so you can connect powerful data storage devices such as hard drives or MO (Magnetic Optical) drives. This gives you practically unlimited storage space. You can simply save your phrases and load them back for playback. In addition, a CD-ROM player can be connected to the SCSI port. The JS-30 is able to read CD-ROMs for the Roland S-Series samplers, so you can also use the huge CD-ROM libraries with first-class stereo samples. Versatile functions for music productions: Sample BPM setting: The tempo of a sample phrase can be easily set by entering the BPM (Beats Per Minute) value with the Ten Key Pad. If you don't know the exact value, you can also use the practical Tap-Tempo function. Master BPM: It is child's play to align different sample phrases with different tempos to form a chain: set the Master BPM value with the Ten Key Pad, and the sample phrases will automatically adjust to the tempo you specify. The original BPM values are retained for later use.

Features

  • Phrase Sampling Instrument
  • 12 Keypads for sample playback
  • High-quality 16-bit sampling at 44.1kHz (using S-770/S-760 sampler chips)
  • Easy recording from CD player, tape recorder, or microphone
  • Automatic sample assignment to keypads
  • Extensive editing options (Truncate, Loop)
  • Internal memory: 22.5 seconds at 44.1kHz
  • Time x 2 function: 45 seconds total sample time
  • Expandable with SIMMs to 45 seconds (90 seconds with Time x 2)
  • Built-in SCSI port for external storage (hard drives, MO drives)
  • SCSI port supports CD-ROM players, reads Roland S-Series sampler CD-ROMs
  • Sample BPM setting via Ten Key Pad or Tap-Tempo
  • Master BPM function for synchronizing different sample phrases
