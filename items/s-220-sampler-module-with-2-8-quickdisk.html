S-220 Sampler Module with 2.8" Quickdisk

Sampler Year: 1987

Description

The Roland S-220 'Digital Sampler' is a 16-voice sampler module in 19-inch rack format (2U height) with a built-in 2.8-inch floppy drive (Quick Disk format, or QD). The S-220 samples at 12-bit resolution and either 15 kHz or 30 kHz, but already features 16-bit A/D converters. The internal memory of 256KB is divided into 4 banks of 64KB each, allowing for a maximum total sample length of 4.4 seconds, or approximately 1.1 seconds per bank. Two banks can be combined for longer samples. Samples can be edited within the device, assigned a dynamic envelope, and looped; however, there is no dynamic filter. The S-220 can read Quick Disks from the S-10 and MKS-100, making it data-compatible with the L-100 series. The S-220 operates in MIDI Mono mode, where up to 8 consecutive channels can be used, or up to 4-part multitimbral in Poly mode, though voice distribution is not dynamic and must be pre-selected. In Poly mode, the note range can be limited per channel. Monitor and mouse operation, as seen on its 'bigger brothers' S-50 and S-330, is not provided; the S-220's front panel controls and two-line display are used for operation. In February 1987, the S-220 was announced under the name MKS-200, but all production models were called S-220.

Features

  • 16-voice digital sampler module
  • 19-inch rack format, 2U height
  • Built-in 2.8-inch Quick Disk (QD) floppy drive
  • 12-bit sampling resolution, 16-bit A/D converters
  • Sampling rates: 15 kHz or 30 kHz
  • 256 KB internal memory (4 banks x 64 KB)
  • Max total sample length: 4.4 seconds (approx. 1.1 seconds per bank)
  • Sample editing (dynamic envelope, looping)
  • No dynamic filter
  • Data compatible with S-10 and MKS-100 (L-100 series) Quick Disks
  • MIDI Mono mode (up to 8 consecutive channels)
  • 4-part multitimbral Poly mode (pre-selected voice distribution, note range limiting per channel)
  • Two-line display
  • Built-in power supply
