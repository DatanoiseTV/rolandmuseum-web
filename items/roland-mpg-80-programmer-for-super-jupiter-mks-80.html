Roland MPG-80 Programmer for SUPER JUPITER MKS-80

Synthesizer Addons Year: 1984

Description

The MPG-80 is a programmer for the MKS-80 sound module, designed for tabletop use or 19-inch rack mounting (occupying 4U). It features 31 sliders, 10 rotary knobs, 20 slide switches, and 3 rotary switches that correspond to the parameters of the MKS-80, allowing for quick and convenient sound creation with an 'analog' control surface. The MPG-80 has a MIDI IN, a MIDI THRU, and a 6-pin DIN Programmer OUT connection. The MPG-80 is connected to the MKS-80 via a 6-pin DIN cable, which transmits all data and simultaneously powers the MPG-80. A controlling keyboard or sequencer can be connected to the MPG-80's MIDI IN, and its data is merged with the data from the programmer and sent to the MKS-80. This is practical as it avoids having to switch the MKS-80 between MIDI control and programmer operation, which would otherwise deactivate its MIDI IN port. The MPG-80 sends its data, i.e., control movements, as MIDI SysEx messages. Only the MIDI data present at MIDI IN is forwarded via MIDI THRU.

Features

  • Programmer for Roland MKS-80 Super Jupiter sound module
  • Tabletop or 19-inch rack mountable (4U)
  • 31 sliders, 10 rotary knobs, 20 slide switches, 3 rotary switches for direct parameter control
  • Provides an 'analog' control surface for quick sound editing
  • MIDI IN, MIDI THRU, and 6-pin DIN Programmer OUT connections
  • Connects to MKS-80 via 6-pin DIN cable, supplying data and power
  • MIDI data from connected keyboard/sequencer is merged with programmer data and sent to MKS-80 (MIDI Merge function)
  • Sends control movements as MIDI SysEx messages
  • MIDI THRU only forwards MIDI IN data
  • Dimensions: 482 x 117 x 78 mm
  • Weight: 3 kg
  • Power via 6-pin DIN connection from MKS-80
