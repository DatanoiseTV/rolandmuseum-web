Roland TR-66 Rhythm Arranger 66

Drum Machine Year: 1975

Description

The Roland TR-66 Rhythm Arranger 66 is an analog drum machine with 9 preset rhythms selectable via push buttons and 18 additional rhythms accessible via rotary switch. Each rhythm has variations A and B. Rhythms from both the push-button group and the rotary switch group can be combined. A switch allows selection between variation A, B, or an alternating AB sequence (called Auto), hence the 'Arranger' moniker. It has a built-in power supply with a fixed power cord. The tempo is adjusted with a rotary knob; synchronization is not possible.

Features

  • 9 preset rhythms
  • 18 rhythms selectable via rotary switch
  • Variations A and B per rhythm
  • Combinable rhythm groups
  • Alternating AB sequence (Auto) mode
  • Tempo control
  • Mono output (high/low level)
  • Footswitch jack
