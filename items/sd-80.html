Edirol SD-80

MIDI Synthesizer Module Year: 2004

Description

The SD-80 is a 128-voice MIDI synthesizer module that, in addition to global effects like reverb and chorus, provides up to three insert effects (90 algorithms) simultaneously. For outputs, two pairs of Line Out and S/P DIF Out are available. The SD-80 has 32 parts, and when connected via USB to a PC or Macintosh, the additional MIDI interfaces provide access to another 32 MIDI channels. The sound library includes 30 drum kits and 1050 GM2/GS/XGlite compatible sounds (6 sound maps: Classical, Contemporary, Solo, Enhanced, Special1, Special2). The SD-80 is delivered with a highly powerful software editor and USB drivers for Windows Me/98/2000/XP Home/XP Professional as well as MacOS 8.5, 8.6, and 9.x.

Features

  • 128-voice MIDI synthesizer module
  • Global effects: Reverb, Chorus
  • Up to three simultaneous insert effects (90 algorithms)
  • Two pairs of Line Out
  • S/P DIF Out
  • 32 parts
  • USB connectivity for PC/Mac (additional 32 MIDI channels)
  • 30 drum kits
  • 1050 GM2/GS/XGlite compatible sounds
  • 6 sound maps (Classical, Contemporary, Solo, Enhanced, Special1, Special2)
  • Includes software editor and USB drivers for Windows and MacOS
