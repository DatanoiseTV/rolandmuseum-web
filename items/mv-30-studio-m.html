Roland MV-30 Studio M

Music Workstation Year: 1990

Description

The MV-30 combines a 30-voice, 8-part multitimbral sound engine with a 16-track sequencer in a desktop chassis. Its sound generation builds upon the U-20, enhanced with filters from the D-70. Sounds, called 'Timbres,' are based on 'Tones' shaped by dynamic resonant filters and dynamic amplitude, modulated by two envelopes and an LFO. Tones consist of one or two oscillators playing one or two assigned PCM waveforms, combined, split, or crossfaded by velocity. The MV-30 has two slots for PCM cards to expand its 220 preset Tones, compatible with U-series and D-70 cards. It has seven synth parts assignable to 128 Timbres and a rhythm part. The sequencer is song-oriented, with 20 patterns per song and 18 tracks (one for the mixer and one for tempo). Eight tracks are dedicated to internal parts, while eight others can record data from all 16 MIDI channels, output via MIDI or routed internally. It supports real-time and step recording, detailed event editing, and various data formats like SMF and its own. It can synchronize with tape via FSK.

Features

  • 30-voice, 8-part multitimbral
  • 16-track sequencer
  • RS-PCM sound engine
  • 2 PCM card slots (compatible with U-series and D-70)
  • Dynamic resonant filters
  • 2 envelopes and LFO per tone
  • Song-oriented sequencer with pattern support
  • SMF compatibility
  • FSK synchronization
