Roland G-808 Guitar for GR-300

Other Year: 1980

Description

The GS-808 is a controller guitar for Roland guitar synthesizers with a 24-pin connection, featuring a distinctive double-cutaway body shape. In addition to the 24-pin connection for the signal of the special "hexaphonic" pickup, which outputs the string signals separately for controlling GR synthesizers, the guitar also has a normal guitar output where the sound of the two built-in humbucker pickups is delivered. The G-808 can therefore also be used as a perfectly normal guitar. The G-808 features a neck-through design made of maple and mahogany, and a body of walnut and ash. It differs from the concurrently available G-303 only by its neck-through construction (instead of a set neck) and the wood used. The G-808 was manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (specifically by the supplier Fuji Gen Gakki) according to Roland's specifications. Sound control is provided by four gold (plastic) knobs for Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, and Balance between guitar and synthesizer levels, two black knobs for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato intensity, a three-stage mode switch, and a three-stage switch for toggling between guitar and synthesizer signals, or both.

Features

  • Controller guitar for Roland GR-300 guitar synthesizer
  • 24-pin connection for GR-synthesizer control
  • Special hexaphonic pickup for separate string signal output
  • Normal guitar output with two built-in humbucker pickups
  • Can be used as a regular guitar
  • Neck-through design (maple and mahogany neck)
  • Walnut and ash body
  • Manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (Fuji Gen Gakki)
  • Controls: Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, Balance (guitar/synth level)
  • Controls: Resonance (VCF), Vibrato intensity
  • Three-stage mode switch
  • Three-stage switch for Guitar, Synthesizer, or Both signals
  • Dimensions: 322 x 1020 x 66 mm
  • Weight: 3.7 kg
  • Includes guitar case
  • Recommended accessories: GR-100 Electronic Guitar Unit, GR-300 Polyphonic Guitar Synthesizer, US-2 24-Pin Switchbox, C-24B 24-Pin Cable (10m), C-24E 24-Pin Cable (1m)
