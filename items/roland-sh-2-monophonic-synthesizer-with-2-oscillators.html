Roland SH-2 Monophonic Synthesizer with 2 Oscillators

Analog Synthesizer Year: 1979

Description

The SH-2 is a compact, monophonic analog synthesizer with 37 (non-velocity-sensitive) keys. Both VCOs can be set to footages of 32, 16, 8, 4, and 2, and waveforms of sawtooth, square, and pulse. VCO-1 additionally offers sine and has a mixable sub-oscillator, while VCO-2, which can be detuned against VCO-1, also offers noise. The subsequent dynamic 24dB low-pass filter is equipped with controllable resonance up to self-oscillation and shares its ADSR envelope with the final VCA, which can also be controlled by Gate or Hold. Modulation options include an LFO, portamento, and the pitch bend lever. There are no audio effects, but CV/Gate inputs and outputs, as well as an external audio input, are present.
