Roland MC-500MKII

Sequencer Year: 1988

Description

The MC-500MKII is a song-oriented MIDI sequencer with two independent MIDI outputs and a 3.5" floppy drive. Its operating system is initially loaded from a diskette. It features a ten-key numeric keypad and other design elements reminiscent of a calculator. It includes 6 drive buttons, 7 function buttons, 2 cursor buttons, a value input wheel (Alpha-Dial), and 5 illuminated track buttons, along with a 2-line display (not backlit). It was shipped with S-MRC software, turning it into an 8-track sequencer capable of recording all 16 MIDI channels on each track. Additional features include a tempo track and a rhythm track for up to 240 rhythm patterns.

Features

  • Song-oriented MIDI sequencer
  • 2 independent MIDI outputs
  • 3.5" floppy drive
  • 8-track sequencer (with S-MRC)
  • 16 MIDI channels per track
  • Tempo and rhythm tracks
  • 240 rhythm patterns
