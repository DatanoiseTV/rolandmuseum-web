Roland MKB-300 MIDI Master Keyboard

Utility Year: 1984

Description

The MKB-300 is a 2-zone MIDI master keyboard with 76 weighted, velocity-sensitive keys and channel aftertouch. Each zone, called UPPER and LOWER, has two buttons to increase or decrease the MIDI channel, displayed on its respective 7-segment display, offering clear visibility. The MKB-300 can be operated in WHOLE, SPLIT, or DUAL mode. Program changes can be generated manually or stored as a patch along with the mode for each of the two zones, which are then sent upon recall. 128 patches can be saved internally or on an M-64C cartridge. The performance data is output by the MKB-300 via four parallel MIDI OUT jacks, with data arriving at the MIDI IN jack being merged. A MIDI THRU jack for exclusive forwarding of MIDI IN data is also available. The functions of the MKB-300 are identical to the MKB-1000, which, however, features 88 wooden keys. The MKB-300 and MKB-1000 were among the very first dedicated master keyboards ever and, along with modules MKS-10, MKS-30, and MKS-80, marked the beginning of the separation of MIDI data and sound generation.

Features

  • 2-zone MIDI master keyboard
  • 76 weighted, velocity-sensitive keys
  • Channel aftertouch
  • UPPER and LOWER zones with MIDI channel controls and 7-segment displays
  • WHOLE, SPLIT, or DUAL operating modes
  • 128 patch memory (internal or M-64C cartridge)
  • Four parallel MIDI OUT jacks
  • MIDI Merge function
  • MIDI THRU jack
  • Dimensions: 1251 x 115 x 431 mm
  • Weight: 18.5 kg
  • Built-in power supply
