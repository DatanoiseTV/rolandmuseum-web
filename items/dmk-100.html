AMDEK DMK-100

DIY Delay Effect Kit Year: 1982

Description

A delay device with good signal-to-noise ratio and a long delay time of up to 300 ms. Separately usable outputs for effect and direct signal provide interesting sound results. AMDEK products offer the joy of assembly and pride in one's own achievement, resulting in a high-quality device that stands up to professional comparisons.

Features

  • Controls: input level, delay time, intensity, delay volume
  • Terminals: Input, Output (2 channels: Dry+Effect, Effect)
  • Input level: Low: -50 dBm, Medium: -35 dBm, High: -15 dBm
  • Output level: High: -10 dBm, Low: -25 dBm
  • Max. Output level: -3 dBm
  • Delay time: 25 ms - 300 ms
  • Dimensions: 210 (W) x 80 (H) x 166 (D) mm
  • Weight: 1.75 kg
  • High-quality, computer-tested components (resistors, capacitors, ICs)
  • Professional metal housing design with precise drilling
  • LED indicator for operation status
  • Click-free on/off switch
  • Connection for optional AC adapter
