Phaser Kit (PHK-100)

Effect Year: 1982

Description

A clean phaser with "Resonance" control for tone color enhancement. AMDEK offers double the fun! The joy of assembly and pride in one's own achievement, and after assembly, the pleasure of a high-quality device that can stand up to professional comparisons. AMDEK provides musicians with many, perhaps previously unrecognized, opportunities to engage with their music and the equipment used. Even if you've never seen the inside of an effect unit, you can still assemble an AMDEK device. Musicians who enjoy not just playing, but also building their own electronic devices. The excellent sound: We know there are many kits that fulfill one point: the fun of assembly; but completely disregard another: the sound. And we are very confident here. An AMDEK device can compete with any other effect unit in terms of sound. This is due to our experience in building effect units and the high-quality components.

Features

  • Phaser kit (DIY)
  • Clean phaser
  • "Resonance" control for tone color enhancement
  • Controls: Rate, Depth, Resonance
  • Functions: Effect changeover switch, Effect indicator
  • Terminals: Input, Output, External Power source
  • Input impedance: 470 kOhm
  • Max. input level: -10 dBm
  • Output load impedance: Over 10 kOhm
  • Phase shift: 4 steps, 720°
  • LFO rate: 130 ms - 9s
  • Residual noise: 90 dBm
  • Power source: 006P battery or external power source
  • Consumption current: 11 mA (DC 9V)
  • Dimensions: 62 (W) x 56 (H) x 128 (D) mm
  • Robust metal housing with pre-drilled holes
  • Click-free On/Off switch
  • LED indicator for operating status
  • High-quality components
  • Modifiable
