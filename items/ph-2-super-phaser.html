PH-2 SUPER Phaser

Compact FX Year: 1986

Description

From a 1986 Boss catalog: The compact PH-2 features 12-stage filter circuits and custom ICs for producing deep, naturally phase-shifted sounds comparable to those of larger and much more expensive phase shifters. On stage or in the studio, with guitars or keyboards, the PH-2 generates all the phase-shifted sounds you need. The PH-2 has two operating modes, selectable via a switch. In Mode I, the PH-2 produces deep, soft sounds suitable for guitar or keyboard accompaniment. By fully activating the 12-stage filter circuits, the PH-2 produces sharp sounds that enrich your performance with a distinctive touch. The PH-2 has three controls. The Rate knob adjusts the LFO speed. The effect depth is determined with the Depth knob. The Resonance knob intensifies the phase-shift effect by feeding the already phase-shifted signal back through the circuits.

Features

  • 9V battery or ACA-series AC adapter power supply
  • Power consumption: 9V, 20 mA (max.)
  • 12-stage phase shift (2160°)
  • LFO speed: 100 ms to 14 s
  • S/N ratio: -80 dBm or less (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
  • Gain: Unity
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 410 g
  • Custom ICs
  • Two operating modes
  • Three controls: Rate, Depth, Resonance
