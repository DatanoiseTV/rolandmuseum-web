Roland G-202 Guitar for GR-100/GR-300

Synthesizer Addons Year: 1982

Description

The G-202 is a control guitar for Roland guitar synthesizers with a 24-pin connector and has the body shape of a Strat (but a distinctive pickguard and two humbuckers). In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal of the special 'hexaphonic' pickup, which outputs the string signals separately and can thus control GR synthesizers, the guitar also has a normal guitar output for the sound of the two humbucker pickups. The G-202 can therefore also be used as a regular guitar. The natural-colored maple neck, consistent across all variants, is bolted to an ash body, which was available in four color finishes. The following variants were manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (specifically by the supplier Fuji Gen Gakki) according to Roland specifications: white with a black pickguard, dark blue, red, or clear lacquer with a white pickguard. Sound control is provided by four gold (plastic) knobs for Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, and Balance between guitar and synthesizer level, two black knobs for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato intensity, a three-stage mode switch in between, and a three-stage switch between guitar and synthesizer signal or both.

Features

  • Control guitar for Roland GR-series guitar synthesizers (GR-100, GR-300)
  • 24-pin connector for synthesizer control
  • Hexaphonic pickup for separate string signals
  • Normal guitar output with two humbucker pickups
  • Can be used as a regular electric guitar
  • Maple neck, ash body
  • Available in four color finishes
  • Controls: Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, Balance (Guitar/Synthesizer level), Resonance (VCF), Vibrato intensity
  • Three-stage mode switch
  • Three-stage switch for Guitar/Synthesizer signal or both
