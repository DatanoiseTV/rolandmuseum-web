Roland TD-6K

Drum Sets Year: 2001

Description

The TD-6K is an almost complete drum kit comprised of the following individual components: TD-6 trigger module (blue), KD-6 kick trigger, five PD-6 snare/tom/hi-hat triggers (rubber pads), two CY-6 cymbal pads, and an FD-6 hi-hat control pedal. To make it playable, you'll need a bass drum pedal, drum stool, and a drum stand (rack with pad and cymbal holders from the MDS series or individual stands).

Features

  • 5x PD-6 Rubber Pads
  • 2x CY-6 Cymbal Pads
  • KD-6 Kick Trigger
  • FD-6 Hi-Hat Control Pedal
  • TD-6 Trigger Module
