Boss PSM-5 Power Supply & Master Switch

Utility Year: 1990

Description

The PSM-5 is a stabilized power supply that can supply voltage to up to five BOSS 9V effect devices (or other suitable effect devices). It is powered by mains voltage. A power adapter from the PSA series is included for connection to a mains socket. This ensures trouble-free operation without the risk of depleted batteries. Included is a parallel DC cable with five outputs, which is connected to the back of the PSM-5 and the power adapter sockets of the individual BOSS effect devices. But the PSM-5 is much more than a conventional power supply; it can also be used as a master switch. The send socket on the left side of the PSM-5 is connected to the first effect device in the chain. The output of the first effect device is then connected to the input of the second, and so on. Finally, the last effect device in the chain is connected to the return socket on the right side of the PSM-5. The guitar (or other instrument) is connected to the guitar socket and the amplifier to the amp socket of the PSM-5. These connections result in two separate loops - one through the effect devices, the other directly from the guitar to the amplifier, bypassing the effect devices. The loops can be easily selected by pressing the foot switch. An LED lights up when the effect loop is activated. The remarkable PSM-5 boosts the performance of your effect devices and controls them simultaneously - all in one housing.

Features

  • Stabilized power supply
  • Powers up to five BOSS 9V pedals
  • Master switch functionality
  • Two separate effect loops
  • Foot switch for loop selection
  • LED indicator
