Roland SH-5

Monophonic Analog Synthesizer Year: 1975

Description

The Roland SH-5 is a robust, monophonic analog synthesizer with 44 non-velocity-sensitive keys, permanently built into a flight case with an angled control surface. It features two Voltage-Controlled Oscillators (VCOs), each offering selectable waveforms (triangle, sawtooth, square, and modifiable pulse), which can be ring-modulated and mixed with controllable noise. The sound passes through a fixed band-pass filter, a dynamic multi-filter (high, band, or low pass), and a VCA, all shaped by ADSR and AR envelope generators. Modulation sources include portamento, two LFOs, a Sample & Hold generator, and the pitch bend lever, first introduced with the SH-5. While it lacks audio effects, it offers numerous control connections.

Features

  • Monophonic analog synthesizer
  • 44 non-velocity-sensitive keys
  • Integrated into a flight case with angled control surface
  • Two VCOs with selectable waveforms (triangle, sawtooth, square, modifiable pulse)
  • Ring modulation capability
  • Mixer with controllable noise
  • Fixed band-pass filter
  • Dynamic multi-filter (High-pass, Band-pass, Low-pass)
  • VCA (Voltage-Controlled Amplifier)
  • ADSR envelope generator
  • AR envelope generator
  • Portamento
  • Two LFOs
  • Sample & Hold generator
  • Pitch bend lever (first introduced with SH-5)
  • Numerous control connections
  • Dimensions: 864 x 257 x 270/335 mm
  • Weight: 22 kg
  • Built-in power supply with fixed power cable
  • Included accessory: PJ-1 Audio Cable (2 x 2.5m)
  • Predecessor: SH-3
  • Successors: SH-7, SH-2
