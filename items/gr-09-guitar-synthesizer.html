GR-09 Guitar Synthesizer

Digital Synthesizer Year: 1993

Description

The Roland GR-09 Guitar Synthesizer is an affordable and intuitive instrument that brings Roland's powerful synthesizer technology to guitarists. It allows users to incorporate the latest digital sounds into their music without altering their playing technique, suitable for professionals, semi-pros, and hobbyists alike.

Features

  • Easy installation of the GK-2A Synthesizer Driver, no tools required for attachment to guitar
  • GK-2A Synthesizer Driver can be easily attached to any electric or acoustic steel-string guitar, regardless of its shape
  • Smaller size of GK-2A fits more guitar models than its predecessor GK-2
  • Extremely precise Pitch/Envelope Detection Circuit and accelerated signal processing ensure immediate and accurate response to playing nuances
  • Perfect reproduction of all guitar techniques including string bending, slapping, pull-offs, chokes, and even bottleneck or slide
  • Light and compact, designed to be placed on the floor like a guitar effect pedal
  • Access to the latest Roland digital sounds, based on high-quality PCM waveforms, processed with advanced digital synthesis technology
  • 180 specially selected preset tones for guitar synthesizer, ranging from natural acoustic instruments (piano, strings, brass) to brilliant synthesizer sounds
  • Optional GR9E-1 Expansion Board adds 180 more sounds, including ethnic percussion and sound effects, totaling 360 sounds and utilizing its 28 voices optimally
  • Each patch contains 2 tones (Main and Sub-Tone) that can be selected and played simultaneously
  • 4 different modes per string: Main Tone only, Sub Tone only, Main and Sub-Tone together, and Mute
  • Each string can be individually transposed
  • Preset tones can be adjusted by editing Attack, Release, and Brightness parameters
