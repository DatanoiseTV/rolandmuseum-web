Roland MSQ-700

MIDI Sequencers Year: 1984

Description

The MSQ-700 is an 8-track MIDI sequencer capable of recording data from all 16 MIDI channels on each track. Notes can be recorded in real-time or step-time, and overdubs are possible. It is song-oriented, meaning there are no patterns. It offers limited editing capabilities, but allows merging multiple tracks and chaining the playback order. It can record approximately 6500 events. The MSQ-700 can operate as master or slave via MIDI, DIN SYNC, or FSK (which it can also generate). Data can be saved to tape/cassette via FSK. It's considered the 'big brother' of the MSQ-100 and the successor to the CSQ-600.

Features

  • 8-track MIDI sequencer
  • 16 MIDI channels per track
  • Real-time and step-time recording
  • Overdubbing
  • Merge and Chain functions
  • DIN SYNC and FSK support
  • Tape storage via FSK
