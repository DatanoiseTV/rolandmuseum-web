Boss TU-12 Chromatic Tuner

Utility Year: 1983

Description

The TU-12H is a chromatic tuner equipped with a built-in microphone and a guitar input. Its tuning range extends from C1 to B5. Tuning is displayed via a needle meter in Hz, while fundamental tones or strings are indicated by LEDs. A switch allows toggling between chromatic and string display modes.

Features

  • Dimensions: 145 x 35 x 53 mm
  • Weight: 0.17 kg
  • Power: 1x 9V block battery (6LR61) or PSA power adapter
  • Current consumption: 7-20mA
  • Chromatic tuner functionality
  • Built-in microphone
  • Guitar input
  • Tuning range: C1 to B5
  • Tuning display: Needle meter (Hz) and LEDs for fundamental tones/strings
  • Switchable display mode: Chromatic or String
