BOSS Fender '65 Deluxe Reverb Amp-Pedal

Amp Modeling Pedal Year: 2007

Description

Equipped with the processing power of the latest chip generation, this is a precise copy of the famous 1965 Fender Deluxe Reverb: sound, response, and character of the original, including all controls and the legendary vibrato and reverb effects, plus original lettering and a Tolex-like black finish. This is the only Fender-approved, licensed, and globally authorized modeling of this amp! The Fender Deluxe Reverb sound is famous for Country, as well as overdriven for Blues and Surf music. An ideal preamp pedal with the typical Fender sound!

Features

  • The world's only Fender-authorized amp modeling
  • The famous Blues and Country sound of the 1965 Deluxe Reverb
  • All control options of the original
  • Original vibrato and reverb
  • Original lettering
