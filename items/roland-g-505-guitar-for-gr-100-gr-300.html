Roland G-505 Guitar for GR-100/GR-300

Other Year: 1982

Description

The G-505 is a control guitar for Roland guitar synthesizers with a 24-pin connector and features a Strat-style body, three single-coil pickups, a typical Strat 5-way switch, and a tremolo system (but a distinctive pickguard). In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal of the special 'hexaphonic' pickup, which outputs the signals of the strings separately and can thus control GR synthesizers, the guitar also has a normal guitar output for the sound of the single-coil pickups. The G-505 can therefore also be used as a regular guitar. The natural-colored maple neck is bolted to an ash body, which was available in three color finishes. According to Roland specifications, the following variants were produced by Ibanez/Greco (more precisely by the supplier Fuji Gen Gakki): in red, blue, or sunburst with a white pickguard. Sound control is provided by four golden (plastic) knobs for Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, and Balance between guitar and synthesizer level, two black knobs for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato intensity, a three-position mode switch in between, and another three-position switch for selecting between guitar signal, synthesizer signal, or both.

Features

  • Control guitar for Roland GR-100/GR-300 guitar synthesizers
  • 24-pin connector for hexaphonic pickup
  • Standard guitar output for single-coil pickups
  • Strat-style body
  • Three single-coil pickups
  • 5-way pickup switch
  • Tremolo system
  • Maple neck, ash body
  • Available in red, blue, or sunburst
  • Controls: Master Volume, Guitar Tone, Cutoff Frequency, Balance (Guitar/Synth)
  • Controls: Resonance (VCF), Vibrato Intensity
  • Three-position mode switch
  • Three-position switch for guitar, synth, or mixed signal
  • Dimensions: 322 x 1020 x 66 mm
  • Weight: 3.7 kg
