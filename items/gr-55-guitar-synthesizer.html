GR-55 Guitar Synthesizer

Digital Synthesizer Year: 2011

Description

The Roland GR-55 is a combination of a digital synthesizer for guitar, virtual guitar modeling, and a guitar-to-MIDI interface, housed in a floor-based unit. The GR-55 must be controlled via a GR cable from a hexaphonic pickup that individually senses guitar or bass strings, such as the GK-2AH or GK-3, or GK-2B or GK-3B. Two synthesizer sounds and the modeling sound can be mixed. The 910 synthesizer sounds are editable and comparable to the sound generation of the Juno-Di, Juno-Stage, etc. The GR-55 has a built-in looper and can serve as an audio/MIDI interface via USB. Audio data can be played back from an optionally connectable USB stick. The GR-55 can be controlled with its four large built-in buttons and the expression pedal, plus additional buttons and pedals, and is available as GR-55S (without pickup and GR cable) or as GR-55GK (with GK-3 and 5m GK cable).

Features

  • Combination of digital guitar synthesizer, virtual guitar modeling, and guitar-to-MIDI interface
  • Floor-based unit
  • Requires hexaphonic GK pickup (e.g., GK-3) and GR cable
  • Allows mixing of two synth sounds and one modeling sound
  • Features 910 editable synthesizer sounds (Juno-Di/Juno-Stage comparable)
  • Built-in looper
  • Functions as a USB audio/MIDI interface
  • Supports audio playback from optional USB stick
  • Controlled by four built-in buttons, an expression pedal, and optional external pedals
  • Available as GR-55S (unit only) or GR-55GK (with GK-3 pickup and 5m GR cable)
  • Dimensions: 405 x 106 x 244 mm
  • Weight: 3.3 kg
  • Powered by PSB-1U adapter
