Boss DF-2 SUPER Feedbacker & Distortion

Guitar Effect Pedal Year: 1990

Description

The BOSS DF-2 SUPER Feedbacker & Distortion combines improved BOSS distortion with entirely new feedback circuitry. It provides the exact feedback needed regardless of guitar or amplifier type, even with headphone amplifiers. The feedback sound sustains as long as the pedal is pressed. An Over-Tone knob adjusts the balance between distortion and harmonic overtones produced by the feedbacker. The enhanced distortion circuit offers a full range of distorted sounds, with tone and distortion knobs for fine-tuning. A level knob adjusts the distortion sound's volume. A 2-mode toggle function allows switching between normal, distortion, and feedback modes with a single pedal. An LED indicates the operating mode.

Features

  • SUPER Feedbacker & Distortion
  • Over-Tone control for distortion/overtone balance
  • Improved distortion circuit with full range of sounds
  • Tone control for sound color adjustment
  • Distortion control for intensity
  • Level control for distortion sound volume
  • 2-mode switching (Normal, Distortion, Feedback) with single pedal
  • LED indicator for operating mode
  • Power supply: 9V battery, AC adapter (ACA series)
  • Current draw: 9V, 10 mA
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 450 g
