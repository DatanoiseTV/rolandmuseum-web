Boss HF-2 Hi Band Flanger

Effect Year: 1986

Description

The sharp, metallic flange sounds of the BF-2 have long fascinated musicians of all genres. To offer an even greater variety of flange sounds, BOSS now introduces the improved HF-2 High-Band Flanger. The center frequency of the HF-2 for the flange effect is an octave higher than that of the BF-2. This means the flange effect is applied only to the overtones, without affecting the fundamental tones. This results in extremely clear, light sounds. The HF-2 is very effective for processing the chord accompaniment of an electric guitar or the string sounds of a synthesizer. The HF-2 has four controls: Manual, Depth, Rate, and Resonance. The Manual knob adjusts the delay time. The Depth knob determines the sweep range. The Rate knob is responsible for the sweep speed. And the Resonance knob is used to adjust the feedback. Electronic FET switching completely suppresses "click" noises. The sound quality of the HF-2 is sufficient for studio recordings.

Features

  • High-Band Flanger effect
  • Four controls: Manual, Depth, Rate, Resonance
  • Manual control for delay time (0.5 to 6.5 ms)
  • Depth control for sweep range
  • Rate control for LFO speed (100 ms to 16 s)
  • Resonance control for feedback
  • Electronic FET switching for click-free operation
  • Effective for electric guitar chord accompaniment and synthesizer string sounds
  • Studio-quality sound
  • Power supply: 9V battery or ACA-series AC adapter
  • Power consumption: 9V, 18 mA (max.)
  • Signal-to-noise ratio: -95 dBm (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 470 kOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 10 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 400 g
