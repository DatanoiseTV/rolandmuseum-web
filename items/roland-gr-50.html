Roland GR-50

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1980

Description

The Roland GR-50 guitar synthesizer is a compact rack-mountable guitar synthesizer system that combines a LA synthesis sound source and a MIDI converter in one powerful device. The core of the GR-50 is a super-fast converter circuit that converts the sampled pitch into digital signals. The playing information can be passed on to the internal sound source, which provides you with a variety of expressive possibilities. The special feature of this system, however, is the extremely high pitch sampling speed and the high sampling accuracy, with which even the finest nuances of your guitar style can be captured. Playing techniques such as bending and vibrato are reproduced cleanly and evenly, and the high response speed of the system enables the guitarist to realize even nuanced techniques such as hammer-ons and pull-offs with exceptional transfer accuracy. The GK-2 synthesizer driver is the pickup belonging to the GR-50 and is available as a special accessory. It can be easily and effortlessly mounted on any electric guitar or even on acoustic guitars (with steel strings). The sound source of the GR-50 works with the same linear-arithmetic (LA) synthesis technology on which Roland's well-known D-series synthesizers are also based. This synthesis method, which has quickly established itself in the professional music scene, produces voices of exceptional quality and is therefore preferred by international top musicians. The GR-50 gives you a choice of various pre-programmed and self-programmable voices, which you can play individually or overlay with the normal, natural sound of your guitar. The GR-50 also allows you to play polyphonically, i.e. you can play 12 different synthesizer voices (2 voices per string) simultaneously with your guitar. In addition, external MIDI sound sources connected to the MIDI jacks can be controlled via the GR-50. An external sequencer, such as the Roland Micro Composer MC-500 Mark II, can also be integrated into your system, via which you can then control two of the GR-50's internal LA synthesis voices as well as a rhythm configuration, while you simultaneously play other internal voices via your guitar. All in all, the GR-50 and the GK-2 together form a highly powerful and flexible guitar synthesizer system that offers the guitarist unsurpassed response speed, extensive MIDI control options, all the possibilities for polyphonic playing and first-class sound quality, in short, a system that opens up many new and fascinating expressive possibilities. GR-50 Guitar Synthesizer High Response Speed GR-50 and GK-2 set new standards for the response behavior of guitar synthesizer systems. A new large-scale integrated solid-state circuit (LSI chip), specially developed for the GR-50, enables a super-fast signal acquisition algorithm in conjunction with the 16-bit central processing unit (CPU), which guarantees a high response speed and precise pitch detection. Even fast runs on the lowest string are captured quickly and reliably. Every guitarist will find the freedom of expression offered by this new guitar synthesizer ideal. In particular, nuanced fingering techniques such as hammer-ons and vibrato can now be played without having to compromise your individual guitar style.
