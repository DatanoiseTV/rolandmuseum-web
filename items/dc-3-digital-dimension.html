DC-3 Digital Dimension

Compact FX Year: 1986

Description

The DC-3 is a new Dimension pedal that operates completely digitally, producing studio-quality chorus effects that surpass all conventional chorus devices. The exceptional performance of the DC-3 even extends beyond the capabilities of the popular DC-2 Dimension device. The chorus effect of the DC-3 sounds completely natural and is characterized by exceptionally high quality. Sounds processed by this Dimension device retain all their characteristics, even the finest nuances. Furthermore, the chorus effects of the DC-3 are completely free of the blurring unavoidable in conventional devices. The DC-3 is therefore the ideal effect unit for keyboards and guitars. The digital circuit of the DC-3 combines a high-quality digital delay with digital modulation, creating sound modulation with ideal two-phase synchronization and thus a natural-sounding Dimension effect without fluctuations and blurring of the original sound. The surprisingly many effects can be easily adjusted with four controls: Effect Level, Equalizer, Rate, and Depth. Exceptional sound quality is ensured by BOSS's specially developed LSI chip, guaranteeing crystal-clear sound without the slightest signal degradation. Since the signal is first routed through the equalizer and then through the chorus section, the sound can be considerably modified to create soft to penetrating effects. With two output jacks, a remarkably spatial and rich-sounding stereo effect can be achieved by connecting each output to a separate amplifier.

Features

  • Fully digital operation
  • Studio-quality chorus effects
  • Four controls: Effect Level, Equalizer, Rate, Depth
  • BOSS custom LSI chip for crystal-clear sound
  • Two output jacks for stereo effect
  • Ideal effect unit for keyboards and guitars
  • Power supply: 9V battery or AC adapter (PSA series)
  • Power consumption: 9V, 65 mA
  • Frequency response: 20 Hz to 20 kHz (+1 dB, -3 dB)
  • Signal-to-noise ratio: -100 dBm or less (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance: 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance: over 20 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 450 g
