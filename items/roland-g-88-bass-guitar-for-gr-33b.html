Roland G-88 Bass Guitar for GR-33B

Other Year: 1980

Description

The G-88 is a control bass for the Roland GR-33B bass guitar synthesizer (with a 24-pin connector) and has the body shape of a typical Fender bass. In addition to the 24-pin connector for the signal of the special pickup, which outputs the signals of the four strings separately and can thus control GR synthesizers, the bass also has a normal bass output that outputs the sound of the single-coil pickup with double magnets. Thus, the G-88 can also be used as a regular bass. The bass has a body made of walnut and ash with a neck-through design of maple and walnut, and was manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (more precisely by the supplier Fuji Gen Gakki). Sound control is provided by three larger, gold, and two smaller, gold and black (plastic) knobs: Master Volume, Cutoff Frequency, and Balance between bass and synthesizer level (large), separate controls for bass and treble (gold), and controls for Resonance (VCF) and Vibrato intensity. A three-stage switch located between the resonance and vibrato controls switches between bass and synthesizer signal or both.

Features

  • Control bass for Roland GR-33B bass guitar synthesizer
  • 24-pin connector for separate string signals (for synth control)
  • Normal bass output with single-coil pickup (double magnets)
  • Can be used as a regular bass
  • Body: Walnut and Ash
  • Neck-through design: Maple and Walnut
  • Manufactured by Ibanez/Greco (Fuji Gen Gakki)
  • Controls: Master Volume, Cutoff Frequency, Balance (Bass/Synth level), Bass, Treble, Resonance (VCF), Vibrato intensity
  • Three-stage switch: Bass signal, Synthesizer signal, or both
  • Dimensions: 342 x 1182 x 66 mm
  • Weight: 4.4 kg
