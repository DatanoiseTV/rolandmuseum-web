OD-2 Turbo-Overdrive

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1985

Description

The Boss OD-2 'TURBO OverDrive' is a distortion effect for guitar, housed in the standard compact pedal enclosure used since 1977. Power is supplied either via an ACA power adapter or a 9V block battery. The OD-2 features a jack input and a jack output, along with three knobs and a rotary switch to engage the TURBO distortion, which shifts the sound from light distortion to medium-heavy and fatter tones. The knobs adjust Level, Tone, and Drive (distortion amount).

Features

  • Guitar distortion effect
  • Standard compact pedal enclosure
  • Powered by 9V battery or ACA power adapter
  • Features one jack input and one jack output
  • Three control knobs for Level, Tone, and Drive
  • Rotary switch to engage 'TURBO' distortion mode, offering light to medium-heavy and fatter tones
  • Dimensions: 70 x 55 x 125 mm
  • Weight: 0.44 kg
  • 16mA power consumption
