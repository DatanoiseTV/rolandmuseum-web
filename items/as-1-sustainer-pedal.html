AS-1 Sustainer Pedal

Guitar/Bass FX Year: 1972

Description

The Roland AS-1 'Sustainer' is a floor effect pedal for guitar in a sturdy die-cast enclosure. This sustainer is used to lengthen played notes and has two controls, SUSTAIN and OUT LEVEL, and an on/off switch on the top. SUSTAIN controls the length of the sustain, OUT LEVEL controls the output level. The input jack (6.3mm mono jack), labeled INSTRUMENT, is on the right side, while the mono output jack 'OUTPUT' is on the left. The AS-1 features a clean sound and, unlike some other compressors, minimally alters the tone. The housing shape is the same as later used for (Roland) AF-60, AP-2, and (Boss) BF-1. To change the battery, the housing must be unscrewed from the bottom; mains operation is not provided. The AS-1 is Roland's first effect unit and was manufactured by the Roland Music Engineering Group (Roland MEG), which was later renamed 'Boss'.

Features

  • Floor effect pedal for guitar
  • Sturdy die-cast enclosure
  • Lengthening played notes (Sustain)
  • Two controls: SUSTAIN and OUT LEVEL
  • On/Off switch
  • 6.3mm mono jack input (INSTRUMENT)
  • Mono output jack (OUTPUT)
  • Clean sound, minimal tone alteration
  • Battery-powered (1 x 9V block)
  • Roland's first effect unit
  • Dimensions: 115 x 62 x 205 mm
  • Weight: 0.85 kg
