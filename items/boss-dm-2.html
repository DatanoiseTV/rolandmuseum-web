Boss DM-2

Compact FX Year: 1982

Description

The DM-2 Delay is an analog delay unit with a delay time range of 20 to 300 ms and a built-in noise reduction system. It's housed in the classic compact BOSS format. It's ideal for quick and easy echo effects.

Features

  • Analog Delay
  • Delay Time: 20ms - 300ms
  • Noise Reduction
  • Compact Size
  • Repeat Rate Control
  • Intensity Control
  • Echo Control
