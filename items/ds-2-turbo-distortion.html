DS-2 TURBO Distortion

Compact FX Year: 1987

Description

The DS-2 is a new distortion pedal featuring a Turbo circuit, enabling a wide range of distortion effects for various music styles. The DS-2's advanced technology ensures excellent sound quality at all settings. It produces sounds from soft saturation to hard distortion reminiscent of overdriven amplifier stacks. Turbo I mode creates a soft distortion effect with a linear frequency response, allowing for a clear, natural sound even at maximum instrument volume. Even with extreme settings, the sound remains musical and free of unpleasant harsh highs. In Turbo II mode, the DS-2 simulates the sound of an overdriven tube amplifier, emphasizing the mid-range of the sound spectrum to produce the powerful distortion sound indispensable in today's music scene. The DS-2 is the first BOSS compact effects unit with a remote jack. By connecting an external footswitch, such as the FS-5L, musicians can toggle between Turbo I and Turbo II modes, offering greater flexibility even in the middle of a song.

Features

  • Turbo circuit
  • Turbo I mode (soft distortion, linear frequency response, clear sound)
  • Turbo II mode (simulates overdriven tube amp, mid-range emphasis, powerful distortion)
  • Remote jack for external footswitch (e.g., FS-5L) to switch between Turbo I/II
  • 9V battery or AC adapter (PSA series) power
  • 9V, 12mA current draw
  • Equivalent input noise -120 dBm or less (IHF-A)
  • Input impedance 1 MOhm
  • Output load impedance over 10 kOhm
  • Dimensions: 70(W) x 125(D) x 55(H) mm
  • Weight: 400 g
